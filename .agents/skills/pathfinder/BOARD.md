# The board of the work

<straw-dog question="q-p7qw6i">
A card is a subgroup: a subtree of the question store under a head the maker names, each open
question falling to its nearest head. A row is a group of cards, named by what the product
document's headings have in common. A column is the card's state, read from the units of work and
the running sessions: in progress; a ready unit; units not ready; questions alone. Colour repeats
the column and says nothing else.

- A pull is a written dependency between units or questions, never read from prose. The card that
  waits names what it waits on, in part where only some of its units do; the card that holds says
  how many units wait on it, through others too.
- What the person put first is framed and said in a word.
- Names are the person's words, with no id. Above the board stands what a number, a frame and
  *holds* mean; below it, what the records could not say.
- Read the records anew for each drawing.
- The maker looks at it rendered, in the form to be shown, and mends it; then a fresh reader says
  from the picture alone what it shows, and what they cannot say is mended, before the person sees.
</straw-dog>
