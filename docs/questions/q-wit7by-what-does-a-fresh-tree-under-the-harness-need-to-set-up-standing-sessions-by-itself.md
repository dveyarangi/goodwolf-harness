orchestrate it, and that session asked core's keeper four things before doing anything: what it
is called, by what the work is cut, how the sessions are kept in step, and what comes first. The
keeper answered from the decisions of 2026-11-15 and 2026-11-16 and from that tree: the name's
form is each tree's local rule and its glyph the person's; a zone is paths, cut where the domain's
seams pass — that tree's architecture already names three, the collectors, the store's service,
the app's pages — and no ticket is a role; the session that holds the harness hands out no work;
the rules that keep sessions in step are the five core carries, the fifth reaching that tree at
its next update; first an update of core, then one proposal to the person on whether standing
roles are wanted at all.

What it shows of this question: a tree at its start asks for the name, the unit of the cut, what
is rule already and what is not, and the first step — and none of the four is written anywhere a
fresh tree's session reads. The answer went by message from one keeper to another.
