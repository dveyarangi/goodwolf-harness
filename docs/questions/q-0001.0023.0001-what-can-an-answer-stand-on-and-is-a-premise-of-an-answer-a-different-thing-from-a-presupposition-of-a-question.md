The positions, 2026-10-09, from the parent's discover pass, none taken:

1. **One link, no kinds** — Quine's web, reason maintenance, build traces. Everything stands on
   everything by one relation; what a change does is read at the change, from how central the
   thing is. Its family's warning: support nobody records silently becomes premise.
2. **Two kinds, by a test** — the logic of questions, precedent, conditional constraints. A
   question's presupposition is whatever every one of its answers entails; an answer's support is
   what this answer used and its rivals do not entail. The test applies per link, not per pair of
   questions: *which language?* presupposes a service, since Python and Go alike entail one; its
   answer Python is supported by the team knowing Python, which Go does not entail. One question
   can hold both links at once, and "no team unmakes the language question" runs along a chain —
   team, then service, then language — not along the support link.
3. **Three kinds** — 2 with *constrains* between them: an answer that narrows another question's
   options without choosing (*we are on AWS* leaves three brokers). Changed, the question stands
   and its options reopen.
4. **A typed graph** — 3 with fact against criterion (an answer resting on a value, re-chosen
   though no fact changed), standing on an absence (*no cache as long as nothing reads heavily*),
   and conflict. Its family's warning, the capture problem: typed links are rarely written and
   inconsistently used.
5. **Kind as a role, not a link** — hinges and research programmes. What something is to the
   question is not fixed in the link but by the role its content plays at the time; content moves
   from answer to ground as the project sets. The user's sense that the kind tracks how set the
   project is.
