The user, 2026-11-16, to a session whose context had been compacted twice in two days: "а почему
после сжатия нет recall?"

What stands today, read from the code and the rules. At a compaction the host's start hook gives
the session its window again, whole — the store near its position — and not the wake's read
(`questions.py`, the start handler). The rule that every conversation starts with its recall says
a conversation the host resumes is the same conversation, "its context holds the recall it ran":
written for a resume under a new id, it takes for granted that the context survives, and after
a compaction the context holds the host's summary instead. So a compacted session works from its
own summary and its position, and reads nothing of the tree again unless its work asks: what
other sessions landed meanwhile, and whether the records agree with the summary, go unread.

The keeper's lean, no decision: a whole recall after every compaction costs what the compaction
saved; a reading bounded to the session's own zone and unit — its ticket, its question and the
commits since — would cost little, and is the same bound a session with a role is to wake by.
