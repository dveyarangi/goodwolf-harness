## The sort by drift, 2026-10-07

Raised by the user inside the environment align, when "an agent reads the files a month later"
was seen to argue against an architecture doc as much as against a description of the
environment. A written thing is sorted by what it drifts against, not by its role:

1. **What it intends to become** — the future form. Drifts in itself, because it is refined as
   the work goes, and the work moves toward it. Spent once the thing reaches it: then what it
   settled lands in kind 2 and the record itself becomes kind 4. A ticket, a spec, an RFC, an
   open question. *(the user, 2026-10-07: first named purpose, then corrected — everything has
   a purpose, an evidence file included, so purpose is a property of every record and not a kind;
   the future form is one, and a landed one can move the invariants.)*
2. **What must always hold** — invariants, and the decisions with their reasons that make the
   thing what it is. Drifts from the doc into its derivatives: the code stops obeying, and the
   repair is to the derivative. Never spent by being reached.
3. **What exists** — a description for quick understanding, a description of the environment
   among them. Drifts from the derivatives into the doc: the code moves on, and the repair is to
   regenerate the doc from it.
4. **What happened** — history. Drifts by definition and is never repaired.

The test between 1 and 2, since both are written before the thing obeys them: a record of kind 1
expires when the thing matches it; one of kind 2 keeps governing after. The tree already runs the
handover from 1 to 2 — a resolved decision lands in its durable home before its ticket closes, and
a spec's surviving agreements get homes before it is archived — so the sort names a flow the
rules already hold apart.

**The sort is of a mechanism's records, never of its instruction** *(the user, 2026-10-07)*: an
instruction — a skill, a rule — is the mechanism itself, not something it writes. The one
exception is the mechanism shape, which produces instructions, so for it a skill is a record of
kind 2 and the invariants of that kind are `/skill-up`'s today.

**Proposed** *(the user, 2026-10-07)*: a clear classification of the kinds; no mechanism mixes
kinds in one record; every mechanism declares the kind of each of its outputs — beside the reader
its format already makes it name. The tree already holds one pair apart this way, a mechanism's
doc from its evidence, kind 2 from kind 4, and the ticket and the queue are the second shape that
forces the same cut: a ticket holds its outcome and criteria (1), its landed decisions (2, until
they land) and the story of what went wrong (4); the queue holds a candidate (1), a table derived
from the ticket headers (3) and a completed-step narrative (4).

**The whiteboard** *(the user, 2026-10-07)*: a working draft is needed in any case — a place
where everything bearing on a task is written down until enough has gathered to see its form,
and only then sorted by topic. It is not a fifth kind: it drifts against nothing, being the state
before the sort, and the kinds are what it is sorted into; maintenance does not hold it (the user,
2026-10-05). The ticket is, by idea, such a draft — unless the drafting moves to the question
store, and the ticket becomes the form a project gives one kind of interaction.

**Decided: the whiteboard is the body of the question the task stands on; the ticket is a record
of kind 1 alone** *(the user, 2026-10-07)*. A ticket is minted only once its form is seen, so it
cannot be the place where the form is found — that is the body of the lowest question containing
the task, which the placement rule already names. From that body a ticket is minted — outcome,
what to build, criteria — and is the form of one kind of interaction, agreed work carried into
code and closed. A decision lands from the body into its durable home; the story of what went
wrong goes to evidence or a session record, and the ticket links.

**Decided: core owns the contract of kind 1; the file form is its default realization, and a
project may substitute its own board** *(the user, 2026-10-07)*. What a unit of agreed work must
state — outcome, what to build, criteria, done when every criterion holds with verification among
them — is core's, since the delivery ring reads nothing else of a ticket. The file in
`docs/tickets/` is one realization; GitHub Issues, Linear or Jira is another, admitted when it
reads as the contract. That no recipient has asked is not the decisive fact: the adoption panel
of 2026-09-26 named tickets in the project's own tracker as a recurring reason across its
simulated readers, so the second shape is already in evidence.

**Decided: the queue is kind 1 alone** *(the user, 2026-10-07)*: the order, the candidate and
what follows what, hand-written. Its table and counts are rendered — 01-0011.0040's decision,
widened to everything of kind 3 in it; its completed-step narrative is a second home for what the
session records hold, and shrinks to a pointer at the last step; its ordering rule is a rule, and
goes to the rules file of the mechanism that owns it when one is declared.

**Decided: every document is marked with its kind** *(the user, 2026-10-07)*. How the mark is
written is the ticket's.

**Decided: the kind is a kind of source of truth** *(the user, 2026-10-07)*, on two axes: which
side is right when the record and the thing it is about disagree — the record, the thing, or
neither — and whether the record's authority expires once the thing reaches it. Kind 1: the
record, expiring. Kind 2: the record, standing. Kind 3: the thing. Kind 4: neither, the referent
being past. Drift, repair direction, who may change what, and whether the record outlives being
reached all follow from these two. Persistence and mutability were weighed and found to be
consequences; drift alone does not tell 1 from 2.

**Decided: a kind is a relation, so every record declares its referent — what it is a record
of — beside its kind, and the thing carries a mark back to the record that governs it** *(the
user, 2026-10-07)*. One file can be a record of one thing and the thing of another record, and
the chains compose. The relation is authored once, at the record, and installed at the thing as a
mark, the way a straw dog names its question in the text, an installed block names its owner in
the skill, and a `TODO q-N` names its question in code: the agent that changes a thing reads the
thing, so the pointer to what governs it must be in the thing, not in a map it would have to ask
for. A check may hold the two ends in agreement, as the installer's does; it is not what serves
the direction. A thing that cannot carry a mark — a remote board, a binary — leaves the reverse
direction unpointed, and says so.

**Refined: the mark back is needed only where the thing is agentic** *(the user, 2026-10-07)* —
where an agent edits it and must meet, in it, what governs it. A thing a script derives — a
rendered table, a binary, a file on a remote board — carries no mark, since no agent is ever at
it with a change in hand. Shown on 01-0010.0171's chain the same day: the one gap is
`harness.py`'s install path, whose docstring names the architecture and not its section.

## Impact — 2026-10-07, the sort and the marks as one work

their homes, the `💡` `🗂️` `⚓` marks and the box under `debug=on`.

**Impact.** Four mechanisms and the entry file. The **mechanism shape**: its format's *What it
produces* gains the kind of each output, read by `mechanisms.py`, which today checks only that
the section exists; its glossary's *Principle* entry rewritten, *documentation / record /

**Hidden edges.** (1) *The mark back from core code cannot point into `docs/`.* `harness.py`
is core and `architecture.md#installing` is this project's record; a recipient's architecture
has no such section, so the mark would be a leak under *core may not depend on the instance*.
The record of the second kind that governs core code is the mechanism's doc; the architecture's
*Installing* duplicates it and should point there — one home per fact, found by the sort. (2)
*Kind declared per record multiplies a fact.* Every ticket is of the first kind, so the kind
belongs to the format, once; a per-record mark is for documents without a format
(architecture, glossary, research) and for formats admitting more than one kind. (3) *The
whiteboard moves P7's staging.* While a decision has not landed, it lives in the question's
body — so the body's format gains a decisions section the mover and the close respect, and a
`close` must refuse while a decision in the body has no home. (4) *The marks rule has no owning
mechanism.* Principles are nobody's; R5 says write it by hand in the entry file as a straw dog
bound to q-0030, or incept a mechanism; a tier-1 rule paid by every reply is its occasion's
tier, so no cheaper home exists. (5) *Grandfathering.* Rewriting thirty-one tickets is a pass
of its own; the sort applies to new records at once and to a live one at its next touch, by
`/maintain`, with the queue done first since it is one file. (6) *The glossary's roles* are
used in fourteen files; reconciling them is a rename across skills and docs, or a declared
second axis, and either is one edit per file.

**Leave alone.** The question store's parts and calls — the sort names the body's role, not the
script; `01-0010.0170` and the environment align, which wait on this and change nothing of it;

**Recommendation: proceed as a spec with five slices, in this order.** Load-bearing by every
factor — four mechanisms, two formats, the entry contract, expensive to reverse. (a) *Names*:
the glossary's five kinds of statement and four kinds of record, the roles reconciled, every
principle and agentic classification named at its home, the contract bumped — no code, and
every later slice cites these names. (b) *The marks on a reply*: the tier-1 rule by hand, bound
to q-0030, graded by the user reading replies; cheap, and the user's first ask. (c) *The
mechanism shape and maintain*: kind per output in the format and its check, A1 split. (d) *The
ticket and the queue*: formats thinned, the table rendered, `/align`'s three rules retargeted,
01-0011.0040 closed into it. (e) *The mark back on agentic things*: the convention, the
core/instance edge resolved by pointing core code at its mechanism doc, `harness.py`'s one gap
repaired. It solves demonstrated problems — the queue's narrative, L3's copy, three
recommendations overturned for standing on nothing — and the one general abstraction, the sort,
is forced by two shapes already apart in the tree, the doc/evidence pair and the ticket/queue
pair. Narrowed: kind at the format, not per record; existing records at their next touch.

Different citizens on different substrates. The method glossary's three kinds — documentation,
