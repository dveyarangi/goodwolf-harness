What bears on the answer:

- **The cut, not the wording.** The draft's root 2 fuses structure and trajectory, root 3 fuses
  four questions — what it is one of, what surrounds it, what else it could be, what is not yet
  known — and root 1 and root 3 overlap through purpose and identity. The pass's cleaner cut is
  purpose (for what, for whom, never what), constitution (made of, how it works), situation (what
  surrounds it, what it is one of, how it is done elsewhere); trajectory, evidence, cost and
  authority become cross-cutting rather than roots.
- **"What is this project?" is the parent, not a sibling**: the question the whole tree answers,
  which as a third root swallows the other two or becomes a junk drawer.
- **Who decides** — who may close a question — is the omission the most families name, and the
  one the tasking did not prime; this tree holds it as roles, q-0016.
- **Fixed roots allocate attention**: the technical root absorbs the work and the exploratory one
  starves (the viable-system model's System 3 over System 4). That is also the case for fixing
  them — an empty root is a visible absence.
