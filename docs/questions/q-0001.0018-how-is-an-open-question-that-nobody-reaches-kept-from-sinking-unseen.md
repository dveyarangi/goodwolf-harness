ranks a question that is reached again, and one nobody reaches sinks. q-0007, height, raised

on 2026-10-03, 28 straw dogs had waited on 01-0017, Planned, since 2026-09-06. A straw dog makes
such a question heavier, since text in the tree waits on it
([01-0011.0100.0030 decision 6](../tickets/01-0011.0100.0030-a-straw-dog-is-bound-to-the-question-it-waits-on.md#decisions-landed-at-this-tickets-align)).
