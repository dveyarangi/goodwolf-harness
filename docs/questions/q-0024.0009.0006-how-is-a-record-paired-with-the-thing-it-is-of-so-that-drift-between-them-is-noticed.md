the mark of a record's kind is unary, so it gives no pair and no detector — *the whole idea is to
see drift, not to hang labels for show* *(the user)*. Seeing drift takes three things at once: the
pair, the record and the thing it is of; the side that leads; and what notices them come apart.
Two already have all three — a mechanism's doc and its parts under the maintenance clock, and an
edge's invariant with its validator. The outside view, families of traceability, conformance,

[what a record drifting from its thing is an instance of](../research/discover-record-drift-2026-10-10.md).
A mark that names its referent is one candidate, to be tried on one class of record where the pair
is plain before any other *(the user, 2026-10-10)*.

## Decisions

1. **A mechanism's doc and its parts are the first class** *(the user, 2026-10-10)*: the pair is
   already declared by path in the doc's parts table, the doc leads, and the maintenance clock
   already fingerprints files. Today the clock makes a mechanism's records due when its doc, rules
   file or skill moved, and its doc due when the meta-rules moved; a script, a test or a shelf
   moving makes nothing due, and `mechanisms.py` holds only that each listed path exists.
2. **Only the parts it owns** *(the user, 2026-10-10)* — the rows of *Install adds, uninstall
   removes*; a part it relies on is its owner's doc's to watch, so one edit makes one doc due.
3. **In another's file a mechanism owns only what it installs there** *(the user, 2026-10-10)*:
   the parts watched are whole files. A hook's wiring merged into a host's shared file is the
   installed copy of a shelf the mechanism owns, and the shelf is watched; a heading another skill
   carries, where a block is installed, is that skill's, relied on.
4. **The mechanism shape holds its meta-rules** *(the user, 2026-10-11; amended at `.0060`'s plan
   the same day)*: the two lines it holds in the entry file by anchor — *method is mechanism work*,
   *an installed block is not yours* — leave the entry file for `/mechanism`'s description, the
   second turned into a use case; nothing of the shape is installed there, since the first restated
   the description at the same tier and the second is guarded by the installer's check — the
   decisions of q-0024.0009.0006.0001. The shape is core enough to hold the meta-rules, and it
   alone holds the true ones: instructions on how to make instructions. A principle that bears on
   everything, instructions among them — Occam — is a meta-principle, also meta, and stays the
   entry file's own. The format's *a meta-rule is never injected* goes; the clock already reads
   the meta-rules as the entry file with the shape's rules file and skill.
5. **Two levels, `parts` and `output`** *(the user, 2026-10-11)*: a record is due when what it
   depends on moved, and there are two dependences. A mechanism's outputs depend on what governs
   them — its doc, rules file and the formats beside its skill — at `output`. Its doc depends on
   what the mechanism is made of — its own parts — at `parts`. Today's `rules` is no third level:
   the mechanism shape produces instructions, so every mechanism's doc and skill are its outputs,
   and `rules` is the shape's `output` read one mechanism at a time. How the marks key that, one row
   per mechanism still, is the plan's.
6. **The clock names what moved** *(the user, 2026-10-11)*: a mark keeps a fingerprint per file,
   so a due level says which files moved since it, and the re-check reads those against the
   record. The right act is made cheaper than the stamp; no note is asked of a mark. If marks are
   still stamped unread, a note is the next step, with that as its evidence.
7. **A due pair is re-checked to one of three outcomes** *(the user, 2026-10-11)*, by whether a
   decision stands behind the move: a landed decision moved the part and the doc lagged — the doc
   is amended; none did — the part is repaired to the doc, or the question taken to `/align`; the
   move touches nothing the doc says — nothing to change. The kind says which side is right when
   no decision stands behind the move; the landing that moved a file is found by its history.
   `/maintain`'s A1, which repairs the thing for a record of *what must always hold* outright,
   gains this.

## Split impact — 2026-10-11

`/impact` on the draft split — a parent owning this question, and three children: the shape
installs its meta-rules; the clock names what moved; a doc is due when its parts move.

**Impact.** The meta-rules move the entry file's tier-1 text: two hand-written bullets become
blocks of `mechanism-shape.rules.md`, the format's *a meta-rule is never injected* goes, the
shape's doc moves its trigger row, and the entry contract takes a version. The parts tables of
`questions`, `mechanism-shape` and `edge` lose their seven anchored rows — three to their shelves,
two to the installed rules, two to *Relies on*. The clock's change runs through `maintain.py`,
`MARKS-FORMAT.md`, the marks file, `test_maintain.py` and `/maintain`'s A1, A4, B4 and F4 text and
the maintain doc; `parts` takes every owned part, now whole files, none a directory.

**Hidden edges.** A recipient's marks file is in today's format and the clock reads it in the
recipient's verification set: a format that cannot read an old row fails every recipient at its
update, so an old row must read as marked without its files named, and the edge record's line
must say so. The installer writes a block at the end of its anchor's section, so the two
meta-rules leave their place in the general rules' list for the section's foot. A session working
q-0026 has edited the entry file this week; the meta-rules' slice collides there. An uninstall
(01-0010.0200) removes what the ref shipped, never reading parts tables, so the hook rows' going
leaves it whole — the merged hook entries are found from their shelves.

*Refuted at the clock slice's plan, 2026-10-11:* a recipient clocks none of the mechanisms it
received with core and so holds no marks; the old-row reading serves this tree's own rows.

**Leave alone.** The kinds and the marks of record (decision 2 of the closed doc-kind question);
the relied-on tables beyond the two headings; the output level's surface; `mechanisms.py`'s
anchor check, which keeps serving a part that is a line, should one be declared.

**Recommendation: narrow.** The parent adds a record for no criterion its children do not hold:
let the last slice own this question, and the first two answer questions of their own under it.
Otherwise proceed — the problem is shown three times over, and `parts` is a second dependence
beside `output`, the shape that justifies the clock's rule over both.
