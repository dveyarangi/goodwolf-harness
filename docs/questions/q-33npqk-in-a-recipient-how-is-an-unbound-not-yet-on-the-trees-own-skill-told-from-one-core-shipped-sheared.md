## What was found — 2026-11-16

Found by the judge of the change that made every skill owned, and older than it; the account is
in [the shape's evidence](../mechanisms/mechanism-shape.evidence.md), under "No skill is unowned
by design". In a recipient the shape check passes a `not yet` that binds no question, because
core's own arrive sheared of their bindings. The pass reads the kind by its first words and never
asks whose the line is, so a skill of the tree's own passes with a bare `Mechanism: not yet`,
with words after the kind, with a wrapper that binds nothing, and with any word that begins
`not yet`.

Two parts, of different weight:

- **What the shear cannot have left.** A sheared line is the kind alone, unwrapped. Words after
  it, a wrapper with no binding and a longer word are the tree's own writing, and the check can
  refuse them by form.
- **The bare line.** A skill of the tree's own that says exactly what a sheared one says is
  told from it only by knowing what shipped, which the tree does not record. The mark of
  [a project's own mechanism](../tickets/done/01-0010.0260-a-projects-own-mechanism-is-told-from-a-shipped-one.md)
  does not reach it: a skill claiming `not yet` belongs to no mechanism, marked or not.

## What the decision of 2026-11-16 changes

Every skill has a mechanism, with its doc at the least — decision 1 of
[q-gbt7cy](q-gbt7cy-does-each-skill-state-only-what-it-owns.md#decisions), the user. A skill's
`not yet` is then a state on the way out. For the bare line this names the end: once core ships
no skill that says `not yet`, nothing sheared arrives on a skill, the recipient's pass has no
skill's line left to excuse, and a skill of the tree's own is named by a mechanism the tree
declares and marks as its own. That waits on core's sixteen, which are that question's. Until
then the bare line passes. The pass on a moment's row is untouched: shipped declarations keep
their `not yet` rows, sheared. What the shear cannot have left is refused since
[01-0010.0260 a-projects-own-mechanism-is-told-from-a-shipped-one](../tickets/done/01-0010.0260-a-projects-own-mechanism-is-told-from-a-shipped-one.md):
the first part is answered, and the bare line is what this question still asks.
