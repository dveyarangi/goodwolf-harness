## The case, 2026-10-25

The question store's script reads the development method's records directly: the path counts the
work in hand from tickets In progress and lists the ready work from tickets Ready, importing the
ticket mechanism (`questions.py`, `_Ahead.read`); the window's counters count the live tickets
(`_tally`); the product mechanism renders each live ticket beside the product (`product.py
--tickets`). q-0037.0019's accepted shape had said the opposite: the unit of work is the installed
method's, *installed into the pathfinder by the method, not looked up*. The plan of 01-0060.0010
crossed it; the register holds the entry.

## Decisions

1. **The store and the product mechanism know only questions; a method adds its own** *(the user,
   2026-10-25)*: a question is in work when it has an owner, whatever record that is; what counts as
   ready, the ticket counter and a rendering of tickets beside the product are the method's to add
   where it is installed, and absent where it is not. Not: the path and the counters reading
   tickets because this tree has them.
2. **A method adds its own through its own hook, in the hooks' turn** *(the user, 2026-10-26)*:
   the store's path and window read only questions; the ticket mechanism's hook, run after the
   store's by the hooks' wrapper, adds to the wake and the window the ready tickets with what each
   builds and the ticket counter, and is absent where the method is; the product mechanism's
   rendering of tickets beside the product goes, the path's lines already saying what each piece
   serves. The same seam the session mechanism takes (q-0037.0006.0024.0003, decision 2). Not: the
   method speaking through a rule installed into `/recall` — held by the agent's memory of a rule,
   where a hook's lines arrive by themselves; nor a mark the method writes onto questions — one
   readiness written twice. Built when the store, session and maintain package is cut, or before
   it on its own.
