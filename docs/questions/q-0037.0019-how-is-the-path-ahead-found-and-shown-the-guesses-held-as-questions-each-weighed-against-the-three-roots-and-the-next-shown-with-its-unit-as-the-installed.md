## The shape, as argued 2026-10-20

The person's picture: the pathfinder investigates what the project is for, keeps that root
coherent, and derives what is done from it; the pacer uses pathfinding; pathfinding can be
freeform. Asked to think, not to take it as given, the agent argued:

- **Not the pacer's child.** The pacer answers *what is the next step inside chosen work, and
  where to resume* — decisions at code and a feature. Pathfinding answers *which work* — a
  product slice. Decisions on different rungs belong to different mechanisms; the pacer consumes
  the path. The ordering rule's binding to q-0037.0013 was the misfiling this question inherited;
  it belongs under q-0037 in its own right, or under q-0037.0018 as the product document's
  derivation into work. The person decides which.
- **Not the investigator of purpose.** Purpose is investigated at an align on a q-0033 question,
  by `/discover`'s outside view and `/advise`'s freeform read, and kept by the product mechanism —
  the document, its check, a decided answer opening its building. A pathfinder that investigates
  too is a second owner of purpose. What nothing does is derive: `product.py --queue` stops one
  step short on purpose, *it ranks nothing*. The pathfinder is that step.
- **Purpose dictates value, not the path.** Purpose says what is worth doing; it says nothing of
  means — the compounding criterion arose because in a self-building project the tool for the next
  slice matters — and it is a hypothesis until the landscape root measures it (q-0038.0001). So the
  path weighs three things, one a root: value from q-0033, leverage from q-0037, evidence from
  q-0038. The weights are the person's, set in the local file; the pathfinder is the one reader of
  all three roots, which is why it saw at once that two of them hold no ticket.
- **Two modes, one method.** In the loop, `/recall` and the pacer ask it for the next; freeform,
  the person asks where to go. `/advise`'s *what next?* becomes its output.

**Not:** a step that populates the purpose root — populating is the person's and the outside
views'; making the imbalance visible is what the pathfinder adds. Not: a rule in the queue —
