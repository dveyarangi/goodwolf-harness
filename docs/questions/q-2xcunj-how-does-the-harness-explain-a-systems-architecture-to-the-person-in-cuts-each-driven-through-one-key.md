**What is asked.** An analysis that lets the person understand a system in a detailed cut, its
resolution reaching the limit of the system's own complexity: «я хочу учиться, а не тупить». The
architect creates, keeps and above all explains the architecture without error, in different
cuts. Each cut is driven vertically through one key event: the profile of its causes at every
level of complexity. Choosing the event is rare, usually one for a theme or a cut; theirs is the
optimization of play, its object the length of a frame. «И самое главное, скил архитектора
должен способствовать тому что меньше и точнее кода перечитывается для понимания архитектуры и
меньше ошибок анализа случается.» Where the analysis finds a basic or critical element of the
architecture missing, the cost of building or changing it is weighed against the gain in the
cut's own measure.

**How it stands in that tree.** A first map of one cut is kept beside its optimization
mechanism: each event, the chain of its causes through the code, who sets it, its measured cost.
It is read before the code and corrected where the code disagrees. A local rule in that tree's
own skill is proposed to its person to hold this until the harness decides.

**What the sender left open:** whether this is a mechanism of its own, a mode of `/discover` or
of `/review-architecture`, or a kind of record — a cut, with its event and its map of causes;
where a cut's map lives; and what keeps it from drifting from the code.
