## Evidence — 2026-10-20

The user, from a sibling session: a reply said *At the dial, 🛠️ self-improvement=a rule* where it
meant *this waits for your decision*. The user barely read it; a reader outside the harness would
not. Two things were wrong at once: the form names the ladder and the rung, which the install
already decided the person never sees as names (01-0020.0010, decision 12: the install shows acts,
not rung names); and the sentence was the stop's only statement, where the entry file's debug list
makes `🛠️ <ladder>=<rung>` machinery, shown under `debug=on` alone.

## Options — 2026-10-20, put to the user

1. **The stop is said as the act** — recommended. One sentence in the person's words, by the
   zone's glyph and the ladder's own line for the rung, the column the entry file already holds:
   `✋ Changing an instruction — settled; it lands on your yes.` at the dial,
   `💬 Changing the way of working — yours to decide; options and a recommendation follow.` above
   it. The ladder's and rung's names appear in it nowhere. Under `debug=on` the machinery follows
   the sentence in parentheses: `(🛠️ self-improvement=a rule, at the dial)`. Same parts as today,
   one of them moved behind the other; the ⚖️ table's rule, *nothing said of why it waits*, stays.
2. **The glyph alone.** The ⚖️ Decisions table's row opens with ✋ or 💬 and no sentence is
   written. Fewest parts; but a glyph without words is what the user just could not read.
3. **The host's box.** On a host that draws a next-step box with buttons, the box is the stop and
   the reply's text says nothing of the dial. True on Claude Code's desktop app, not core's to
   rule: the edge's, if taken.
4. **Gloss the form in place.** `🛠️ self-improvement=a rule — a change to a rule, which you
   approve`. Least change; two notations for one thing, which Occam strikes.

Owner stays 01-0020.0020, whose first criterion this is one case of. Rung: self-improvement, a
rule — at the dial, so the wording is shown before it lands.

## Decided — 2026-10-20

Option 1, with ✋ for both zones: a stop at or above the dial is one sentence in the person's words,
the glyph and the ladder's own line for the rung, *settled; it lands on your yes* at the dial,
*yours to decide; the options and a recommendation follow* above it; `🛠️ <ladder>=<rung>` follows in
parentheses under `debug=on` alone. Landed in the entry file as v100 *(the user, 2026-10-20)*. The
ticket's first criterion — a real session's replies read by the person — stays open.

Amended the same day, v101: one form for every stop, `✋ <the decision, as the ladder shows it>`,
no zone told; the two cases were bloat *(the user, 2026-10-20)*.

v102, the same day: the parenthesis is `(🐛 <ladder>=<rung>)` — 🐛 marks any debug-only item with no
glyph of its own, and ✋ is the stop's one icon in both modes *(the user, 2026-10-20)*.
