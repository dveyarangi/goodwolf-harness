## Edge changes waiting for their line

Until the edge record exists, a change to the installation edge is written here as it is made,
so the record's first pass has them to take.

- **2026-10-08, 01-0011.0110.0030** — a declared mechanism's doc carries `- **kind** what must
  always hold` in its header and a kind after the name of each output it lists,
  `- **<output>** — *<kind>* — <who reads it>`. A recipient's own declared mechanism fails
  `mechanisms.py --check` after its update until its doc does; each diagnostic names the output
  and the phrases admitted.
- **2026-10-10, 01-0011.0040** — the ticket format no longer keeps a table in the queue, P8 is
  retracted from `/maintain`, and P12 has `/recall` and `/ticket` read `tickets.py --list`
  instead. A recipient's queue keeps whatever table it holds, but after its update no instruction
  tends it: it can delete the table, or keep it by hand knowing nothing will.
- **2026-10-10, 01-0011.0110.0040.0010** — the mark is renamed: a declared mechanism's doc
  carries `- **record of** what must always hold`, and `- **kind**` is reported. Every live
  ticket and the queue now open with `- **record of** what it intends to become` after their
  title, and every question entry with `- **record of**` as its first part, by its state. After
  its update a recipient fails `mechanisms.py --check`, `tickets.py --check` and
  `questions.py --check` until its records carry the mark: its mechanism docs and tickets by
  hand, the queue with them, its entries as `questions.py` rewrites each — or all at once by
  running every entry through the writer. A ticket and its RFC closed by `move_doc.py` land in
  `done/` marked `what happened`.
- **2026-10-11, 01-0011.0110.0040.0020** — every live ticket carries `- **Minted:** YYYY-MM-DD`
  after `Type`, the day it was written; a recipient fails `tickets.py --check` after its update
  until its live tickets do. One minted before 2026-10-11 is held to the required sections and the
  `/verify` box alone, so no older ticket is reported for its sections; one minted since holds the
  five named sections and nothing else. `questions.py close` refuses while the question's
  `## Decisions` list holds an item with no `→ <link>` to a home, and the check reports a live
  closed entry that does; a body without the list closes as before. A straw dog waiting on a
  question closed against any ticket, owner or not, stays undue until its answer is repointed to a
  home. `/align` lands a decision as it is made by the questions mechanism's Q6, and its anchor is
  `### Land resolutions as they are made`.
- **2026-10-11, 01-0011.0110.0060** — the entry file's *method is mechanism work* and *an
  installed block is not yours* leave its general rules for `/mechanism`'s description; nothing
  changes for a recipient but where an agent meets them. The mechanism format admits an anchored
  `where` for a relied-on line only, an owned part being a whole file: a recipient's own mechanism
  doc naming a line in another's file as an owned part still passes `mechanisms.py --check`, and is
  brought to the format at its next touch.
- **2026-10-11, the commit prefix** — `/commit` no longer names a commit's prefix: it asks for the
  subject in the project's own convention, where it states one. A recipient that named its commits
  by the loop's steps states that in its own local rules file, installed into `/commit`.
- **2026-10-11, 01-0011.0110.0080** — the clock's levels are `parts` and `output`; `rules` is no
  level, so `maintain.py --mark <mechanism> rules …` is refused and a marks row at `rules` fails
  `maintain.py --check` until removed. A recipient clocks none of the mechanisms it received with
  core and holds no marks, so nothing of its moves; one that wrote a marks file by hand removes its
  `rules` rows. The outcome vocabulary gains `repaired`. A mechanism's doc now comes due when a
  file of its *Install adds, uninstall removes* table moves, so a recipient's own mechanism doc that
  names a directory there fails the check: an owned part is a whole file.
- **2026-10-12, 01-0011.0110.0050** — every skill a mechanism names and every script, test and hook
  it owns opens with `Mechanism: <slug>`, code as the comment `# Mechanism: <slug>` after any
  shebang. Core's own parts carry it, so an update arrives green. A recipient's own skill named by
  a declaration of its own, or its own code listed in one's parts, fails `mechanisms.py --check`
  until it opens with the line; a skill no declaration names keeps its `Mechanism: not yet`. A file
  under `.agents/scripts/` naming a mechanism whose parts do not list it fails too. R6 in
  `/skill-up` and `/mechanism` says so to whoever adds a file.
- **2026-10-12, 01-0011.0110.0055** — `/implement` asks that a project's code following a rule of
  its architecture or an ADR name the rule in the comment beside it, as
  `(<path from the tree's root>: "<the rule's name>")` on one line, the name a heading or bold
  lead word for word; `/verify` reads those pointers in a slice's code. Nothing fails a check: a
  recipient's existing code is untouched, and its next `/implement` and `/verify` apply the rule.
  A comment pointing loosely — a date, a paragraph with no bold lead — is brought to the rule when
  next touched.
