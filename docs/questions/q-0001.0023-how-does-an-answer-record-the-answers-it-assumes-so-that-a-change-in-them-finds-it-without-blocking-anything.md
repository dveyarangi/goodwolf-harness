# q-0001.0023 How does an answer record the answers it assumes, so that a change in them finds it without blocking anything?

- **part of** q-0001
- **state** open
- **lean** assumes is between answers, not questions: written with an answer or lean, pinned to the answer it took, gone when the answer is replaced; depends on stays between questions, before any answer, and blocks
- **struck** 0, last 2026-10-08T22:15Z

Raised by q-0001.0022, 2026-10-09: an answer about the landscape a project evolves in can move
both its purpose and its structure, yet the landscape is never answered whole. The store carries
re-examination down *part of* and along *depends on*, and *depends on* also says a question cannot
be asked until the other is answered — too strong here. The user, 2026-10-09: the soft link is
wanted, but not hard-coded as one root's influence on another; a general mechanism resting on a
rule. Candidate: a relation *rests on* between any two questions, written when an answer assumes
another question's answer, and the existing rule — suspect every open question whose assumption
a changed answer breaks — followed along it by the script. Seeded links between the roots at
install would be data a project can drop, not code.

The user, 2026-10-09: *rests on* is a good option and wants formalizing. The draft, for /align
before anything is built:

- **The part.** `rests on` on entry A, a list of ids, like `depends on`.
- **What it means.** A's answer — or its lean, while A is open — holds only while B's answer, or
  B's lean while B is open, stands. Validity, not order. Beside the other two: *part of*, A is a
  piece of B; *depends on*, A cannot be asked until B is answered; *rests on*, A can be asked and
  answered at any time, and its answer is conditional on B's.
- **Never blocks, cycles allowed.** Nothing is ordered along it, so purpose and landscape may rest
  on each other.
- **Who writes it.** The agent, by a call `rest q-A --on q-B` and `unrest q-A --on q-B`, in the
  turn that writes an answer or a lean of A taking B's as given. A judgement made at answering,
  never seeded.
- **When it fires.** B closes, B is closed again with another answer, or B is marked suspect. A
  reword fires nothing: the same question means the same answers.
- **The rule, the agent's.** Read each question resting on B and mark suspect the ones whose
  assumption broke. One step: a question marked suspect passes it on only when its own answer,
  re-read, changes.
- **The script's half, mechanical.** Each call that touches B's answer prints the questions
  resting on B, so the reading cannot be skipped; a move renames ids in `rests on` as in
  `depends on`; the check reports a closed A resting on a B closed superseded, pruned or moot, A
  not suspect — the ground went away under it. A resting on an open B is normal and not reported.
- **Readers.** The agent at the call's output; the check; the window, which shows nothing new.

The user, 2026-10-09, on the draft: the relation is made toward an answer, not a question — then
what are its two ends, and how is it not *depends on*? Taken: the ends are answers. A question
assumes nothing; its answer, or its lean while it is open, takes another question's answer or
lean as given. So the draft above is corrected:

- **Two relations between questions, one between answers.** *Part of* and *depends on* join
  questions and exist before any answer: *depends on* says A cannot even be asked well until B is
  answered — which database, before what data is kept — and so blocks. *Assumes* joins answers,
  is born when an answer or lean is written, blocks nothing, and dies with the answer that made
  it: a replaced answer states its assumptions afresh, so no stale link outlives it.
- **Pinned to the answer it took.** The link records which of B's answers A's took — a mark of
  B's answer or lean as it stood — so the script, not the agent's memory, finds every A whose
  pinned B has since changed. Whether the assumption broke stays the agent's judgement.
- **The word.** *assumes*, since its subject is an answer; *rests on* fitted questions.

What *depends on* already carries stays: the check reports a closed A whose dependency is open,
so a dependency is also an assumption of A's answer by construction.
