   hears is said in, whose value is the project's — L9 here, a plain voice for the person until a
   recipient chooses — and installs one rule saying so; the voice of instructions and records is
   left to its own homes *(the user, 2026-10-24)*. Not: a cleanup alone — a recipient would keep the
   conflicting phrases core ships and get no voice; nor the voice written as a list of what not to
   do — rule failure 48, refused.
2. The mechanism's work opens with a pass over the corpus, every text its answer will change
   wrapped as a straw dog bound here *(the user, 2026-10-24)*. Done the same day, 14 places:
   - `AGENTS.md`: *A capable reader*, whole; *in their words* in *A reply speaks the method*; *in
     the person's words* in its stop clause.

   - the ticket rules file: P10, whole — *in plain words, each of its terms explained*.
   - `/harness`: *in their language*, *in their terms*; its `MEETING.md`: *in plain words, never a
     table of fragments*, and *the way Lem wrote …* — a project's own voice standing in core.
   - `/maintain`: *in their terms*.
   - `/recall`: *as they would say it*, *in their terms*, *in simple, precise language*.
   - `/mechanism`: *in plain terms*.
3. What is said at a moment is the speaking skill's: each skill decides for itself, in its own
   `What the person hears`. How it is said is the voice's. Records are not the voice's *(the user,
   2026-10-24, twice amending the first wording the same day — first to the voice choosing what of
   a skill's result is said, then back: the sections stay, each skill decides what it says)*. Not:
   the voice choosing what is said from a skill's result — it knows no skill's moment; nor the
   sections dissolved into one general rule — the agent's proposal, refused. Where *A reply speaks
   the method*, what a reply outside any skill's moment tells, goes is left open.
4. The voice's value is the name of the voice and nothing else — here, Lem's — a voice the model
   already knows; no description of it, no samples *(the user, 2026-10-24: Lem is not exact and dry;
   no samples and no description of Lem's voice)*. Not: a description — L9's *plain, exact and dry*
   stood in every run of the speech bench and the voice never showed, and it misdescribes the voice;
   nor samples written here — they would teach their own content and a voice not Lem's. What else L9
   holds — that speech invents nothing, that tables and texts for a record keep their forms'
   exactness — is not the voice's value; its home is settled with the mechanism's rules.
5. The voice reaches every skill that marks itself as speaking, as one installed block, and the
   entry file for a reply outside any skill's moment; the voice knows no skill in advance, and a
   skill joins by marking itself *(the user, 2026-10-24)*. Not: a block per skill authored in the
   voice's rules file — the voice cannot know which skills a tree installs. The installer has no
   group target yet: that is q-0037.0002, whose lean — membership declared from both ends, the
   group in the rules file and the file and anchor in each member — this decision stands on.
