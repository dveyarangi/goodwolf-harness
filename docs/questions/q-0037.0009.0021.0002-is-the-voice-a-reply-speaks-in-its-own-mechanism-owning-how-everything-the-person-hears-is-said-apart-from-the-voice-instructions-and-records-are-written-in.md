## Where it stands

**The problem.** How a reply sounds has three homes in tier 1 that do not know their borders:
*A capable reader*, right for instructions and records and read as governing replies too; five
phrases in skills and the entry file — *in their words*, *in their terms*, *in their language* —
each pulling its own way; and L9, the project's voice, a local rule. Core ships the first two to
every recipient and not the third, so a recipient gets the quarrel and no voice. In this tree the
voice never showed: across the October speech bench, five moments and up to four editions of the
speech rules, no reply reads in it, though L9 stood in every run. Where a skill wrote its own speech
the editions came out near word for word alike; where it bid little, the reply shrank to a line.

**The shape agreed.** The voice is a mechanism of its own. It owns how everything the person hears
in a session is said — a reply, a report told to them, a stop's words — and no record. What is said
at a moment stays the speaking skill's, in its own `## What the person hears`. The voice's value is
its name alone, set by the project in its local file — Lem's here, a voice the model already
knows — with no description and no samples. The voice knows no skill in advance: it is one block,
installed under the heading `## What the person hears` in every skill that holds one, and in the
entry file for a reply outside any skill's moment; a skill joins by holding the heading.

**What it stands on.** The installer installs into explicit targets only; installing one block
into every member of a group is q-0037.0002, whose shape is next to align.

**What changes when it lands.** The fourteen places wrapped as straw dogs bound here are rewritten
through it: the voice phrases in `/harness`, `/maintain`, `/recall`, `/mechanism`, the align's
opening and the entry file go; L9 becomes the voice's value; *A capable reader* keeps to
instructions and records; `/harness`'s `MEETING.md` loses the Lem passage that stands in core.

**Open here.** Where *A reply speaks the method* — what a reply outside a skill's moment tells —
goes; where L9's other clauses go — that speech invents nothing, that texts for a record keep their
forms' exactness; what a recipient that names no voice is told; whether the four skills that speak
without a section — `/ticket`, `/conclude`, `/questions`, `/tdd` — take one.

## Decisions

1. The voice is a mechanism of its own: it declares one setting, the voice everything the person
   hears is said in, whose value is the project's — L9 here, a plain voice for the person until a
   recipient chooses — and installs one rule saying so; the voice of instructions and records is
   left to its own homes *(the user, 2026-10-24)*. Not: a cleanup alone — a recipient would keep the
   conflicting phrases core ships and get no voice; nor the voice written as a list of what not to
   do — rule failure 48, refused.
2. The mechanism's work opens with a pass over the corpus, every text its answer will change
   wrapped as a straw dog bound here *(the user, 2026-10-24)*. Done the same day, 14 places:
   - `AGENTS.md`: *A capable reader*, whole; *in their words* in *A reply speaks the method*; *in
     the person's words* in its stop clause.
   - `local.rules.md`: L9, whole — its text is the setting's value to be.
   - the ticket rules file: P10, whole — *in plain words, each of its terms explained*.
   - `/harness`: *in their language*, *in their terms*; its `MEETING.md`: *in plain words, never a
     table of fragments*, and *the way Lem wrote …* — a project's own voice standing in core.
   - `/maintain`: *in their terms*.
   - `/recall`: *as they would say it*, *in their terms*, *in simple, precise language*.
   - `/mechanism`: *in plain terms*.
3. What is said at a moment is the speaking skill's: each skill decides for itself, in its own
   `What the person hears`. How it is said is the voice's. Records are not the voice's *(the user,
   2026-10-24, twice amending the first wording the same day — first to the voice choosing what of
   a skill's result is said, then back: the sections stay, each skill decides what it says)*. Not:
   the voice choosing what is said from a skill's result — it knows no skill's moment; nor the
   sections dissolved into one general rule — the agent's proposal, refused. Where *A reply speaks
   the method*, what a reply outside any skill's moment tells, goes is left open.
4. The voice's value is the name of the voice and nothing else — here, Lem's — a voice the model
   already knows; no description of it, no samples *(the user, 2026-10-24: Lem is not exact and dry;
   no samples and no description of Lem's voice)*. Not: a description — L9's *plain, exact and dry*
   stood in every run of the speech bench and the voice never showed, and it misdescribes the voice;
   nor samples written here — they would teach their own content and a voice not Lem's. What else L9
   holds — that speech invents nothing, that tables and texts for a record keep their forms'
   exactness — is not the voice's value; its home is settled with the mechanism's rules.
5. The voice reaches every skill that marks itself as speaking, as one installed block, and the
   entry file for a reply outside any skill's moment; the voice knows no skill in advance, and a
   skill joins by marking itself *(the user, 2026-10-24)*. Not: a block per skill authored in the
   voice's rules file — the voice cannot know which skills a tree installs. The installer has no
   group target yet: that is q-0037.0002, whose lean — membership declared from both ends, the
   group in the rules file and the file and anchor in each member — this decision stands on.
6. A skill marks itself as speaking by holding a `## What the person hears` section; the heading is
   both the mark and the anchor the voice's block is installed under. No list, no separate mark; a
   skill the voice never heard of joins by holding the section *(the user, 2026-10-24)*. The
   installer must learn to install one block into every member of such a group — q-0037.0002.
7. Being a speaking skill is no new property: the mechanism shape already asks every mechanism to
   name each output's reader, a person at a stated moment among them. The shape is to require the
   two ends to agree — an output read by the person at a moment of a session in the mechanism's doc
   exactly when its skill holds `## What the person hears` — and the mechanisms' check to hold the
   pair, so a new mechanism falls in or out of the voice's group by what it declares *(the user,
   2026-10-25)*. Not: a mark a skill carries for the voice's sake alone. Lands with the voice, in the
   mechanism shape's format and check.
