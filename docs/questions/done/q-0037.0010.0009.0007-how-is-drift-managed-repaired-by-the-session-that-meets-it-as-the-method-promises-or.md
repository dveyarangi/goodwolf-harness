**Why it is open, the user, 2026-10-23.** The product document promises that a disagreement
between records, or between a record and the code, is repaired where a session meets it, every
session meeting its own — the method is repair by encounter, not audit. The rules do not yet allow
it everywhere: `/recall` reports drift and leaves the fix outside its scope; the entry file's reply
form lists drift *met and not repaired*; `/maintain` repairs over a declared scope. What is to be
settled: which repairs any session makes on meeting them — a stale line, a link, a copy that
parted from its home — and records how; which wait for `/maintain`'s pass; and how a repair made
in passing is kept from becoming a change of substance made without its decision.

## Decisions

1. **Drift a session meets is repaired there, below the dial, by a subagent, in the session's own
   work or in nobody's** *(the user, 2026-10-25)*. Not: repair left to `/maintain`'s passes alone —
   the front page promises repair where a session meets it, and on 2026-10-25 a session carried two
   rows of drift through a day of replies while a separate pass repaired nine of thirteen parted
   pairs; nor every drift met, whatever it decides — a change of substance made under the name of
   a repair, with no decision behind it. → [M2](../../../.agents/mechanisms/maintain/maintain.rules.md)
2. **Drift not repaired where met goes to its owner or becomes a decision** *(the user,
   2026-10-25)*: in another session's work, whatever its rung, it is told to that session; at or
   above the dial in the session's own work or in nobody's, it is a decision in the ⚖️ table, its
   row marked 🍂. A record that does not say which side wins against what it is of is itself
   drift. Not: a 🍂 table of drift met and not repaired — it held only what fell between the
   other two, and let a session carry drift unrepaired without a reason. → [M2](../../../.agents/mechanisms/maintain/maintain.rules.md)
3. **The rule is the maintenance mechanism's**, one rule in its rules file installed into the
   entry file, read on every turn, and into `/recall`, in place of *report drift rather than fixing
   it* *(the user, 2026-10-25)*. Not: the session mechanism's — its rules are about neighbours, not
   records; nor written by hand in the entry file — a mechanism's rule reaches it only as an
   installed block. → [M2](../../../.agents/mechanisms/maintain/maintain.rules.md)
