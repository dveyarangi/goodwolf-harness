## Decisions

1. **The judge looks for load-bearing parts alone** *(the user, 2026-10-12)*: every shape hides
   unbounded detail; what matters is a part that, once examined, changes the shape's contract — the
   entry file's *does expanding its structure change the contract or even what the shape is*. The
   two runs below asked for any part that could be named, and read nothing from them about a judge
   asked this. → [the judge's prompt](../../research/judge-bench/prompt.txt)
2. **A draft whose result proved wrong or misleading missed a part** *(the user, 2026-10-12)*: the
   bench's key labels by what came of the result, not by whether the draft hedged. A ticket's size
   counts too: it matters to the model. → [the bench](../../research/judge-bench-2026-10-12.md#the-set)
3. **The judge is recorded as it stands** *(the user, 2026-10-12)*: it flags about half of the
   hidden parts and is right three times in four when it flags; if it cannot generalize, it is
   taught later, under [q-0037.0001.0020.0002](../q-0037.0001.0020.0002-how-is-the-judge-taught-to-generalize-so-it-flags-a-part-only-what-the-shape-is-for-reveals.md).
