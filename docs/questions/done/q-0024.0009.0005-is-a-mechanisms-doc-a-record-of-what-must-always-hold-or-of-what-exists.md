## Decisions

1. **What exists is the thing being right, by whatever hand** *(the user, 2026-10-10)*: the
   glossary's *never edited by hand* goes — no mechanical way to make a doc exists, and how a
   record is brought to the thing is no axis of the sort. The invariants, what must always hold,
   live in the instructions and the tests.

**The user, 2026-10-10, on the next fork** (part of an architecture is invariants, part not; are
invariants ADRs?): *the whole idea is to be able to see drift, not to hang labels for show — think
hard about what we are doing.* A `/discover` pass followed:
[what a record drifting from its thing is an instance of](../../research/discover-record-drift-2026-10-10.md).

**The user, 2026-10-10:** a mechanism's doc leads its instructions after all — *sometimes leading,
sometimes following is no place to be*; a change by decision moves both in one landing, and drift
is the instruction moving without one. The fear: `what must always hold`, met without the
glossary, reads as *never edit this doc*.

**A cold read, 2026-10-10.** Three agents, one per label set, were shown the same four files — a
procedure's doc, a work item, a host's observations, a session journal — each opening
`- **record of** <label>`, and asked what the label says, whether they may edit the file, and
which side they would change on a disagreement. The sets: the current four; `what will be / what
must be / what is / what was`; `intent / standard / state / history`. **All three read all four
files as meant**: the procedure edited only when the task is to change the rule, with the user's
approval, the code repaired to it; the work item refined, the code moving toward it; the
observations brought to the system; the journal left alone. One sample each, one model, the
entry file in their context (one of them answered with its entry-contract line), and the files'
own bodies — a status line, *Observed*, *We tried* — hinting the answer. The fear was not borne
out; the test could not tell the sets apart.

2. **A mechanism's doc stays `what must always hold`** *(the user, 2026-10-10)*: it leads its
   instructions and changes by decision; the four names stay, `what decides` weighed and left —
   read after *record of* it names the one who decides, and leans toward a decision record.
3. **The mark says how a record is to be treated, not which thing it is paired with** *(the user,
   2026-10-10)*: a unary label cannot carry a direction, having no second end. Drift and the side
   to repair live where a pair is declared and something notices it — a mechanism's doc and its
   parts under the maintenance clock, an edge's invariant and its validator. A mark naming its
   referent waits on
   [q-0024.0009.0006](../q-0024.0009.0006-how-is-a-record-paired-with-the-thing-it-is-of-so-that-drift-between-them-is-noticed.md).
