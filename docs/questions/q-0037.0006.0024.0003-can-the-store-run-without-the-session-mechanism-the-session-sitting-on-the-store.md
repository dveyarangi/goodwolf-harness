maintain taken as the first package: why does the store import the session? Read by a fresh

**What `questions.py` takes from `sessions.py`.** At `--window`: the session seen, its holds, the
window memory that is also its sign of life, the rows, the other running sessions and their holds.
At `--wake`: registering and tending rows. At `--hook`: the tag, forgetting the window, being seen,
arriving. At every event: a tag not registered is refused, and the row is written after `at`; on a
rename the rows' ids are rewritten; `--check` checks the rows against the store; the path borrows
the session's `GONE_AFTER`. None of it touches the store's own data: entries, strikes, open, move,
close, `--tree` and `--seed` need nothing of the session. The reverse holds too: `sessions.py`,
whose docstring says the row stands without the store, imports from `questions.py` its refusal and
finding types, the id pattern its rows are written in, and the store's reader. Both test suites
import the other's.

**Essential and accidental.** Essential is one thing, a current position: `at` and the window's
path are defined from it. Registration, liveness, holds, other sessions, the wake, the hook's
session half, the rows' check and their renames are the session's duties, moved to it in its rules
on 2026-10-22 while the code stayed in `questions.py`. Two records disagree with the code:
`session.md` says the hook's session half is one call into the session script, where the hook makes
seven; `sessions.py` says the row stands without the store.

**The cut proposed.** Invert the dependency: the store exposes seams and imports nothing of the
session — the window takes the position and extra lines as arguments, `at` returns where it placed
the session and writes no row, a rename takes the extra records to rewrite, `--check` covers
entries, the window memory moves to the store whose record `questions.md` already says it is, the
path keeps its own constant; `sessions.py` takes the wake, the hook's half by an optional import,
the other sessions and holds, the row write, the row renames and their check. Without the session
mechanism a window draws from no position or from an explicit `--at q-N`, and `at` still strikes.
About 200 lines move; Q1's `--session` becomes optional and the session rule pointing at
`questions.py --wake` is amended; the hook files stay; the hook, wake and other-sessions tests move
to the session's suite.

**Re-checked at the align, 2026-10-25.** The reading holds: `questions.py` imports `sessions` at
load (line 54), so a tree without `sessions.py` has no store at all; `sessions.py` takes the
store's `Refused`, `Diagnostic`, `Skipped`, `Finding`, `_ID`, `STORE` and `read_store` inside its
functions. The two disagreeing records were repaired in `a118773` and wrapped against this

names the hook wiring and its entry the questions mechanism's — and `at` is the agent's call into
the store's script. Moving the session's code into `sessions.py` therefore leaves nobody to call
it: the store must call out, at `at`, at the hook's start and message, at a rename, at `--check`
and in the window, to a mechanism it does not import. That is the seam q-0037.0006.0024.0001 left
open — a method adding readiness and the ticket counter to the path and the window — met a second
time. And q-0037.0017.0005.0002 moves the rows into a git ref namespace (`92bdda4`), so the row
code the cut moves is being rewritten by the session on that question.
The unit of work is settled apart: the store, the session and maintain read it through a seam,
perhaps one local rule installed into all three
([q-0037.0006.0024.0002](q-0037.0006.0024.0002-does-every-rule-of-the-entry-file-belong-to-a-mechanism-so-that-an-install-adds-the.md)
decision 6, `d88777b`) — a record of which records are units, read by the scripts. The session's

store's moments, which no rule can carry.

## Decisions

1. **One package for now, the dependency to be inverted inside** *(the user, 2026-10-26)*: the
   store can stand without the session, the session never without the store. They are installed
   together, with maintain, since a store left without the session and maintain may drift; the
   inversion is for correctness, not for installing them apart. Not: the three offered apart.
   → [session.md, Delivery](../../.agents/mechanisms/session/session.md)
2. **Each mechanism's hook in its turn** *(the user, 2026-10-26)*: the hooks' wrapper runs the
   hook of each installed mechanism in turn; the store calls no one, and the session's hook reads
   the store. Not: a list of listeners the store calls; not the session taking over the store's
   entry points. → [session.md, Delivery](../../.agents/mechanisms/session/session.md)
3. **The cut deferred** *(the user, 2026-10-26)*: "оставим это на будущее, пока оставим все как
   есть, все равно же устанавливаем пакет целиком". The code stays as it is while the three
   install as one package. → [session.md, Delivery](../../.agents/mechanisms/session/session.md)

## For the cut, when it comes due

Weighed with the user and judged by a fresh agent, 2026-10-26; nothing of it is decided beyond
the list above.

- **Where a chat stands.** Two shapes. The session places: `sessions.py at q-A` asks the store to
  validate and strike, then writes its row, so others see the move at once; Q1 and an S-rule then
  name two calls for one act, and no rule can override another's. The store places:
  `questions.py at` stays the one call, the store keeps the position in its per-chat memory, the
  session's hook copies it into the row, others see it a turn late; S9's hard stop rests on holds,
  which `sessions.py --hold` writes at once, so the lag touches only the courtesy of telling a
  neighbour. The judge and this session leaned to the second.
- **The store needs a chat's identity, not its position**: the unchanged-window line and the
  counters' deltas are kept per chat (`questions.py` `window`, `_counters`); the key becomes the
  host's conversation id, opaque, with `memory` and `tag_of` moving to the store.
- **Hooks in turn break a host that reads JSON**: each answer is one object (`_hook_specific`,
  `_cursor_answer`); the wrapper must gather plain context and render the host's form once, so
  `HOSTS` leaves the store; the store's hook must not draw the roots beside the session's window.
- **Renames**: today the store rewrites rows' ids (`_records_naming_ids`, `sessions.renamed`);
  without a call-out it needs a record of its renames, old to new, for the session to read.
- **Smaller**: `--session` optional; `--wake`'s registering and S1's pointer at it move to the
  session; `_landed`'s "(from q-left)" is the session's print; the fingerprint's holds and taken
  marks are the session's half.
