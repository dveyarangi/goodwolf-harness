## Where it stands

A spec is no longer a record of this method: the question a piece of work answers is its working
document ([q-0037.0009.0017](done/q-0037.0009.0017-what-does-spec-own.md), decision 1). Five specs remain
in `docs/spec/` as this tree's own documents, which nothing in core reads. Each is folded into its
question at that question's next touch — its argument into `Where it stands`, its decisions into
`Decisions` or their durable homes, its story into dated evidence — and its file leaves once
nothing cites it, the citing records repointed to the question.

- `01-0010` harness spec, a draft → [q-0037.0006](q-0037.0006-how-do-projects-share-one-development-method-without-losing-their-own-conventions.md).
