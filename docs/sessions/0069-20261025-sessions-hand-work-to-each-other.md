  the entries, so a taken holder read *unchanged*. The builder's test drew only a first window,
  which is always whole; the first verify judge found it with a probe. Fixed; the lesson is the
  principle's own: a validator written by the builder passes for the builder's reason.
- **Rules landed the turn they were shown** — the neighbour sentence; entry 58 struck. Showing in the
  same reply is not showing before acting.
- **Two sessions numbered one contract version** (entry 75), and two session records share 0068 —
  hand-bumped counters shared by sessions running at once.
- **A clause an open question will change could not be wrapped** in its rules file (entry 77).
- Cold cases that read the question body knew they were cases; a judge's brief must not point at the
  builder's summaries without saying so.
