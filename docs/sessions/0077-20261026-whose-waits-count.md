## Decisions

- **The case strikes rule failure 55, not a new entry.** Entry 55 had found the same boundary —
  whose waits count — met through a record; here it was met through another session's message.
  The ⚖️ clause said how long a wait is carried, never how its question is read, and the session
  read it from what the answer would rewrite (a skill it held), not from what it decides.
- **The rule goes into the session mechanism's S9, not the ⚖️ clause.** First drafted into the
  entry file's reply form. The user: *почему это правила agents.md а не механизмов?* S9 already
  governs another session's work; the sentence landed there and was reinstalled (v129): a decision
  on another running session's question or unit, read from what it decides, is that session's to
  put to the person, though its message brings it.
- **Every change is shown Before and After, with `−N +M`.** The user's *первая починка*, after not
  following two proposals shown whole. Weighed: the debugging mechanism as its home; refuted by
  q-0037.0009.0021's decision 3 — what is shown before landing is the dial's, seen whatever debug
  is. The user: *пока пусть всегда*. Landed in the reply form (v128), bound with it to the voice
  question, the voice not yet a mechanism.
- **R5 opens with finding the mechanism, and reaches `/skill-up`.** The user: *почему наш скиллап
  не инструктирует поискать подходящий механизм прежде чем класть правило в корень?* R5 existed
  but was installed only into `/align` and `/mechanism`, never into the skill every instruction is
  drafted through. Registered as a new entry, kin to 11; a rewording, since no script tells a
  principle from a mechanism's rule.
- **A commit names its paths (S5).** Rule failure 56 struck: another session's bare commit, 588d90e,
  took this session's staged S9 work under its own subject. The sentence landed in S5, again by
  R5, not in `/commit`'s body. Its refinement for a file two sessions both edited — commit HEAD's
  text with one's own changes only — the user set aside: *это слишком жирно*.
- **The closed Dial steps row was ended by hand.** Messages sent to it after its conclude turned
  its row running, so others' windows showed it on the front page and addressed it; it then
  recorded a withdrawn message (af7f7e9) and relayed what the product session already had. The
  user: *мне кажется мы уже починили это* — and it is designed:
  [01-0011.0100.0060](../rfc/done/01-0011.0100.0060-a-silent-session-ends-at-the-next-wake.md)
  turns a concluded session spoken to again running, and three silent hours end it.

## What went wrong, and what it taught

- **A test that does not reproduce.** The S9 sentence was tried on two fresh agents, current text
  and amended; both kept the queue's fate out. The live slip came more than a thousand messages
  into a session. A short scene does not judge a rule whose failure needs a long context.
- **The person could not follow dense rule text.** Two rounds of *ничего не понял* before the rules
  were said in one plain sentence each. Proposals now show Before and After.
- **Commits outside L8's list.** Two commits were prefixed `REGISTER`: `/commit` was never opened.
  Rule failure 37 struck; a commit-msg hook proposed, not built.
