## Principles without a home yet

- **Pushed only by the person, turned only by its own project.** In live cases 5 and 7 a reader
  turned only when asked about its own project, and the person asked it each time. A page that
  asks the question itself is the only scalable version.
- **A check a reader cannot answer for the product is a promise.** Worktree sessions and checks
  run at commit were held off the page until built.
