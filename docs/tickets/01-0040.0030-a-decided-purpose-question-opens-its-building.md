## What to build

- **Allocation**, installed from the product rules file into `/align`: the decision lands through
  `/product`, never written into the document by `/align` itself (decision 10).
- **The statement at opening**, installed into the questions skill: a question opened directly under
  the structure root names its answers or says it is derived; no refusal in the store, which knows
  nothing of the product (decision 4).
- **The zigzag** (decision 6): building that shows an answer or its causes must change opens a
  purpose question from the structure work; its decision adds or replaces answers, never
  renumbering one.
- **A tree's purpose root named once** as a project-local answer where the store predates the seed;
  this tree's is q-0033.
- Each rule holds where a project keeps a product document — a condition bound to q-0018.0024.
