## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] In a tree set to the second mode, two sessions each in a worktree of its own see each
      other's position and one tree of questions, and two questions opened at once get two
      ids — observed by someone who did not build it.
- [ ] A tree in the first mode, this one and a recipient after an update, behaves as before: the
      window unchanged, the store's and the session's tests green.
- [ ] Every child ticket is Done.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket and the governing docs.
