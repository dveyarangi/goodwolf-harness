## What to build

Decision 9: the coordinator keeps the pairs a move computes, and the path pairs of rewords
and archives, which change a file's name without a new id; a repair-only pass of `move_doc`
runs a branch's links through them before its merge, moving no file. Paired closes stay as
they are.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A doc on a branch linking to a question moved on main meanwhile links to its new home
      after the repair, and the store's check is clean after the merge.
- [ ] A paired close of a ticket and its RFC moves and repairs as before.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket and the governing docs.

## Out of scope

- Ids that do not encode their place in the tree: refused, decision 9.
