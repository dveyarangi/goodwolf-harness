## What to build

- **A voice mechanism**, declared to the mechanism shape: it owns how everything the person hears
  in a session is said, and no record. Its one setting is the voice's name, set by the project in
  its local file as `debug` is; this tree's is Lem's. No description and no samples, because a
  description narrowed the voice wrongly and samples teach their own content.
- **One rule**, installed in the entry file for a reply outside any skill's moment and, as a group,
  under every `## What the person hears`, so the voice reaches skills it never heard of.
- **Each skill's own section stays its own**: what is said at a moment is the skill's.
- **The mechanism shape** requires that a mechanism's output read by the person at a moment of a
  session and its skill's `## What the person hears` agree, and the mechanisms' check holds the
  pair; the skills that speak without a section today are settled here, since the check fails on
  them at once.
- **The fourteen texts** wrapped as straw dogs bound to the question are rewritten through it: the
  voice phrases go, L9 gives way to the setting, *A capable reader* keeps to instructions and
  records, and the Lem passage in `/harness`'s meeting document leaves core.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A tree whose local file names a voice carries the voice's block in the entry file and under
      every `## What the person hears`, and nowhere else.
- [ ] A skill that gains a `## What the person hears` gains the voice's block at the next install,
      and the check reports it until then.
- [ ] A mechanism declaring an output the person reads at a moment, with no such section in its
      skill, fails the mechanisms' check; and the reverse.
- [ ] No text of core describes a voice or names this tree's.
- [ ] The speech bench, run on its five moments, reads in the named voice, judged by a reader who
      did not build it.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.
