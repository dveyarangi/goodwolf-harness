## What to build

- **The voice is one rule of the local file**, L9, naming the voice and nothing more of it — no
  description, no samples, since a description narrowed the voice wrongly. Its two clauses that are
  not the voice stay beside the name.
- **It reaches two places**: the entry file's `## Project-local`, as now, for a reply outside any
  skill's moment; and the group `.agents/skills/*/SKILL.md` · `## What the person hears`, so every
  skill that speaks carries it where it speaks, those the tree adds later included.
- **Core holds no voice and stops contradicting one**: the fourteen texts wrapped as straw dogs
  bound to the question are rewritten — the voice phrases go, *A capable reader* keeps to
  instructions and records, and the Lem passage in `/harness`'s meeting document leaves core.
- Each skill's own section stays its own: what is said at a moment is the skill's.
