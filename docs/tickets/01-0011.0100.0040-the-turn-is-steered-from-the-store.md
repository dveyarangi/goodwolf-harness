# The turn is steered from the store

- **Status:** Planned
- **Type:** HITL
- **Depends on:** [01-0011.0100.0010 the-store-and-questions-writes-it](./01-0011.0100.0010-the-store-and-questions-writes-it.md)
  (the store, which is the state to steer from)
- **Related:** [01-0010.0120 host-delivery-surfaces](./01-0010.0120-host-delivery-surfaces.md) —
  where the judge runs unasked; until then the turn calls it
- **Outcome:** At every turn the current question is read from the store, the request is judged
  for which open question it addresses and whether it leaves the current one — up, down, or to
  nothing — the shift is named, a premature descent is steered back and a worthwhile one follows
  after the question being left is written as a semi-formed decision, and none of it depends on
  the agent watching its own tokens.

## What to build

- The steering judge on the request: observed question, shift, whether a descent expands a higher
  shape, confidence — `jev` through a script, or `agent` through a subagent, `off` to skip; in the
  local file.
- The turn rule at tier 1, hand-written and wrapped as a straw dog on
  [01-0020](./01-0020-pacer.md), which owns the turn: read the current question from the store;
  judge the request; name a shift; on a premature descent, steer back and ask; on a worthwhile
  one, write the question being left — settled, leaning, open — and follow; rising is free.
- The written question as a store entry, replacing the interim homes.
- Judge ≠ generator: the main drafts and emits, the judge reads; the reply opens with the
  question it answers at.

## Acceptance criteria — provisional, firmed at the align

- [ ] A request judged against the store returns the question it addresses, or none, and the
  shift's direction, with a confidence; each of the three judge modes produces the same shape of
  verdict, and `off` produces none.
- [ ] A descent taken while the current question is unsettled leaves that question written in
  the store as a semi-formed decision before the reply goes below.
- [ ] The tier-1 rule is in the entry file, wrapped on the pacer, and a fresh session's turn
  after `/recall` opens with the question it answers at.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its ticket, RFC, and governing docs.
