## Decisions landed at this ticket's align

The evidence the align opened on: in the session of 2026-10-04 to 2026-10-05 the agent, placing
alone, showed the right question on one turn of seven — a maintenance pass shown against the
session's stale position, and an installer defect placed on a sibling that resembled it, *what a
first install says when something stops it*, for an update that removed links.

1. **A turn is one of three kinds** *(the user, 2026-10-05)*: on a question; a process — the agent
   carrying out what it knows how to do, a maintenance pass, a commit, the wake — which is a
   question only where the agent does not know how, or where running it turns one up; or
   uncharted.
2. **Placement is by containment, never by resemblance** *(the user, 2026-10-05)*: a message lands
   on the lowest question that contains it. A sibling that looks alike is not its home; the move
   from a question that does not contain it is upward.
3. **A missing parent is opened, and shown as new** *(the user, 2026-10-05)*: where only a far
   question contains the message and several of its children resemble it, the parent between them
   is missing; a question the agent can word is opened by the agent in the turn, and the reply
   marks it new. It waits on nobody.
4. **Uncharted means the home takes the user's decision** *(the user, 2026-10-05)*: a question
   whose home the agent cannot settle. The reply asks, and the ask stands until answered. No turn
   count: questions arrive in quick succession, and one that can be told apart from the next
   already has a parent to stand on.
5. **A process turn names its process** *(the user, 2026-10-05)*: under `debug=on` the reply's
   table shows the process and no question, the stored position left as it was and not shown; a
   process run for a question shows the question.
6. **The rows** *(the user, 2026-10-05)*: `↳` a question the turn stands on, as today; `+` a
   question opened in the turn, with no other word for new; `▶` a process; `?` uncharted.
7. **The rules land first, and the judge is decided from what they show** *(the user,
   2026-10-05)*: the placements failed because the rule gave no honest answer for a process or a
   near miss, and a judge handed that rule would have been forced the same way.
8. **The agent gathers the children under a missing parent itself** *(the user, 2026-10-05, at
   the plan)*: a grouping that is correct and improves the tree is worth its renames, and judging
   that is the agent's; the renames are the script's. A grouping it cannot settle is uncharted.
