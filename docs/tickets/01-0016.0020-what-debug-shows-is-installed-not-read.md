## What to build

A `debugging` mechanism under `.agents/mechanisms/`, whose rules file holds everything a tree shows
the person about its own working, sent to the files that now say it under `debug=on`. Installed, a
tree shows it; retracted, the same files read plain. One text never holds both readings behind a
switch a session reads *(the user, 2026-10-18)*, because a reader who must evaluate the switch pays
for both readings and can take the wrong one.

What moves is every straw dog bound to q-0037.0009.0021 on 2026-10-20 that says what shows under
`debug=on`, as `straw_dogs.py` lists them:

- the entry file's announce line, and the harness doc's two mentions of the session announcing it;
- the reply form's parts it gives to `debug=on`: the 💡 🕯️ 無 marks, the 🧩 ⚓ ✏️ frames, the
  footnotes, the 🛠️ dial, the 🪢 table, and the 🪶 commits table with the counters line — the 🪢
  table's words since 2026-10-22 the session mechanism's rule S4, an installed block that says
  *under `debug=on`* inside itself, so the debugging mechanism either switches a block another
  mechanism owns or the debug half of S4 becomes its own rule, a decision of this ticket's align;
- *A reply speaks the method*'s last sentence;
- `/maintain`'s *what was wrong in it*;
- what the two scripts print for debugging: `questions.py`'s landed `at` line, and the announce line
  `harness.py` stamps for a recipient's session to repeat.

The reply form's parts that stand debug or not — the ⚖️ and 🍂 tables — stay where they are. The
head table and its 🔁 row go where q-0037.0009.0021 puts interaction's share.

## Acceptance criteria — provisional, firmed at the align on q-0037.0009.0021.0001

- [ ] Each text listed under *What to build* stands in the debugging mechanism's rules file, and
      reaches its target as that mechanism's installed block.
- [ ] With debugging installed, a session's replies show what they showed under `debug=on` on
      2026-10-20 — read by the person in one real session.
- [ ] With debugging retracted, the same session's replies show none of it, and every file it
      touched reads plain and byte-identical to its state before the install — read by the person,
      and by the injector's retraction check.
- [ ] Debugging is switched on and off by one stated call, made by whom
      q-0037.0009.0021.0001.0001 decides; where a block's retraction leaves a sentence that must
      read otherwise, its plain counterpart is swapped in as q-0037.0009.0021.0001.0002 decides.
- [ ] What the two scripts give for debugging reaches the person only while the tree has it
      installed, as q-0037.0009.0021.0001.0003 decides; no script or text in core reads
      `debug=on`.
- [ ] L4 is gone from `local.rules.md` and this tree has debugging installed; a recipient whose local
      file says `debug=on` comes out of its next update with debugging installed and the line gone.
- [ ] No straw dog in the tree is bound to q-0037.0009.0021 for what debug shows; the TODOs in
      `harness.py` and `questions.py` are gone; q-0037.0009.0021's decision 2 no longer says
      *meanwhile*.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.
