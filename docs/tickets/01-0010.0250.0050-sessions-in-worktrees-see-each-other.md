## What to build

The row's life goes through the coordinator (decision 11): registering, seen, silence, the
end, a hold, a take, a release, and `at`. The sign of life goes with `seen`, or the
coordinator's wake would end every session after three hours; the window's fingerprint stays
the session's own. Whether the rows are tracked in git is [q-0037.0017.0005.0003](../questions/q-0037.0017.0005.0003-do-the-sessions-rows-stay-tracked-in-git-once-a-coordinator-can-hold-them.md)'s. The session
mechanism's doc on one folder is rewritten to what then holds.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two sessions in two worktrees each see the other's position in its window and the wake's
      read.
- [ ] A unit one holds shows as held in the other's window; a take moves it, as in the first
      mode.
- [ ] A session silent past the limit is ended by the next wake, and one that keeps talking is
      not.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket and the governing docs.
