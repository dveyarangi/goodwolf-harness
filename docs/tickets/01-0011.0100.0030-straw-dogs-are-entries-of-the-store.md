# The straw dogs are entries of the store

- **Status:** Planned
- **Type:** HITL

- **Outcome:** Every straw dog in the tree is an entry of the store — a decision landed with an
  open question bound to the ticket that will answer it — the listing is a view of those entries,
  and a reconciliation proves the wrapped text and the store hold one set with nothing lost.

## What to build

- Each straw dog read from where it is wrapped and written as an entry of the store: the
  question its `until` states, bound to the ticket its binding names, closing when that ticket
  closes or the condition is met.
- The `<straw-dog>` syntax and the entry file's duty to wrap stay where they are; the listing
  script's output becomes a view of the store's entries, or the store indexes the wrapped text.
- A reconciliation test: the straw dogs the listing finds and the entries the store holds for
  them are one set.

## Acceptance criteria — provisional, firmed at the align

- [ ] Every straw dog the listing finds is an entry of the store, bound to the same ticket.
- [ ] Retiring a straw dog closes its entry by the recorded kind, and a straw dog bound to a
  closed ticket is reported as due.
- [ ] The reconciliation test passes and fails when one straw dog is dropped from either side.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its ticket, RFC, and governing docs.
