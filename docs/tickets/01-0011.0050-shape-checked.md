## Why this exists

[01-0011.0010](./done/01-0011.0010-mechanism-declared.md) ships a check that **one** declaration is
*true* — its parts exist, its files are distinct, its gaps name tickets. It ran over the mechanism
that declared it and nothing else; `.0022` and `.0025` made it a sweep across every declared
mechanism. This slice adds the other direction, so a skill that belongs to no mechanism is
reported rather than invisible, and gives the gap its straw-dog form so it can ship.

The allowlist half has its own reason. Once rules are injected, a rule can be written into a skill
that no mechanism knows about, and nothing would be able to enumerate it. A skill belonging to no
mechanism is listed with its reason, because an allowlist is how a gap stays declared instead of
becoming invisible.
