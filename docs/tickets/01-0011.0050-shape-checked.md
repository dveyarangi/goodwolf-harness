## Why this exists

[01-0011.0010](./01-0011.0010-mechanism-declared.md) ships a check that **one** declaration is
*true* — its parts exist, its files are distinct, its gaps name tickets. It runs over the mechanism
that declared it and nothing else. This slice makes it a sweep: across every declared mechanism,
and in both directions, so a skill that belongs to no mechanism is reported rather than invisible.

The allowlist half has its own reason. Once rules are injected, a rule can be written into a skill
that no mechanism knows about, and nothing would be able to enumerate it. A skill belonging to no
mechanism is listed with its reason, because an allowlist is how a gap stays declared instead of
becoming invisible.

## What to build

- A check reporting, across every declared mechanism, each moment's instruction or which kind of
  absence it carries — instructed elsewhere and named, embedded in the wrong home, unowned by
  design, or not yet with its ticket.
- Failure, not a warning, on a `not yet` that names no ticket or a ticket that does not exist, and
  on a doc and instruction file that are the same file.
- **Read in both directions**: rows name parts, and the reverse pass asks whether every installed
  skill is named by some mechanism. Every skill named or present in an allowlist with a written
  reason. Fifteen are installed as of 2026-09-07 and three are declared, so most will be
  allowlisted at first — the honest reading rather than a failure.
- Absences distinguished rather than counted. *Elsewhere* looks like a gap and is not; *embedded*
  looks covered and is not; they pull opposite ways and both get missed.

## Input from `01-0011.0022`'s plan, 2026-09-07

**The report is the map of maintenance owed across the corpus** *(the user)*. Each `not yet` or
`embedded` row it lists is work for a skill not yet through inception, and

clause where the taxonomy already puts it.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The check reports every moment of every declared mechanism, with its instruction or its kind
  of absence.
- [ ] A `not yet` naming no ticket, or a ticket that does not exist, fails the run.
- [ ] A doc and instruction file that are the same file fails the run.
- [ ] Every installed skill is named by a mechanism or allowlisted with a reason; none is silent.
- [ ] The four kinds of absence are distinguishable in the output, not collapsed into a count.
- [ ] A skill named by no mechanism and absent from the allowlist is reported.
- [ ] The check is part of the project's verification set.
- [ ] Behavioral tests cover each kind of absence, both failures, and an unlisted skill.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its ticket, RFC, and governing docs.
