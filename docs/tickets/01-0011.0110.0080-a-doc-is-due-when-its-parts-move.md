## What to build

- The clock's two levels are `parts` — a mechanism's doc against the files it owns — and
  `output` — its outputs against its doc, rules file and the formats beside its skill. Today's
  `rules` is the mechanism shape's `output`, read one mechanism at a time: every mechanism's doc
  and skill are the shape's outputs. One row per mechanism stays.
- `/maintain`'s A1 gains the three outcomes of a due pair: a landed decision moved the part and
  the doc lagged — the doc is amended; none did — the part is repaired to the doc, or the question
  taken to `/align`; the move touches nothing the doc says — nothing to change. Its other text
  naming the levels, the maintain mechanism's doc and the marks' format follow.
- The edge record's line for recipients says what their marks become.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A part a mechanism owns moving makes its doc due at `parts`, naming the part; a part it
      relies on moving does not — each tested.
- [ ] A change to the mechanism shape's parts makes every mechanism's doc due, one row each.
- [ ] `/maintain` re-checks a due pair to one of the three outcomes.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against the align's decisions and the governing docs.
