# An output declares its kind

- **Status:** Planned (`.0010` supplies the names)
- **Type:** AFK
- **Depends on:** [01-0011.0110.0010 the-kinds-have-names](./01-0011.0110.0010-the-kinds-have-names.md)
  (the kinds the format names)
- **Answers:** [q-0024.0009.0002](../questions/q-0024.0009.0002-how-does-a-mechanism-declare-the-kind-of-each-output-and-how-does-maintenance-repair-by-kind.md)
- **Outcome:** Every output a mechanism declares names its kind beside its reader, the shape
  check reports one that does not, and a maintenance pass repairs a disagreement in the direction
  the kind gives — the derivative for the second kind, the record for the third — instead of
  deciding by feel.

## Parent

[Every written thing has a source of truth](./01-0011.0110-every-written-thing-has-a-source-of-truth.md),
third of five slices: the mechanism shape and maintain. `/impact`: the parent's.

## What to build

- The mechanism format's *What it produces, and who reads it* carries the kind of each output;
  the shape check reads it and reports an output with no kind or with a kind outside the four.
  The five declared mechanisms' docs are brought to it.
- `/maintain`'s *docs to their implementation, both ways* becomes two repairs by kind: a record
  of the second kind repairs its derivative, one of the third is regenerated from it. The levels
  and the marks are unchanged.
- An instruction — a skill, a rule — is the mechanism itself and no record of it; for the
  mechanism shape, which produces instructions, a skill is a record of the second kind, and the
  invariants of that kind are `/skill-up`'s, which says so.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every declared mechanism's doc names a kind for each output, and the shape check is clean.
- [ ] An output declared without a kind, or with a kind outside the four, is a diagnostic; with
      a kind it is not — both polarities tested.
- [ ] `/maintain` names the repair direction by kind, and the next maintenance pass cites it.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against the spec and the governing docs.
