## What to build

The session mechanism, `session`, declared to [the shape](../../.agents/skills/mechanism/MECHANISM-FORMAT.md)
with its doc, its rules file and its evidence, and `/mechanism`'s inception steps run on it: the
check before the thing it checks, the count of prior violations, what retires it — nothing, it is
always on — and what would show it working.

- **Its records**, declared where the shape says: the row, one file per session in
  `docs/questions/sessions.d/`, gone seven days after its session ended, because a chat the host
  reopens finds its row under the same id until then; and the session record under
  `docs/sessions/`, written at the conclude and kept. The sessions section leaves
  `QUESTION-FORMAT.md`, which declares entries alone.
- **Its skills**, `/recall` and `/conclude`, each opening with `Mechanism: session`, the two ends
  of every session whatever method runs between them. The method's rules reach them as installed
  blocks: of the questions mechanism's Q2 and Q3, the wake's read and `--end` become the
  session's, the `at` and the leans stay the questions mechanism's.
- **Its script**, the row's whole life out of the question script — the folder, registering,
  seen, silence after three hours, end, resume under the host's steadiest id, the removal — with
  its tests; the question script asks it for the other sessions' positions, and the hook calls
  both at start. The wake's read lists running sessions only.
- **Its rules on how sessions interact**, authored in its rules file and installed where read:
  the maintain pass's commit of rows another wake changed, from `/maintain`'s F5; the 🪢 Tangle
  block of the reply's layout under debug, into the entry file under its straw dog; a recall
  re-ranking nothing another running session is on; one checkout and one main shared, a commit
  holding only its own session's work, where `/commit` and the architecture state them.
- **Seams, not fields**: a lock pinning a session to one method, and a role, each a setting at
  the session's start that the row admits and nothing writes yet.

The single-file carry-over from before the folder, and whose the hook wiring is, are the RFC's to
decide. The glossary's *Session* stands as landed at the align.
