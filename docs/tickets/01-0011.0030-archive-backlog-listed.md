# Finished work that has not been archived is listed, and the backlog only ratchets down

- **Status:** Planned (the record shape precedes)
- **Type:** AFK

  declared, and a declared record shape for the listing to read)
- **Outcome:** A command lists records whose work is finished and whose folder does not say so, and
  the standing count is held as a ratchet — silent at or below its mark, loud when it rises.

[A mechanism has one home for its rules, and a derived index](../spec/01-0011-mechanism-shape.md).

## Why this exists

`/maintain` was changed on 2026-09-06 to say archiving is not scope-limited: whatever scope a pass
declares, it looks at the whole tree for records whose work is finished and whose folder does not
say so. Nothing sweeps. The five tickets that prompted the rule were found with a shell loop written
by hand, and a rule enforced by diligence is how the previous one decayed.

`<temporary>` statements have an enumerator and held all day. This is the missing twin.

## What to build

- A command that reports records finished and unarchived, deciding eligibility from the acceptance
  boxes including `/verify`, and reporting rather than moving. The move stays a judgment.
- A ratchet, not a threshold: hold the count, stay silent at or below the mark, speak when it
  **rises**. Lower the mark freely as the backlog clears — that is what progress looks like — and
  raise it only by a stated change of definition with the cause written beside the number. A
  re-baseline and the drift the mark exists to catch are otherwise indistinguishable.
- Both ticket header forms parsed. Sixteen records use `- **Status:**` and two use bare
  `**Status:**`; a scan on 2026-09-06 mis-read exactly those two.
- An unreadable or unparseable record reported, never skipped. A scan that silently narrows and
  reports success is the defect already repaired once in `temporary_statements.py`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The command lists records that are finished and unarchived, and lists nothing when the tree is
  clean.
- [ ] A record with an unchecked acceptance box, including `/verify`, is not listed as eligible.
- [ ] Both ticket header forms are parsed; neither is mis-read.
- [ ] An unparseable record is reported, and the run does not claim a complete scan.
- [ ] The ratchet is silent at or below its mark and speaks when the count rises.
- [ ] Lowering the mark needs no ceremony; raising it requires a stated cause recorded beside the
  number.
- [ ] Behavioral tests cover eligible, ineligible, both header forms, unparseable, and each ratchet
  direction.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its ticket, RFC, and governing docs.

## Out of scope

Performing the moves, which stays `/maintain`'s judgment. Listing anything other than archive
eligibility. The queue index ([0040](./01-0011.0040-queue-derived-index.md)), which derives a view
of records rather than reporting what they owe.
