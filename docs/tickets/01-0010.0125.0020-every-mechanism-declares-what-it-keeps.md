## What to build

Each of the nine mechanisms writes its table: its records — the question store, the session rows,
the tickets and RFCs, the product document, the edge records, the marks, the evidence — and the
parts it guards, each with its skill. A record a script alone writes is declared with no skill, so
a commit that stages it asks for none. The shape check's hand-held list of record paths retires
into the tables, as do the straw dogs that wait on a mechanism declaring its records.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each mechanism's doc carries its table, and the shape check and `/maintain` read records
      from the tables, with no list of record paths held by hand.
- [ ] A record written by script alone is declared with no skill, and nothing asks for one at its
      edit or its commit.
- [ ] In Claude Code an edit to a ticket with `/ticket` not loaded, and to a question's body with
      `/questions` not loaded, is refused — observed live by someone who did not build it.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket and the governing docs.
