## What to build

- The study and the report as [MEETING.md](../../.agents/skills/harness/MEETING.md) states them — the shelf is this ticket's text
  and is not restated here: the inventory with the grain of the project's work; the eight
  readings, counted and quoted; the ledger, a blind run by a separate fresh agent over a sample of
  five groups, its key built before the run; the report in the project's language, its product
  faces addressed to the person, the migration plan cut at the project's grain, the ledger whole in
  the appendix. The shelf is read before an install and again after a migration; the second
  reading is the migration's, [`.0030`](./01-0010.0150.0030-the-migration-as-open-work.md)'s.
- The harness skill gains the occasion in its description and the stops in its flow — the offer
  before the study, and the main recommendations with one question after it — as drafted in the
  parent question's body; how many stops the meeting needs is open as
  [q-0037.0006.0020.0001.0003](../questions/q-0037.0006.0020.0001.0003-does-the-meeting-need-two-stops-and-the-advise-form-at-the-second.md),
  and the first live meeting is its first case, so the skill ships the stops as drafted and the
  question decides on evidence.
- No script: the report is the skill's work until the counts it needs are known (*Occam*). The
  blind run's token count, calls and time come from the host's account of the run.
- The name GoodWolf and the eight readings stand written in the shelf as straw dogs until
  [q-0037.0006.0020.0013](../questions/q-0037.0006.0020.0013-how-do-the-harnesss-name-and-metadata-its-product-readings-among-them-reach-the-skills-from-one-authored-home-rather-than-written-into-each.md)
  gives them an authored home.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] On xuanxue-cabinet, a live meeting by the skill: the install meets the project's `CLAUDE.md`,
      writes nothing, makes the offer naming the file and its place, and after the person's yes
      the report stands at that place in Russian, in the shelf's form, with its ledger; nothing
      else in the tree changed. Graded by the user in that tree.
- [ ] The report read against the shelf by a reader other than its author: every section headed,
      the methodology first, eight readings each counted, five groups in the sample, the product
      faces in the second person, no term of the harness's own, no sentence comparing the tree's
      method with GoodWolf, the plan cut at the project's grain — recorded as the grade.
- [ ] The second stop puts the report's main recommendations and one question to the person, and
      either answer leaves the report in the tree and nothing else written.
- [ ] A session in a tree with a method that is asked to install reads the shelf before any
      study — observed in the live meeting's transcript, not asserted.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its RFC and governing docs.
