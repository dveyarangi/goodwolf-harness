## What to build

A repeatable offline replay over the recorded conversations, with an independent reader's
evidence kept separate from the program's validation and continuation preview. The record names
the cases, observed results and limits; no live session acquires the check.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Independent readings identify the recorded unfinished self-correction and preserve the
      three recorded legitimate stops; disagreements and insufficient evidence are visible.
- [ ] A reading for another case or case version, fabricated evidence, missing or duplicate
      readings cannot yield a continuation or permission to stop.
- [ ] Uncertainty and an already-continued turn remain distinguishable from completion.
- [ ] The run can be repeated from saved inputs and readings, with the reader's accuracy
      reported separately from program tests.
- [ ] Live hooks and the voice rule remain unchanged.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.
