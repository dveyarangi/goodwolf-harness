## What to build

- The `questions` mechanism declared to the shape: its skill `/questions`, read mid-turn by
  attach's pointer and invoked by a person to see or reshape the tree (parent decision 42),
  holding the entry format (24), the window format (33) and the close and prune rules (35, 37);
  its doc, with the principles under *How it works* and the retirement and grading of decision
  41; its rules file, installing attach with the declared line at tier 1 and the wake's read and
  the conclude's write into `/recall` and `/conclude` (32, 38, 40); its script — the window from
  the session's position, the entry and position written from the declared line (39), the
  maintainer's checks (27) — and its tests; and the store's painted door.
- Each host's hook wiring, all three pointing at `questions.py --hook`: Claude Code and Codex
  register the session at its start and receive the window before every message; Cursor
  registers at session start, and its agent runs the window by the rule. The session's tag is
  the host's session id. The rule at tier 1 stays the floor *(parent decision 44, the user,
  2026-10-02)*.
- The store, `docs/questions/`: one small file per question, flat id and slug, the parent line
  the hierarchy's one home, `done/` for wholly closed subtrees by the maintainer's criterion, and
  a sessions file where each running session registers its position (34; 39, amended
  2026-09-29).
- Nothing installed into `/align`: attach and close at the interview's moments are already at
  tier 1 (40).
- `/align` stripped to the interview, everything else installed from its owner or wrapped as a
  straw dog on the ticket that will install it, per the parent's decision 15.
- `concerns.md` folded in: its three entries become entries of the store, the core references
  to it become references to the store, and the file is deleted after the migration.
- Whether an abstraction height can be established is held as an open question in the store,
  not defined, swept or checked here *(the user, 2026-09-29; parent decision 22, withdrawn)*.
- Judge `off`: this slice writes the store by the agent's judgement alone.
