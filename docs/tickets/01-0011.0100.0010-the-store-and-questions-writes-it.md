# The store exists, and `/questions` writes it

- **Status:** Planned (the parent's align precedes)
- **Type:** HITL
- **Depends on:** [01-0011.0100 the-open-questions-are-kept-by-a-mechanism](./01-0011.0100-the-open-questions-are-kept-by-a-mechanism.md)
  (the record shape, the moments and their outputs, the store's home)
- **Outcome:** Every open question of the tree is written in one store with its relations and its
  state, opened and closed through the mechanism's own skill; `/align`, `/recall` and `/conclude`
  work from it through installed rules; `/align` carries the interview and nothing else; the
  three concerns are its first entries; and *height* is defined once, as depth in the question
  structure, with every statement about height in core a pointer to it.

## What to build

- The `questions` mechanism declared to the shape: its skill `/questions` with the store's format
  and the traversal rules — open, attach, close by kind, prune — its doc, its rules file, its
  maintainer script and tests, and the store's painted door.
- The store under `docs/`, with the two relations and the state the parent's align decides.
- Rules installed into `/align`, `/recall` and `/conclude`: a question raised in an interview is
  opened in the store; a decision landed closes it; `/recall` reads what is open and current;
  `/conclude` writes what is left.
- `/align` stripped to the interview, everything else installed from its owner or wrapped as a
  straw dog on the ticket that will install it, per the parent's decision 15.
- `concerns.md` folded in: its three entries become entries of the store, and the seven core
  references to it become references to the store or its view.
- Height defined once in the method glossary as depth in the question structure, and every
  statement about height across the entry file, glossary and skills — 33 on 2026-09-06 — a
  pointer, a correction, or kept with its reason; the count reported and the check rerunnable.
- Judge `off`: this slice writes the store by the agent's judgement alone.

## Acceptance criteria — provisional, firmed at the align

- [ ] `mechanisms.py --check` is clean with the `questions` declaration, and `inject_rules.py
  --check` with its installed blocks.
- [ ] A question opened, attached, closed and pruned through `/questions` reads back from the
  store as written; the maintainer reports a malformed entry and leaves a conforming store
  byte-identical; a full suite run leaves the live store byte-identical.
- [ ] The three concerns are entries of the store and `concerns.md` is a view of it or gone, with
  no core reference dangling.
- [ ] `/align`'s body holds only the interview and installed blocks; the shelves it no longer
  owns are wrapped on their tickets.
- [ ] Height is defined once, every statement about height is dispositioned, the count is
  reported, and a check finds the avoided synonyms in core.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its ticket, RFC, and governing docs.
