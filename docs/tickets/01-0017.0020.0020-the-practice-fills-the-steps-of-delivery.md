## What to build

- **Its doc and its rules file**, owner `core`: `/tdd` and `/improve-comments` claim it.
- **The steps lose their practice**: what in `/plan`, `/implement` and `/verify` is how code is
  written, tested or commented leaves their bodies for its rules file and returns as an installed
  block; a step keeps what holds for any unit of work. Read on 2026-11-16: about 7 of `/plan`'s 31
  rules, about 4 of `/verify`'s 20, all of `/implement` but four paragraphs.
- **The architecture and ADR formats** beside `/align` — how architecture is documented — come under
  it, their straw dogs rebound.
- `/align`, `/maintain` and `/impact` may take its blocks; they are not its
  ([q-qmq6ty decision 2](../questions/q-qmq6ty-how-does-a-project-change-how-it-builds-in-one-edit.md#decisions)).

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The practice is declared, and `mechanisms.py --check` and `inject_rules.py --check` pass.
- [ ] No step of delivery holds practice in its own text; each meets it as an installed block.
- [ ] A rule of practice appears once.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its governing docs.
