## What to build

A repeatable offline replay over the recorded conversations, with an independent reader's
evidence kept separate from the program's validation and continuation preview. The record names
the cases, observed results and limits; no live session acquires the check.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] Independent readings identify the recorded unfinished self-correction and preserve the
      three recorded legitimate stops; disagreements and insufficient evidence are visible. (2026-10-26)
- [x] A reading for another case or case version, fabricated evidence, missing or duplicate
      readings cannot yield a continuation or permission to stop. (2026-10-26)
- [x] Uncertainty and an already-continued turn remain distinguishable from completion. (2026-10-26)
- [x] The run can be repeated from saved inputs and readings, with the reader's accuracy
      reported separately from program tests. (2026-10-26)
- [x] Live hooks and the voice rule remain unchanged. (2026-10-26)
