## What to build

- The window counts three things at the turn's start, for the whole tree: the live tickets under
  `docs/tickets/`, the open questions of the store, the straw dogs of the listing. Each count
  carries how many came and how many went since this session's previous window, matched by name,
  so one ticket closing as another is minted reads `(+1/−1)`, not `(+0/−0)`.
- It prints them as one line the reply copies: `🎫 <n> (+<came>/−<went>) · ❓ … · 🐕 …`. The line
  stands in the window's full form and in its one-line *unchanged* form alike, since a ticket or a
  straw dog moves without an entry moving.
- The names counted are remembered beside the window's fingerprint, outside the tree. The
  session's first window prints the totals alone, never counting everything as come and saying
  nothing of what it cannot compare *(the user, 2026-10-12)*; a compaction forgets the
  conversation's last window, not what the tree held then, so the window after it still compares *(amended at the plan, 2026-10-12)*.
- The straw-dog scan is cached per file by its fingerprint, so a turn rescans only the files that
  moved; drawing the window stays within a tenth of a second of what it takes today.
- The entry file's reply rule places the row: after the `🪶 Commits` table, before the head table.
- The questions mechanism's doc says what the window prints, the counters included.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A window drawn after a ticket is minted, a question opened and a straw dog wrapped shows each
      count with `+1`; after one closes, is decided or is removed, `−1`; one going as another comes
      shows `(+1/−1)` — each tested.
- [x] The one-line *unchanged* window carries the counters, tested with a straw dog added while no
      entry moved.
- [x] The session's first window prints the totals alone, and a window after a compaction still
      compares — both tested.
- [x] A turn's window with no file moved draws in no more than a tenth of a second over today's,
      measured on the same tree, the measure recorded in the question's body.
- [x] A reply under the entry file's rule shows the row after `🪶 Commits` and before the head
      table.
- [x] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its RFC and the governing docs.

## Out of scope

- A count at the turn's end, the turn's own moves included: refused for its cost, in the
  question's decisions.
- Counting by kind of ticket or by subtree of questions: no one has asked.
