## What to build

- **The count in work.** Read from what exists: the owner tickets In progress and, for each, its
  question; against the cap the local file sets. Sessions' positions are not counted — the ring
  mark is deferred (q-0037.0019.0001) — so the count is the tickets' and says so.
- **What the cap held back.** The questions opened since the last boundary and not in work — the
  side questions — named, so a new idea is seen waiting rather than lost.
- **The candidates at the next boundary.** A handful, each with its reason in the person's words:
  the answer of the product document it serves and how much of that answer is built, read from
  the trace links and coverage; what waits on it, its strikes and the straw dogs bound to it; what
  it unlocks, read from `depends on`. Where a reason cannot be read, the candidate says so rather
  than inventing one. No order is taken from a written queue; the candidates stand unordered and
  one is recommended with its reason (q-0037.0019, decision 7).
- **The roots' balance.** For each of the three roots: open questions, questions with a ticket.
  A purpose root with no ticket is named thin.
- **Where it is shown.** In `/recall`'s *What the person hears*, after where the work stands; and
  freeform, when the person asks where to go, the same picture. Under `debug=on` the script's
  output as well. The picture is computed by a script of the store, not by hand, so two sessions
  show the same path.
- **In a tree without rungs, trace links or a product document** the picture shows what exists and
  names what it cannot read; nothing fails.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A `/recall` in this tree shows the count of questions in work against the local cap, read
      from the tickets In progress, and names what the cap held back.
- [x] It shows at most a handful of candidates at the next boundary, each with its reason read from
      the records, and says *unread* where a reason cannot be read.
- [x] It shows the three roots' open and ticketed counts and names a thin root.
- [x] Asked *where should we go* in conversation, the agent shows the same picture.
- [x] The person reads one `/recall` and can say what is next and why without opening a file,
      read by a judge who did not build it.
- [x] In a tree with no product document and no rungs, the picture shows what exists and nothing
      fails, tried on an empty install.
- [x] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.

## Out of scope

The weighing — value, leverage, evidence under the person's weights — and the ordering of
candidates by it: the parent's later slice, after the cap and the cadence are watched. The
time-axis picture, now/next/later: the parent's. Counting sessions in the ring: q-0037.0019.0001,
deferred. Re-ranking the queue: never this ticket's; the order is the person's.
