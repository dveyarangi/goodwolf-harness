## What to build

The installer rehearses the injection — every rules file, the local one and its overrides
included — over a scratch tree of what the copy will leave, before it writes anything, since a preflight refusal changes no files
([Interruption and recovery](../../architecture.md#interruption-and-recovery)) and the
[harness skill](../../../.agents/skills/harness/SKILL.md) promises every refusal writes nothing.
Today the local rules are parsed by the injection step, after the copy. An I/O failure after
writing has begun stays reported, not rolled back; its message, and a later `--install` meeting
the half-installed tree, point to `--update --overwrite`.
