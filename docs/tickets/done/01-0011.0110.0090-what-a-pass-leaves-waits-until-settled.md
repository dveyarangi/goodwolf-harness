## What to build

- **What waits on the person is an open question of the store, marked as asking them**: its id
  kept for good, its decision landing in its body when it closes, no other write touching the
  mark, a merge carrying it to the target *(the user, 2026-10-25)*. Any caller may mark one; the
  rule by which a pass leaves its decisions so is the next slice's.
- **A script, not a model, says whether anything waits.** The store reads the mark.
- **Every wake shows every waiting question until it closes** — no week's horizon, nothing hidden
  behind a count — and shows nothing when none waits.
- **`/recall` puts each asking question to the person as a decision**, through the rule that
  tells it what to take from the wake — save one another running session stands on, which is
  that session's to put.
- **A wait goes when its question closes, never when it is read.**

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] A question marked as asking the person shows in every wake until it closes, and then goes;
      with none asking, the wake shows no section.
- [x] The store holds the mark, and its check fails on a closed question carrying it.
- [x] A fresh session started on a tree with one waiting item puts it to the person as a decision
      in its first report, read by a judge who did not build it. *2026-10-26: on a clone of the
      built commit, one question marked; the fresh session gave it a section of its own, a ✋ and a

      procedural, with no recommendation, and the report's last advice could cover it.*
