## What to build

- A mark keeps a fingerprint per file of the surface it was checked against, and a due level
  says which of them moved, was added or went since. It works on the levels the clock has today.
- A mark written in today's format still reads, as marked without its files named: current while
  nothing moved, due without names once something did, its files named from its next mark.
- The marks' format and `/maintain`'s re-check move with it. A recipient clocks none of the
  mechanisms it received with core and holds no marks, so no line goes to the edge record *(found
  at the plan, 2026-10-11)*.
