## What to build

- **The mark in the row.** A session's row names the unit it holds beside its position, by the
  question the unit answers: a unit is an open question with an owner, and the ticket that holds
  the work is read through the store's `owner`, never from the ticket *(the user, 2026-10-25)*. A
  ticket names no session: sessions are lost and the record stays, and the session mechanism
  cannot stand on the ticket's format, which may be replaced *(the user, 2026-10-24)*.
- **When it is written and cleared.** A session takes a unit when `/plan` or `/implement` begins
  work on it — never at `/verify`, which may be a judge's — and lets it go when the person drops it
  or the unit closes *(the user, 2026-10-25)*. A row that ends — concluded, silent past the
  mechanism's limit, removed — holds nothing, by the row's own lifetime; nothing else clears it.
- **Where it is read.** The wake's list of sessions and the window show each running session's
  unit beside its position. One unit held by two rows is a finding of the session check.
- **The ring mark.** The mark deferred in
  [q-0037.0019.0001](../../questions/q-0037.0019.0001-how-does-the-store-tell-a-session-in-the-ring-holding-a-unit-from-one-in-conversation.md)
  — a session in the ring, holding a unit, told from one in conversation — is this mark. Whether
  the cap of L12 then counts from it is the pathfinder's to decide, not this ticket's.
