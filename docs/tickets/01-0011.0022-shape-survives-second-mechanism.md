## Why this exists, and why it is not "proves the shape generalises"

`01-0011.0010` declares `/mechanism` by its own shape. A shape that has only ever described itself
has not been tested: every choice in it was made while looking at the one thing it describes. The
second application is where a shape either survives or shows which of its rules were about the
first case all along.

This slice is placed here for **falsification, not for confidence**. Three slices —
`01-0011.0025`, `.0030` and `.0040` — build on the shape. Buying the test before they do is worth a
slice; buying it after them means amending a shape three things already depend on.

**It does not exercise injection or the record rule, and must not claim to.** `/maintain` has no
records: `temporary_statements.py` reads `<temporary>` blocks in place, and there is no marks file,
no register, nothing that accumulates. So `/maintain`'s record obligation is `none` **by property**,
not deferred — and the rule `01-0011.0020` installs into it has no subject here. Its first subject
is `/ticket`, one slice later.

## What to build

- `.agents/mechanisms/maintain/` with its doc, declaring `/maintain`. Parts named with their
  standing: `/maintain`'s skill, `temporary_statements.py` and `test_temporary_statements.py` owned;
  `docs_corpus.py` and `tests/harness.py` **relied-on**, naming the owner — which is `/ticket`'s
  mechanism from `01-0011.0025`, and until then a declared gap.
- A moments table covering every occasion at which a person acts on `/maintain`, each row carrying
  its instruction or which kind of absence it is. A `not yet` names a ticket that exists.
- The record obligation stated as `none`, with the reason. An absence that is a property reads as
  an oversight forever unless it says so.
- Its evidence at `docs/mechanisms/maintain.evidence.md`, moved from
  `docs/research/maintenance-findings.md` and **sorted on the way**: what explains why the
  instruction is what it is belongs in the doc; what explains why the doc is what it is stays
  evidence. The file today is an unsorted mixture.
- [maintain/SKILL.md:138](../../.agents/skills/maintain/SKILL.md)'s link into `docs/` moved inside a
  `<project-local>` block, per `AGENTS.md`'s *Core and instance* rule. The same treatment for its
  links to `docs/process.md` and `docs/architecture.md`, which are references to documents only this
  project has.
- A register row in `.agents/README.md`, in the `<project-local>` block `01-0011.0010` establishes.
- Whatever the second application breaks, amended **in `/mechanism`'s body**, with what broke it
  recorded in `docs/mechanisms/mechanism-shape.evidence.md`. A shape bent to fit a declaration is
  the failure this slice exists to catch.

## Decisions this ticket's align owns

- **`/maintain`'s moments, and which absences are honest.** The taxonomy is only worth having if
  the second application is as honest as the first; a moment nobody acts on is not a moment and
  the row does not exist.
- **Whether `/maintain` is always-on.** Nothing can uninstall the thing that maintains, which is
  the argument that made `/mechanism` always-on — but `/maintain` is not the definition of
  installing, so the argument may not carry. It decides whether the doc owes lifecycle pieces.
- **The authority on each of its rules** — who may change it, at what strength.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] `.agents/mechanisms/maintain/` holds a doc naming every part with its standing, and every
  relied-on part names an owner or a declared gap with a ticket.
- [ ] The moments table covers every moment, each row carrying an instruction or a kind of absence;
  every `not yet` names a ticket that exists.
- [ ] The record obligation reads `none` with its reason, distinguishable from unstated.
- [ ] `docs/mechanisms/maintain.evidence.md` exists and `docs/research/maintenance-findings.md` no
  longer holds the mechanism's story; doc material has moved into the doc rather than travelling
  with the evidence.
- [ ] No link from `.agents/skills/maintain/` into `docs/` sits outside a `<project-local>` block.
- [ ] `.agents/README.md` carries a `maintain` row and the shape check resolves it.
- [ ] The check from `01-0011.0010` runs against two mechanisms and reports both declarations true.
- [ ] Every amendment the second application forced is in `/mechanism`'s body, with its cause in
  `mechanism-shape.evidence.md` — or the ticket records that the shape needed none, which is a
  result rather than an omission.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its ticket, RFC, and governing docs.

## Out of scope

Declaring `/ticket` ([0025](./01-0011.0025-archive-duty-reaches-maintain.md)), which is the third
mechanism and the first with records. Building a maintainer script — `/maintain` has no records to
maintain. Moving `docs/process.md`'s *Autonomy and repair*, *Verification* or *Mechanisms and
skills*: they belong to mechanisms nobody has declared, and `01-0016` owns where repair-and-report
lands. Declaring any mechanism beyond the three the spec scopes — [01-0017](./01-0017-io-graph-coherent.md)
applies the shape to the rest.
