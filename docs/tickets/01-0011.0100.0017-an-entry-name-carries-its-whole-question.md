## What to build

- The writer's slug holds every word of the question, cut at a word boundary only past 160
  characters.
- The skill tells the agent to phrase a question short, since its name is made from its words.
- The live entries renamed with the mover, which repairs every link to them.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A question of up to 160 characters of words is named in full, and a longer one is cut at a
  word boundary; tested.
- [ ] Every live entry's slug holds its whole question, every link to an entry resolves, and
  `questions.py --check` and `mechanisms.py --check` are clean.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its ticket, RFC, and governing docs.

## Out of scope

What a slug is read for: nothing but a person reading a listing (decision 55).
