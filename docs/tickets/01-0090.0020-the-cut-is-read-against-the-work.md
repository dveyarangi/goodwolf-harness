## What to build

*2026-11-24: the person's words of 2026-11-21 and 2026-11-22 —
[the plan](../rfc/01-0090.0020-the-cut-is-read-against-the-work.md#decided-by-the-person),
"Decided by the person" — overturn what is said below, and in the criteria, of a role's own
mechanism, brief or skill and of a zone written there: a role is the mechanisms a session holds,
and who holds which is one table in the local rules, a session by its name and what it holds.
The cut the first run is read against, here and in the second criterion, is decision 1 of
[q-mz6gd4](../questions/q-mz6gd4-where-does-a-session-of-this-tree-read-each-roles-zone-and-brief-and-which-session-holds-it.md#decisions)
as its decisions 4 and 6 amend it. The text and the criteria are worked anew at the next `/plan`
pass.*

A collision between standing sessions is mended where it happened today, so its cause — two zones
that overlap — stays and returns; and nothing says when a cut has gone stale. The parent
question's accepted
[Where it stands](../questions/q-ja5mp4-does-the-method-have-one-session-direct-the-others-an-orchestrator-and-what-does-it-owe-the-sessions-it.md#where-it-stands)
— *The skill*, *Its moments* — answers with a part of
[the session mechanism](../../.agents/mechanisms/session/session.md), kept apart so that it can
leave as a mechanism at the cost of a move: the skill `/pack` and a section of the mechanism's
doc with the part's moments.

The cut is derived by `/pack`'s own rules, never written by hand, and `/pack` answers for the
cut's record *(the user, 2026-11-20 —
[q-mz6gd4](../questions/q-mz6gd4-where-does-a-session-of-this-tree-read-each-roles-zone-and-brief-and-which-session-holds-it.md#decisions),
decisions 2 and 3)*:

- The skill holds the rules a cut is derived by — the whole's *The cut* and *One session to a
  zone*.
- The part's declaration says where a cut's record stands, in what form, who reads it and at
  which moment. The whole gives the home — a role's own mechanism for its zone and brief, a table
  in the local rules for who holds which; the declaration states it, with its readers.

- *Sessions step on each other.* `/pack` says which zones overlapped, read from what the tree
  already keeps — the rows, the session records, git history, the sessions' own words — and from
  a read by someone outside. One collision is mended where it happened; a collision repeated in
  one zone brings a proposal: the code or the records separated, paths shifted from one role to
  another, or a new role named for what it answers for.
- *A zone outgrows its session.* It reads the flow of the zone's tasks and its load and proposes
  a split; where the work does not split, the zone's session works through subagents.
- *The cut is read against the work.* On the person's ask it reads which sessions the person
  actually runs against the roles, and proposes a role opened, closed, merged, or given to a
  session that holds another.
- *A session takes a role.* It writes its name on its row, reads the role's skill, and reads the
  tree by the role's zone.

`/pack` recommends and the person decides: it starts and ends no session, hands out no work and
writes nothing in another role's zone, because the person stays the one judge between zones.
Keeping the cut is itself a role, held in this tree by the session that holds the store: that
session plans this slice, writes the part's text and puts it to the person.

The first run is this tree's: a fresh session derives the tree's cut by the skill's rules,
untold, and it is read against the cut the person accepted on 2026-11-20
([q-mz6gd4](../questions/q-mz6gd4-where-does-a-session-of-this-tree-read-each-roles-zone-and-brief-and-which-session-holds-it.md#decisions),
decision 1). Writing that cut where the declaration says is the next slice's. How many collisions
in one zone count as repeated is set after that read
([q-jz3mlm](../questions/q-jz3mlm-how-does-a-project-cut-its-work-into-standing-sessions-and-how-does-the-cut-change-as-the-project-grows.md)).

Every instruction's text is shown to the person before it lands. Each step is written in
[the journal](../research/standing-sessions-journal.md) with what a fresh tree would need to do
the same.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fresh session given a collision — two sessions editing the same two mechanism docs — and
      running `/pack` names the overlapping zones and a way to separate them, untold; judged by
      someone who did not build it.
- [ ] Run over this tree by a fresh session, untold, `/pack` derives the tree's cut by its own
      rules and names the sessions the person actually runs; the cut is read against the one the
      person accepted on 2026-11-20 and every difference is put to the person; it proposes a new
      role only where a collision repeated or a zone outgrew its session; judged by someone who
      did not build it.
- [ ] A fresh reader given the part's declaration alone says where a role's zone is written,
      where who holds which is written, who reads each and at which moment.
- [ ] The session mechanism's declaration holds the part, and `mechanisms.py --check` passes.
- [ ] The skill's text and each rule's were shown to the person before they landed.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket and the governing docs.
