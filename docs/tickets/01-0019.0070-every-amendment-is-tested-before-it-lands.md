## What to build

The test becomes a step of amending: the self-amendment mechanism is declared, owning the
register, the cases and the runs, which decides
[q-0037.0012.0001](../questions/done/q-0037.0012.0001-is-harness-self-amendment-its-own-mechanism.md);
`/skill-up` and `/mechanism` receive from it the rule that an amendment passes its test before it
lands. The case folder becomes a door the mechanism declares rather than one the leak check holds
by hand.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The self-amendment mechanism is declared, and its declaration names the cases and the runs as its records.
- [ ] `/skill-up` and `/mechanism` carry the installed rule, and the next amendment made through either is tested before it lands, seen by someone who did not make it.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.

## Out of scope

Whether a fresh author writes better amendments, q-0037.0012.0009.0003.
