## What to build

The shape is the question's *Where it stands*; this slice lands the dial itself, the same for
every ladder and particular to no method.

- The entry file states the ladder and the dial's meaning in place of the autonomy table and the
  switch names in its *Human checkpoints* line, with an entry-contract bump. A project's dials live
  in its local file, and this tree runs on them.
- Development's ladder lands as the practice mechanism's — a straw dog bound to
  q-0037.0010.0002.0005 while that mechanism is undeclared — and nothing else of development's
  enters core.
- Self-improvement's ladder lands the same way, as self-amendment's, bound to q-0037.0012.0001.
- Every skill that reads a switch reads the dial of the ladder it acts on, naming a rung by what it
  decides.
- A question carries its highest rung on each ladder it touches: the agent estimates it at opening
  and raises it, only the person lowers it, and the question stops for the person while it is at or
  above the dial. The store holds the part's form; the rungs' names and order are the ladders'.
- The install shows each ladder as a list of acts with everyday examples, marked 🤖 ✋ 💬, proposes
  development at a feature and self-improvement at a rule, and the person names the line for ✋. An
  unset dial is at the lowest rung.
- The entry file says the agent never writes its dial; the host's refusal of such an edit is
  01-0010.0125's, and its clause waits on it as a straw dog.
- Product rows A13 and A14 are rewritten through `/product`.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] No skill, rule or entry-file line names a removed switch, and the checks hold it.
- [ ] Given decisions from this tree's history with the rungs the person gave them, an agent
      without this session's context places them on the same rungs.
- [ ] An install into an empty folder shows each ladder as acts with examples and a proposed line,
      and a person new to the harness can say where the agent will stop.
- [ ] A question opened at a rung at or above its dial stops for the person before its decision is
      taken, and the store refuses a rung part out of its form.
- [ ] A session at a set dial asks, acts and proposes as the rungs say, read by the person.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.
