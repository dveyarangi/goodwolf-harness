## What to build

- A mechanism declares the root it owns, in its own words (q-0037.0018, decision 9); the seed writes
  the declared ones and takes the rest from the shelf, whose sentence of ownership is a straw dog
  bound to q-0037.0018 until then.
- The harness mechanism's doc names who words the roots in its *Relies on* table; that row moves.
- Nothing reseeds, rewords or removes a root in a store that holds an entry, as now.
