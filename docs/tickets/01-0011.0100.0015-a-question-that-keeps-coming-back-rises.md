# A question that keeps coming back rises

- **Status:** Planned
- **Type:** AFK
- **Depends on:** [01-0011.0100.0010 the-store-and-questions-writes-it](./01-0011.0100.0010-the-store-and-questions-writes-it.md)

  and ranks it first where a session starts, and nothing an ordinary reach does rewrites the store.

## What to build

- An entry's part `struck <n>, last <time>`: stamped when a question is opened, and moved by the
  writer when a declared `at` lands on a held open question at least X hours after its last stamp;
  X is twelve unless the project sets it. Written at those two moments only. An entry written
  before the part existed is stamped at its first reach, never struck by it.
- The check holds the part's form; the skill declares it in the entry's record.
- Each line of the window carries `struck <n>` when it is above zero; the wake's read lists the
  most-struck open questions first.
- A declared-line clause that removes a dependency, so q-0008's stale dependency on q-0015 is
  dropped and q-0008 closed against where decisions 53 and 54 land.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A reach of a held open question twelve hours or more after its last stamp strikes it once,
  and a reach sooner does not; an unstamped entry is stamped at its first reach and not struck;
  tested with the time injected.
- [ ] Only opening and striking write an entry: a run of ordinary reaches leaves the store
  byte-identical and the window answers *unchanged*.
- [ ] The window shows the count on struck lines, and the wake's read ranks the most-struck open
  questions first.
- [ ] A dependency can be removed by a declared line; q-0008 is closed and `questions.py --check`
  is clean.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its ticket, RFC, and governing docs.

## Out of scope

Keeping a resumed conversation one session (q-0015), which the time gap does without. Ordering the
window by strikes.
