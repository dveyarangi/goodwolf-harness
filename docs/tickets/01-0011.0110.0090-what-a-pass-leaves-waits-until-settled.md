## What to build

- **A pass leaves what waits on the person as an open question of the store, marked as
  waiting**: its id kept for good, its decision landing in its body when it closes, no re-check
  touching it *(the user, 2026-10-25)*.
- **A script, not a model, says whether anything waits.** The store reads the mark.
- **Every wake shows every waiting question until it closes** — no week's horizon, nothing hidden
  behind a count — and shows nothing when none waits.
- **`/recall` puts each waiting question to the person as a decision**, through the rule that
  tells it what to take from the wake.
- **A wait goes when its question closes, never when it is read.**

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A wait a pass leaves shows in every next wake until its question closes, and then goes;
      with nothing waiting, the wake shows no section.
- [ ] The store holds what waits, and its check refuses a wait on a closed question.
- [ ] A fresh session started on a tree with one waiting item puts it to the person as a decision
      in its first report, read by a judge who did not build it.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.
