## What to build

- The two lines the shape holds in the entry file by anchor — *method is mechanism work*, *an
  installed block is not yours* — become rules of `mechanism-shape.rules.md` and are installed in
  the entry file, as R7 is; their text is shown before it lands. A meta-rule is an instruction on
  making instructions and the shape's; a meta-principle stays the entry file's own (the method
  glossary's *Meta-rule*, 2026-10-11).
- The format's *a meta-rule is never injected* goes, and with it the anchored part as the form
  for a line in the entry file.
- Every parts table names in another's file only what the mechanism installs there: the three
  hook-wiring rows point at their shelves, the two entry-file rows at the installed rules, and
  edge's two headings in `/align` and `/verify` move to *Relies on*, the target skill's.
