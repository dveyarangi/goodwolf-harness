## What to build

- **The mark in the row.** A session's row names the unit it holds, a ticket In progress, beside
  its position. A ticket names no session: sessions are lost and the record stays, and the session
  mechanism cannot stand on the ticket's format, which may be replaced *(the user, 2026-10-24)*.
- **When it is written and cleared.** A session takes a unit when it begins the ring on it and lets
  it go when the person drops it or the unit closes. A row that ends — concluded, silent past the
  mechanism's limit, removed — holds nothing, by the row's own lifetime; nothing else clears it.
- **Where it is read.** The wake's list of sessions and the window show each running session's
  unit beside its position. One unit held by two rows is a finding of the session check.
- **The ring mark.** The mark deferred in
  [q-0037.0019.0001](../questions/q-0037.0019.0001-how-does-the-store-tell-a-session-in-the-ring-holding-a-unit-from-one-in-conversation.md)
  — a session in the ring, holding a unit, told from one in conversation — is this mark. Whether
  the cap of L12 then counts from it is the pathfinder's to decide, not this ticket's.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A session that takes a ticket In progress shows it as its unit in the next session's wake and
      in its own window; one that drops it or concludes shows none.
- [ ] A row ended by silence or removed holds nothing, and no ticket file changes when a session
      takes or drops a unit.
- [ ] Two rows naming one unit are reported by the session check.
- [ ] Tried live with two running sessions, one holding a ticket, read by a judge who did not build
      it.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.
