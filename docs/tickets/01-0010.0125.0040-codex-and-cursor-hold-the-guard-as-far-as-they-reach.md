## What to build

Codex's row in `HOSTS` declares its loads — a typed `$name` seen in the prompt hook, a skill the
model picks read through a tool call the pre-tool hook sees — and its lifetime: the turn, by its
`turn_id`, since its own catalog tells the model not to carry a skill further, and a compaction
drops every skill. Cursor's row declares what its hooks show; where no hook sees a load, its edits
are not refused, and the commit stands in. The [hosts edge](../edge/hosts.md) carries each host's
**Edit refusal**, **Skill load** and **Skill lifetime** as observed.
