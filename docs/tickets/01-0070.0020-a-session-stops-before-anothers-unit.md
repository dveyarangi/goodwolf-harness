## What to build

- **The stop.** A turn that would finish, close, commit, continue or duplicate a unit another
  running session's row names settles nothing on it: the reply says whose the unit is and where the
  two touch, brings two ways to act with a recommendation, and nothing is done on the unit until
  the person names one. The weight is in the refusal to proceed by default, not in prose *(the
  user, 2026-10-24)*. *Serious* is not judged: a held unit stops, an unheld one does not. A shared
  position with no work on the unit stays the 🪢 row. The stop holds whatever `debug` says, since a
  tree with debug off must keep it.
- **The two ways.** This session takes the unit over by the person's word, acted on without a
  second ask once said, and the holder's row keeps the mark of who took it, so a holder still alive
  learns its unit is gone — from its window, and from a message where the host offers one; the
  session mechanism manages every session from any one of them *(the user, 2026-10-25)*. Or a
  sub-task is cut through `/ticket` and taken here, the rest left with the holder.
- **The judge is a subagent.** Where a criterion needs someone who did not build the work, the
  holder starts a subagent, gives it the criterion and where its evidence stands, never its own
  conclusion, and closes on the verdict; a later session judges only what no subagent can see, and
  closes on its own verdict *(the user, 2026-10-25)*. TICKET-FORMAT's judge sentence says so.
- **The bias.** The entry file's general rules name the agent's default to do as much as it can
  itself, and that work another session holds is not done without the person's word *(the user,
  2026-10-23)*, worded through `/skill-up`.
- **The session mechanism's doc** says sessions cannot call one another; a host that can message
  makes that untrue, and the doc says what the method relies on and what is the host's.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Asked to close, finish or commit a unit another running session holds, a session stops with
      the two ways and does nothing on the unit — tried live, read by a subagent judge from both
      sessions' rows and windows.
- [ ] Told to take a held unit over, a session takes it without asking again, and the holder's
      window shows who took it; the holder's next hold, release or end clears the mark.
- [ ] A criterion needing a judge is judged by a subagent the holder starts, briefed with the
      criterion and its evidence and not the holder's conclusion, and the holder closes on it.
- [ ] The stop stands with `debug` off.
- [ ] The entry file's general rules carry the bias principle.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.
