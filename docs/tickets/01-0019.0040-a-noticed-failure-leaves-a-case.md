## What to build

The chain's first two moments: where a failure is noticed, the situation is captured — the
message, what was loaded, what of the session the case needs — and the register's entry carries the
case it makes, the fix case for the amendment it proposes. The entry file's *Self-improvement* gains
this; [01-0019.0010](./01-0019.0010-self-improvement-follows-its-dial.md) rewrites the same section for
the dial, and one lands after the other.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The next rule failure entered in the register carries a case in `docs/`, and that case fails on the standing text when run.
- [ ] The *Self-improvement* section says the case is written where the failure is noticed.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.

## Out of scope

Cases drawn from transcripts afterwards, a reserve source; near-misses and bias cases, written with the rule, in 01-0019.0050.
