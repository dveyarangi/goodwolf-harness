## What to build

The chain's first two moments: where a failure is noticed, the situation is captured — the
message, what was loaded, what of the session the case needs — and the register's entry carries the
case it makes, a case the amendment it proposes is tested on. The case's shape is the one
[q-j5s7gp](../questions/q-j5s7gp-how-is-a-recorded-rule-failure-replayed-as-a-test-against-the-amended-rule.md#where-it-stands)
states: the instruction versions belong to the experiment, not the case, and the replay input keeps
the host's messages and tool results verbatim, without an authored summary. The entry file's
*Self-improvement* gains this; [01-0019.0010](./01-0019.0010-self-improvement-follows-its-dial.md)
rewrites the same section for the dial, and one lands after the other.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The next occurrence recorded in the register, a new entry or a strike of an existing one, carries a case in `docs/` whose checks reject the recorded response and accept an attributed control, independently reviewed.
- [ ] The *Self-improvement* section says the case is written where the failure is noticed.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket, its RFC and the governing docs.

## Out of scope

Cases drawn from transcripts afterwards, which [01-0019.0090](./done/01-0019.0090-historical-failures-carry-source-grounded-cases.md) extracted; near-misses and bias cases, written with the rule, in 01-0019.0050.
