## What to build

The chain's first two moments: where a failure is noticed, the situation is captured — the
message, what was loaded, what of the session the case needs — and the register's entry carries the
case it makes, the fix case for the amendment it proposes. The entry file's *Self-improvement* gains
this; [01-0019.0010](./01-0019.0010-self-improvement-follows-its-dial.md) rewrites the same section for
the dial, and one lands after the other.

The capture stage is implemented in entry contract v131: the mechanism skill owns the act,
`capture.py` preserves the observation and validates live records, and the register links a
[genuinely noticed source-link failure](../cases/proposal-source-link-verified/failure.json).
A fresh independent reader also followed the instruction in an explicitly synthetic scenario;
its [evidence and verification](../case-runs/capture-2026-10-26/verification.json) are preserved.
The next criterion is not established: no fresh standing-condition run reproduced the genuine
source-link failure: the [bounded standing probe](../case-runs/capture-2026-10-26/standing-record.json)
returned correct source links in all three continuations. The complete ticket and RFC remain live until that criterion and whole-ticket
verification pass; the synthetic trial and original failed response do not substitute for it.
