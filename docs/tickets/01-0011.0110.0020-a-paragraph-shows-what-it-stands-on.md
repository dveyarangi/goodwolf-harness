- **Answers:** [q-0030](../questions/q-0030-how-does-a-reply-show-the-principles-each-of-its-claims-stands-on-and-where-are-the-principles-named-for-it.md)
- **Outcome:** Under `debug=on`, every paragraph of a reply that claims or recommends opens with
  the principle it reasons by and the classification it sorts by, the reply ends with the
  footings its claims rest on, and a paragraph whose reasoning follows a principle the list lacks
  is boxed with that principle worded, so a missing principle is caught as it is used.

## What to build

- One rule at tier 1, written by hand in the entry file and wrapped as a straw dog bound to the
  owning question, since no mechanism owns principles yet: a paragraph that claims or recommends
  ends, as its last sentence in parentheses, with `💡 *<name>*` the one principle it is
  governed by and `🧩 *<name>: <kind>*` the classification it sorts by with the kind it sorts
  into, in that order, each only when earned; rarely, where the reasoning follows a principle the
  list lacks, `⬜ *<that principle, worded>*` stands in the lamp's place, and where no principle
  is at work there is no lamp and no box; what the claims rest on — a decision, evidence or the
  user's word, by its record or by who and when — goes to one blockquote at the reply's end, a
  line `⚓ *<footing>* — <the claim>` for each; a paragraph that links, narrates or restates
  carries no mark. *(The user, 2026-10-08, over the replies after the first under v34, where the
  marks opening a paragraph interfered with the text: the lamp and the piece close the paragraph
  in parentheses, the anchors gather in one blockquote at the end, the piece shows the kind, not
  only the classification, and the box is only for a missing principle; v35.)*
- Whatever waits on the user's decision goes to a one-cell table above the anchors, headed
  `| ⚖️ Decisions |`, a row `| <the decision, and its options> |` for each, under any debug
  setting *(the user, 2026-10-08; the glyph in the header alone, v36)*.
- Core states the marks with its own glyphs; this tree's local rule overrides them as it does
  the turn's rows.
- The entry contract moves.
- No code; no script checks a mark.
