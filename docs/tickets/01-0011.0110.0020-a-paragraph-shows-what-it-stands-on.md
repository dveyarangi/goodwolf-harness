## What to build

- One rule at tier 1, written by hand in the entry file and wrapped as a straw dog bound to the
  owning question, since no mechanism owns principles yet: a paragraph that claims or recommends
  opens with a blockquote of `💡 *<name>*` the one principle it is governed by and
  `🧩 *<name>: <kind>*` the classification it sorts by with the kind it sorts into, in that order, each only when earned; where no principle fits,
  `⬜ no principle fits` stands in the lamp's place; what the claims rest on — a decision,
  evidence or the user's word, by its record or by who and when — goes to one list at the
  reply's end, an item `⚓ *<footing>* — <the claim>` for each; a paragraph that links, narrates
  or restates carries no mark. *(The blockquote and the end list: the user, 2026-10-08, on the
  first reply under v34, where the marks inline interfered with the text; the piece shows the
  kind, not only the classification, the user the same day; v35.)*
- Core states the marks with its own glyphs; this tree's local rule overrides them as it does
  the turn's rows.
- The entry contract moves.
- No code; no script checks a mark.
