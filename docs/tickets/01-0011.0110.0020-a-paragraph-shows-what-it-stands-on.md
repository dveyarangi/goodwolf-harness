# A paragraph shows what it stands on

- **Status:** Planned (`.0010` supplies the names)
- **Type:** HITL
- **Depends on:** [01-0011.0110.0010 the-kinds-have-names](./01-0011.0110.0010-the-kinds-have-names.md)
  (the names the marks cite)
- **Answers:** [q-0030](../questions/q-0030-how-does-a-reply-show-the-principles-each-of-its-claims-stands-on-and-where-are-the-principles-named-for-it.md)
- **Outcome:** Under `debug=on`, every paragraph of a reply that claims or recommends opens with
  the principle it reasons by, the classification it sorts by and the footing it rests on, and a
  paragraph no principle fits is boxed, so a claim that stands on nothing is seen before it is
  read.

## Parent

[Every written thing has a source of truth](./01-0011.0110-every-written-thing-has-a-source-of-truth.md),
second of five slices: the marks on a reply. `/impact`: the parent's.

## What to build

- One rule at tier 1, written by hand in the entry file and wrapped as a straw dog bound to the
  owning question, since no mechanism owns principles yet: a paragraph that claims or recommends
  opens with `💡 *<name>*` the one principle it is governed by, `🧩 *<name>*` the
  classification it sorts by, `⚓ *<footing>*` what it rests on — a decision, evidence or the
  user's word, by its record or by who and when — in that order, each only when earned; where no
  principle fits, a blockquote `> ⬜ no principle fits — <what it rests on>` stands in the lamp's
  place; a paragraph that links, narrates or restates carries no mark.
- Core states the marks with its own glyphs; this tree's local rule overrides them as it does
  the turn's rows.
- The entry contract moves.
- No code; no script checks a mark.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The rule is installed at tier 1, bound to its question, and a fresh session's first
      claiming reply carries the marks without being asked.
- [ ] Over a week of replies the user grades the marks honest: a principle named is the one the
      paragraph reasons by, a box appears where none fits, and no narrating paragraph is marked.
- [ ] At least one box in that week named a principle the list lacked, or the user recorded
      that none did.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against the spec and the governing docs.
