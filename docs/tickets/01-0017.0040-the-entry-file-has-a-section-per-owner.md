## What to build

- **One section per owner**, by [decision 2](../questions/q-0037.0006.0024.0002-does-every-rule-of-the-entry-file-belong-to-a-mechanism-so-that-an-install-adds-the.md#decisions): the meta-principles, the mechanism shape, the session, the
  store, the ticket mechanism, maintain, debugging, the development method, autonomy, and
  *Project-local* last, as the local block must be. Text whose owner is not declared stays authored in
  its future section, wrapped as a straw dog bound to the question that declares it.
- **Existing blocks moved** by retract and reinstall, in one commit with their rules files' anchors.
- **References repaired**: *installed below*, *see the definition below*, and the links into the
  entry file's sections — `#autonomy`, `#straw-dogs` and the rest; the entry contract bumped.
- The section order is shown to the person before it lands.
- **A recipient's update meets the moved anchors**: since 01-0017.0030 a matching block outside its
  section refuses an install, and the harness's rehearsal says to fix `local.rules.md` where a
  retraction is the remedy; the update path and its words are settled here.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Every installed block stands in its owner's section and the check holds it.
- [ ] No link into the entry file's sections leads nowhere, and no *below* or *above* points at text
      that moved.
- [ ] A fresh agent reading the entry file alone says what each section owns.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its governing docs.
