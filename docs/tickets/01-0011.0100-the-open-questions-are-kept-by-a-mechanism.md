  and `.0017` done 2026-10-03; `.0020` next)

  state, the turn's current question is known and every shift from it is judged and recorded, a

  store instead of carrying it in session text, so a question left behind is found again and a

## Why this exists

A discussion, or a pass, opens a question, splits it, dives into one branch and moves on; what
was left open above is carried in nobody's record and never amended. The day this was raised
showed it four times in one align, and the tree's own register holds the same shape at
[rule failures 11 and 14](../rule-failures.md). The harness already keeps open questions — in
`concerns.md`, in tickets' *Open issues*, in eighty-two straw dogs — three stores with three
formats and no state saying which question the work stands at. `/align`'s own text describes the
walk — *walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions
one-by-one* — with nothing written behind it. Life records the same failure from the other side:
of its first seven specimens of conclusions reached badly, none was caught by the agent, and it
names the missing part outright — no dedicated judge, no reader
([trace](../research/life-reasoning-failures-2026-09-28.md)).

## What to build

*(The parts, proposed by the user on 2026-09-28 and filled at the align; a declaration will name
them. The slices below cut them into deliverable passes.)*

- **The store** — every open question written, one record kind, its format in the mechanism's
  skill; identity apart from structure, an id being placement and never hierarchy.
- **Two relations, kept distinct** — *part of*, a decomposition without an AND/OR mark (decision
  24), and *depends on*, cannot be asked until; the second makes the structure a DAG, and project
  practice never merges the two.
- **The state** — the current question and its path, set by a judge from the request, read at
  every turn; the pacer's resume state is this.
- **The unit** — an *issue*, resolved by a decision. A ticket stays a *goal*, satisfied by evidence,
  and links to the issues it owns.
- **Closure by recorded kind** — decided; pruned as wrong; merged; deferred with a default for
  acting meanwhile; moot; superseded. Core records the kind; the method says which count.
- **Four judgements as moments with defined outputs** — *attach* (which open question a message
  addresses, opens, or neither), *branch* (a question acquiring children, including a shape hiding
  them), *close*, *prune*. Judge `jev` | `agent` | `off`, in the local file; the unasked hook is
  [01-0010.0120](./01-0010.0120-host-delivery-surfaces.md)'s.
- **Link maintenance** — a child goes *suspect* when its parent's answer changes, until cleared:
  [01-0011.0060](./done/01-0011.0060-mechanism-rechecked-when-governing-moves.md)'s marks applied
  to links.
- **The method's instruments, injected** from each method mechanism's rules file: how to branch,
  how to dive, how to close, what counts as evidence, what to discard, what counts as complexity.
  The ticketing method's slicing is the branch instrument for questions that are goals; the
  routing answer [01-0010.0080](./01-0010.0080-impact-work-shape.md) owes is another.
- **The HITL policy, read, not owned** — which questions stop for a human comes from the autonomy
  switches and the ticket's type; the threshold is [01-0020](./01-0020-pacer.md)'s. `/align` is
  the instrument for that moment, not this mechanism's instruction file.
- **What folds in** — `concerns.md`, tickets' *Open issues*, the straw dogs, the written height on
  a descent; each already does one part right and becomes an entry or a view.
- **Records small, formalised incrementally, with derived views** — capture cost is the one
  well-evidenced failure of the idea, and ceremony the adoption panel's first blocker.

24. **The entry, eight parts** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*: id, stable and placement-only; the
    question, one line as asked; part of, the parent if any, roots allowed; depends on, optional;
    state, `open` or `closed:<kind>` over the six kinds, plus a `suspect` flag; owner, the record
    holding the deliberation, optional at birth since a chat may have no ticket yet; answer, on
    closure a link to the deciding artifact with provenance, and for *deferred* the condition and
    the default to act on meanwhile; lean, one optional line. Arguments and positions stay in the
    owner record; level is depth, never written. The store keeps one fact outside every entry:
    the current question. *Amended at `.0020`'s align (the user, 2026-10-03):* the entry holds its
    own argument in a body after its parts, and the owner is the record of the work that answers
    it — [`.0020` decision 3](./01-0011.0100.0020-open-issues-are-entries-of-the-store.md#decisions-landed-at-this-tickets-align).
    - **No AND/OR mark on *part of***: the self-application produced only ANDs; what looked like OR
      was competing answers to one question, and competition is a relation between answers.
    - **The lean is in the entry**, revising decision 11's interim home: written only in the
      owner's *Open issues* it is invisible from the tree, and four of this align's own questions
      carried one.
    - **Entries can be born closed** (an answer arriving before its question was asked) **and born
      suspect** (closed while a dependency was open); a reopening is a new closure of kind
      *superseded* pointing at the old answer, not a history on the entry.
25. **The attach moment's outputs** *(2026-09-28)*: continues the current question; attaches to a
    named open question; opens a new one under a named parent; opens a new one *between* two
    existing ones, re-parenting the lower; answers a named question, closing it by a kind, current
    or not; nothing. *Between* is explicit because without it the tree lies and nothing later
    corrects it. The output is declared in one line at the head of the reply, which is decision
    7's declared state made visible for correction in the same turn. **Visible while young, in
    the background at the end** *(the user, 2026-09-29, at `.0010`'s plan)*: the mechanism's end
    form runs in the background; in its starting stages the line is shown in the reply, for
    debugging. Shown when the project sets `debug=on`, a setting of its own beside the autonomy
    switches — not a switch, since it decides nothing about proceeding — and hidden when it is
    unset or `off`; the user ends the visible stage by setting it, not a ticket. **What is shown**
    *(the user, 2026-10-03)*: the question the message landed on, by its id and its question as
    the window writes it, and each move from one question to another where it happens — never
    the call, whose command line and session tag tell a person nothing; `at` prints that line.
    **How it is shown** *(the user, 2026-10-03)*: a one-cell table, `| ↳ **q-N** · <question> |`
    over `|---|` — boxed, the id bold, no lead word. Refused: a box drawn in characters, padded by
    hand and unwrapped; a `Headline:` lead, heavy. **The table holds the turn's path** *(the user,
    2026-10-03, at `.0020`'s align)*: a row per question the turn stood on, in order — `↳` on the
    one it ends on, `✓` and `(<kind>: <answer>)` on one closed in it, the question alone on one
    it left open. Refused: a move as a loose line beside the box, which no rule gave a form; a
    row per event with an arrow for its direction.
26. **The judge reads a window, never the tree** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*: the path from root to
    the current question; the frontier, the open children of every question on the path; and every
    open question's title. Each line carries id, title, state with kind, flags and lean — without
    them a reopening cannot be told from an attachment. Closed entries appear only on or under the
    path: *decided* there so a re-ask is treated as a reopening, *deferred* there so the judge can
    tell the condition being met from drift, and *suspect* anywhere under it. Closed questions
    grow without bound and stay out; the open ones were said to be bounded by attention, forty to
    sixty, and measured the same day at about a hundred — 35 open-issue bullets, 60 distinct
    straw-dog conditions, 3 concerns — so the bound is the current root's subtree, not the store
    (decision 33's scope). Paraphrase is handled
    because the reader is a model.
27. **The whole tree is read by a script only** *(2026-09-28)*: the store's maintainer reports
    malformed entries, orphans, closed children whose parent's answer changed, closed entries with
    an open dependency, and title pairs sharing enough words to be twins, for a human to merge.
28. **No glossary in the path** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*. Refuted on the way: candidate search by

30. **The store is core's own record, on a clean slate** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*. Questions are
    core's internal substrate for steering, aligning, exploring and wayfinding; tickets are the
    developer-facing way a method records goals and features, and the ticket method can be swapped
    by local practice, so the substrate cannot live inside it. An open ticket spans several
    questions or is one, depending on the ticket. What the two share is probably the management
    structure. Today's tickets mix the two concepts and most will migrate to questions, directly or
    indirectly; a ticket's deliberation is the exploration of subquestions — which is exactly why the
    mechanism starts clean rather than from the tickets' sections. Refuted: the tickets as host of
    the entries, recommended on the one-home rule after the self-application found the closed half
    already written under *Decisions landed*; and one file for all questions, since the list grows
    large.
31. **The agent follows rules; the judge applies principles; a rule is what puts the judge in the
    loop** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*. A principle is a reason with no moment and no outcome,
    applied by judgement, so it becomes checkable only through a judge: the judge's criterion is a
    principle, its invocation a rule — moment, a message arrives; instruction, judge it; outcome, a
    typed value. A principle that did not fire is a missing rule, which is a missing judge moment.
    Both terms now defined in the method glossary; the rules-file grammar's gap — it admits a
    principle as a rule — is opened on [the mechanism-shape spec](../spec/01-0011-mechanism-shape.md#further-notes).
    Specimen from this align: *depth is the instrument against breadth* is a principle, for the
    questions doc; its rule is *when a question's open children exceed what the frontier can show,
    find the question they jointly serve and insert it above them*, after which the frontier fits
    the window.
32. **Rule: attach, on a newcomer that answers nothing on the path or frontier** *(the user,
    2026-09-28)*. Moment: a message arrives that does not continue the current question and
    answers nothing on the path or its frontier. Instruction: name what the current question cannot
    be answered without deciding, or what the method's plan expects here; open that as a question;
    if answering it narrows the current question *and* answering the current question contributes
    to it, insert it above the current one; if only the first holds, open it beside and add
    *depends on* from the current one. Outcome: the store holds the new question with its
    placement, the current position is written — on every attach output, *continue* included —
    before the reply is drafted, and the reply's first line declares it *(the position clause
    added 2026-09-29, when the user asked whether its writing had been written down: it had not)*.
    Behind it, cited never installed: depth is
    the instrument against breadth, and a skipped question is found by impasse or by the plan,
    never by walking presuppositions upward — a sibling with a dependency flattens the tree, and
    breadth is the pressure both passes measured. Re-parenting is cheap under placement-only ids
    and untyped levels. **Home:** the questions mechanism's rules file,
    `.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.rules.md`, targeted at the entry file, since the moment
    is every turn and every turn reads tier 1; created at `.0010`. Until the store exists the
    rule's outcome cannot, so it is not hand-installed under R5 — this decision is its interim
    record. When the judge is not `off`, the instruction is the judge's schema, run from
    [`.0120`](./01-0010.0120-host-delivery-surfaces.md)'s hook: one rule, two executors. The
    principle's home is the questions doc under *How it works*.
33. **Five settled together** *(the user, 2026-09-28: nothing wrong, land it)*.
    - **The excerpt is the mechanism's index, not a rule.** In the shape's sense — derived on
      request, enough to decide, never a second home — as the ticket queue, the clock's dueness
      and the straw-dog listing already are: a second and third shape, so the generalisation is
      forced. Its content is a format in the questions skill: the path from root to the current
      question; the open children of every question on it; every open title under the current
      root, with one line per other root, since open questions are bounded by attention only
      within a root; the questions the last few turns attached to, since recency is the strongest
      single predictor of where a message lands in the one family that measures it; each line
      with id, title, state with kind, flags and lean. It is read as the first act of attach
      (decision 32) and of the wake; a rule installed at *every turn* was refuted as a moment the
      questions mechanism does not own.
    - **Descend or hold is a value question**: resolve the higher question first only when its
      answer could flip this one and is cheaper to get than the flip would cost; otherwise decide
      under it and record the dependency, which is *born suspect*. Decision analysis's rule,
      reconciling the families that call descent a fault with those that call it the method.
    - **Same question means same answers, never same wording**: same answer set, same partition,
      same practical consequences — every family with an identity rule states it so. Closes the
      identity item this align owned.
    - **Worth holding and well-posed**: value against cost, and the *pshita* test — an obvious
      question is not held unless someone can say what one might have thought instead; the clarity
      test — a clairvoyant who knew every future fact could answer it without judgement, or it is
      reformulated before it is held.
    - **In chat the agent declares its placement and proceeds**, asking only where the switches
      and the ticket type already make the question HITL.
34. **The store, physically** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*: a directory, `docs/questions/`, its
    painted door; one small file per question, `q-0041-which-package-manager.md`, a flat id and
    the slug; the parent line in the entry is the hierarchy's one home, and the index derived from
    those lines is the only rendered tree. Closed entries stay live while they are open or while
    any open question sits under them or depends on them; when a subtree is wholly closed and
    nothing open depends on any node in it, the maintainer moves it to `docs/questions/done/`,
    ids unchanged, links repaired by the mover, as tickets pair into `done/`. Partition of the
    live directory by root is deferred with the flat directory as default, until a listing stops
    being readable. Why: lifetime counts are five to six figures — this project wrote about 190
    question-events in 23 days; a medium project is estimated at 5,000 to 30,000 a year and open
    at once 200 to 1,000 — so archiving is mechanical and the live directory tracks the open
    count. Evidence: [the window test](../research/window-test-2026-09-28.md), 83 trials over
    four renderings at 100 to 30,000 entries — the window exact on every near placement at
    constant cost, whole-store reading stopping silently at 10,000 and answering anyway, and no
    accuracy difference between flat, tree and path renderings where a whole store is readable at
    all; so the written form is decided by writing and insertion cost, which the two passes
    settled for flat files with a parent line, and not by the reader, who never reads the store.
    Refuted: nested directories for legibility, since a person reads the tree through the index
    and the path would be a second home for the parent; one file per root, since insertion
    rewrites the page and a question moving between roots moves between files.
35. **Rule: close** *(the user, 2026-09-29)*. Moment: a message, or the agent's own work, settles
    a question — an answer lands, a question is found wrong, two are found to be one, one is
    parked, one is made irrelevant, or one is replaced. Instruction: write the kind, and for
    *decided* the link to the deciding artifact, for *deferred* the condition and the default,
    for *merged* and *superseded* the id it points to; mark every open child and every open
    dependent *suspect* if the answer changes what they assumed; move the current position to the
    parent if the closed question was current. Outcome: the entry reads `closed:<kind>` with its
    pointer, the children and dependents that need re-reading say so, and the reply's first line
    declares the closure. Behind it: a question closes only by a recorded kind (decision 7's
    list); a child goes suspect when its parent's answer changes, `.0060`'s marks applied to
    links; most families close by an act rather than a document, so the artifact link records the
    act and is not the act. Home: the questions rules file, beside attach.
36. **Rule: branch** *(the user, 2026-09-29)*. Moment: the agent or the judge, reading the
    request or the draft, detects hidden complexity — a question's shape hides parts whose
    expansion would change the answer; or `/impact` returns a split. Instruction: open each hidden
    part as a child question under the one it was found in, with *depends on* between children
    where one cannot be asked before another; leave the parent open; where the split is more than
    a name, `/impact` checks it; move the current position to the first child that can be worked.
    Outcome: the parent has children and is still open, the reply's first line declares the
    branching, and no answer lands on the parent until the children close or are deferred with a
    default. Behind it: expand before acting, the commitment lands where the question is settled
    (decision 5); the draft is judged for hidden complexity and never for level (decision 10);
    Life's *unexpanded* kinds are this moment's failure catalogue (decision 13); breadth is the
    measured pressure, so a node whose open children outgrow the frontier is repaired through
    attach's *between* on the maintainer's report. How a kind of question branches is the method's
    instrument, injected; this rule names no method — the user struck a reference to ticketing,
    and the moment's first draft, *about to be answered*, in favour of the detection itself. Home:
    the questions rules file; when the judge is not `off`, the detection is the expansion judge's
    schema.
37. **Rule: prune** *(the user, 2026-09-29)*. Moment: the agent or the judge finds a held
    question not worth holding — its answer is already entailed by what is settled and nobody can
    say what one might have thought instead (the *pshita* test); or the value of answering it is

    stand on their own to the pruned question's parent and prune the rest with it; if it was
    current, move the position to its parent. Outcome: the entry reads `closed:pruned` with its
    reason, no open question is left under it, and the reply's first line declares the prune.

    refused as evidence, as this ticket keeps its refuted shapes, and no family in the passes

    *moot*, which another answer causes, and *deferred*, which keeps a default. Home: the
    questions rules file.

## The turn, walked *(2026-09-28, the shared understanding the doc's *How it works* is written from)*

The store keeps one fact outside every entry: the id of the current question, written by whoever
attached last. On every message, unconditionally and before drafting, the agent runs the window
script, because a tier-1 rule says so — as `/recall` runs at every wake — or, where the host's
hook can, the hook runs it and the window is in context before the message is read (decision 44).
The script
reads nothing but the store: from the stored id it follows *part of* lines to the root (the path),
lists the open children of every question on the path (the frontier), lists every open title under
the same root with one line per other root, and the last few positions. It prints position, never
relevance; it has no idea what the message says. The one judgement of the turn is the agent's, or
the judge's where one is configured: reading the message against those lines and answering with
one of the attach outputs of decision 32, declared in the reply's first line, then written to the
store as the new position. A prose jump lands the same way — on an open title in the root, on
another root's line, on a new question under what it helps settle, or on a closed one found by
title search and treated as a reopening — and a wrong landing is corrected in passing, one line in
one file. Three levels, each usable without the next: the store alone (`.0010`), where the agent
judges and nobody watches the agent; plus a judge outside the generator (`.0040`), which is what
catches the agent's own descents; plus the hook, after which nothing is left to remember. The
window's half of the hook lands with the store (decision 44); the write after the turn waits on
the judge.
