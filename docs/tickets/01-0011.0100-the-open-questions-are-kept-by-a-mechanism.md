# The open questions are kept by a mechanism, and every skill works from it

- **Status:** In progress (align underway — the inception of the `questions` mechanism)
- **Type:** HITL
- **Blocks:** [Scope reads the same everywhere](./01-0014-scope-coherent.md),
  [Each skill owns its own responsibility](./01-0016-responsibility-coherent.md),
  [Every skill and document has named producers and consumers](./01-0017-io-graph-coherent.md) —
  the coherence tickets refactor onto the question store rather than onto prose
- **Outcome:** Every open question of this tree is written in one store with its relations and its
  state, the turn's current question is known and every shift from it is judged and recorded, a
  question closes by a recorded kind, and every skill that opens, splits or closes a question —
  `/align` in an interview, `/plan`, `/recall` and `/conclude` on their own — works from that
  store instead of carrying it in session text, so a question left behind is found again and a
  decision made above an unexpanded shape is caught.

## Why this exists

A discussion, or a pass, opens a question, splits it, dives into one branch and moves on; what
was left open above is carried in nobody's record and never amended. The day this was raised
showed it four times in one align, and the tree's own register holds the same shape at
[rule failures 11 and 14](../rule-failures.md). The harness already keeps open questions — in
`concerns.md`, in tickets' *Open issues*, in eighty-two straw dogs — three stores with three
formats and no state saying which question the work stands at. `/align`'s own text describes the
walk — *walk down each branch of the design tree, resolving dependencies between decisions
one-by-one* — with nothing written behind it. Life records the same failure from the other side:
of its first seven specimens of conclusions reached badly, none was caught by the agent, and it
names the missing part outright — no dedicated judge, no reader
([trace](../research/life-reasoning-failures-2026-09-28.md)).

## What to build

*(The parts, proposed by the user on 2026-09-28 and filled at the align; a declaration will name
them. The slices below cut them into deliverable passes.)*

- **The store** — every open question written, one record kind, its format in the mechanism's
  skill; identity apart from structure, an id being placement and never hierarchy.
- **Two relations, kept distinct** — *part of*, an AND/OR decomposition, and *depends on*, cannot
  be asked until; the second makes the structure a DAG, and project practice never merges the two.
- **The state** — the current question and its path, set by a judge from the request, read at
  every turn; the pacer's resume state is this.
- **The unit** — an *issue*, resolved by a decision. A ticket stays a *goal*, satisfied by evidence,
  and links to the issues it owns.
- **Closure by recorded kind** — decided; pruned as wrong; merged; deferred with a default for
  acting meanwhile; moot; superseded. Core records the kind; the method says which count.
- **Four judgements as moments with defined outputs** — *attach* (which open question a message
  addresses, opens, or neither), *branch* (a question acquiring children, including a shape hiding
  them), *close*, *prune*. Judge `jev` | `agent` | `off`, in the local file; the unasked hook is
  [01-0010.0120](./01-0010.0120-host-delivery-surfaces.md)'s.
- **Link maintenance** — a child goes *suspect* when its parent's answer changes, until cleared:
  [01-0011.0060](./done/01-0011.0060-mechanism-rechecked-when-governing-moves.md)'s marks applied
  to links.
- **The method's instruments, injected** from each method mechanism's rules file: how to branch,
  how to dive, how to close, what counts as evidence, what to discard, what counts as complexity.
  The ticketing method's slicing is the branch instrument for questions that are goals; the
  routing answer [01-0010.0080](./01-0010.0080-impact-work-shape.md) owes is another.
- **The HITL policy, read, not owned** — which questions stop for a human comes from the autonomy
  switches and the ticket's type; the threshold is [01-0020](./01-0020-pacer.md)'s. `/align` is
  the instrument for that moment, not this mechanism's instruction file.
- **What folds in** — `concerns.md`, tickets' *Open issues*, the straw dogs, the written height on
  a descent; each already does one part right and becomes an entry or a view.
- **Records small, formalised incrementally, with derived views** — capture cost is the one
  well-evidenced failure of the idea, and ceremony the adoption panel's first blocker.

## Decisions landed at 01-0012's align

Extracted 2026-09-28; each with its provenance. The narrative behind each is on
[01-0012](./done/01-0012-hierarchy-coherent.md) under *Why this exists* and the sections after it, and
the passes are research records:
[height](../research/discover-height-2026-09-27.md),
[the conversation's level](../research/discover-conversation-level-2026-09-28.md),
[the open question](../research/discover-open-questions-2026-09-28.md),
[Life's reasoning failures](../research/life-reasoning-failures-2026-09-28.md).

1. **Purpose is steering, not a taxonomy** *(the user, 2026-09-27)*: know where the work stands,
   notice a shift, send the moved topic to its home or name the move, and see where a unit of
   work has a gap — because a gap nobody names is one nobody amends. A pass that moves the
   level — `/discover`, `/impact` — must return an outcome robust enough to read its structure
   and gaps from, not one sheet mixing every level *(the user, 2026-09-27)*.
2. **A gap is work left at a level when the discussion shifted**, not a missing field; a ticket
   minted with no problem stated is something *dislocated*, a candidate word *(the user,
   2026-09-27)*.
3. **Levels are not the loop's steps**; four were named — intent, strategy, behaviour, action —
   and later found to mix two kinds, the fixed levels of a method's document kinds and the
   dynamic level of a conversation *(the user, 2026-09-27 and 2026-09-28)*; superseded by 12.
   Tier 1 / Tier 2 is not a level of either kind: the glossary already defines *tier* as
   reachability.
4. **The two directions are not alike** *(the user, 2026-09-27)*: going down before the higher
   question is settled leaves the gap; going up restructures the lower levels, which the loop
   descends again anyway. Qualified by the second pass: the asymmetry holds where a party acts
   at the lower level under pressure, which is this agent.
5. **Life's counter-case** *(the user pointed at it, 2026-09-27)*: *expand before acting* — a
   shape acted on without being expanded, when expanding is cheap and would have changed the
   shape; rule failure 11 is that error. So the rule is not about direction but where the
   commitment lands: go down to look, decide at the level asked, return. A nested pass with an
   output contract is the controlled descent; rule failure 8 is a broken return.
6. **A principle and a procedure, kept apart** *(the user, 2026-09-27)*: the general rule is
   read at tier 1 and applied by judgement; enforcement is an instruction with a defined output
   at each moment where the failure happens.
7. **The moment is the turn, and it needs a state and an observer** *(the user, 2026-09-27)*: the
   current position declared constantly, and a judge outside the generator, since the agent
   cannot watch its own tokens; a shift is observed ≠ declared. *Before landing a decision on a
   term* is not a moment; its checkable event is the write of the decision.
8. **Jev fits the observer slot** *(the user, 2026-09-27)*: text in, a schema-typed value out with
   a confidence, 70–500 ms, API-only; needs crisp definitions as its schema and a place to run,
   which is `.0120`'s.
9. **An optional judge in the turn** *(the user, 2026-09-27)*: switchable off; `jev` or `agent`
   (renamed from *subagent* 2026-09-28, since the main model may judge); judge ≠ generator, so
   the main drafts and emits while the judge reads request and draft; the height set by the judge,
   never self-declared; the judge reads the record of what has landed.
10. **Two judgements on two inputs** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*: the steering judge on the request
    — level, shift, whether a descent expands a higher shape; the expansion judge on the draft —
    a shape whose hidden complexity would change the answer; the draft is never judged for level,
    since the model answers at the request's level on its own (test kept: recurrence of
    answer-side descents once expansion is in place). On a user's descent: premature — steer back
    and ask; worthwhile — write the level being left, then follow.
11. **What is written at a worthwhile descent is the left level as a semi-formed decision** —
    settled, leaning, open — to a home robust enough to resume from *(the user, 2026-09-28: *emit*
    meant *write*)*; interim home the ticket's *Open issues* or the session's *Continuation*.
12. **The unit is the open question, not a level** *(this align on the second pass; the user
    extending it, 2026-09-28)*: level is depth in the question structure, derived; a shift is a
    message attaching to a different open question; the record is the open questions with their
    links; the fixed levels of a method's document kinds are its plan of questions, coupled to the
    dynamic stack by accommodation. **The ticketing is this same tree**, muddied by carrying a
    method's bookkeeping in the same record.
13. **The judge loop is detection and amendment of what Life calls reasoning failures** *(the
    user, 2026-09-28)*: the expansion judgement is Life's `unexpanded-*` kinds; leaving a question
    before it landed is a kind Life has not named; our rule-failures register is Life's `/failure`
    and [01-0019](./01-0019-harness-amends-itself-by-explicit-meta-rules.md) its amendment method.
14. **A question mechanism at the core** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*: the parts above; pushbacks
    held — capture cost, fragmentation, no controlled evidence anywhere, scope — and the way in is
    `/mechanism`'s *Incept*.
15. **`/align` carries the interview and nothing else** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*: the necessity
    gate, problem before machinery, one question at a time with a recommendation and
    alternatives, facts looked up and decisions asked, the decisive fact, the shared assumption,
    sharpening language, scenarios, cross-reference with code, no enacting before confirmation.
    Everything else is installed from its owner or straw-dogged: the concerns rules become this
    mechanism's; recording resolutions in the owning ticket stays ticket's, installed; the
    glossary challenge, inline update and `GLOSSARY-FORMAT.md` on
    [01-0017.0010](./01-0017.0010-terms-defined-before-they-land.md); good architecture, domain
    awareness, ADR checks, inline architecture updates, offering ADRs, `ARCH-FORMAT.md` and
    `ADR-FORMAT.md` on [01-0017.0020](./01-0017.0020-practice-swaps-in-one-edit.md); the edge
    challenge and `EDGE-FORMAT.md` on [01-0010.0100](./01-0010.0100-remaining-named-corpus.md).
16. **`/align` is not this mechanism's instruction file** *(the user, 2026-09-28, revising the
    align's first reading)*: the agent walks the tree on its own in most passes; which questions
    stop for a human is the autonomy switches' and the ticket type's, with the pacer owning the
    threshold; `/align` is the instrument for that moment and a client of the store. The
    mechanism's own skill instructs the traversal; `/align`, `/recall`, `/conclude` receive its
    rules installed.
17. **Branching is this mechanism's moment; how a kind of question branches is an instrument**
    *(the user, 2026-09-28)*: the ticketing method's slicing branches goals; it does not own
    branching. `/align`'s *decomposing is `/ticket`'s* becomes: branching is recorded in the
    store, and a goal branches by the method's slicing, with `/impact` on the split an instrument.
18. **Expansion is the branch moment** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*: a question acquiring children;
    a child slice, not a sibling mechanism.
19. **Steering depends on the store and nothing else** as a ticket dependency; the judge is what
    the steering slice builds; the unasked hook stays `.0120`'s.
20. **All slices HITL** *(the user, 2026-09-28)*: everything in work this size is discussed.
21. **The scout** *(2026-09-28)*: 01-0017's *disposition of `concerns.md`* is resolved — the store
    owns it; the spec's *intake home whose representation remains open* is resolved — the store —
    and its *`/align` handles capture/routing* splits, capture and routing here, unresolved

22. **01-0012's residues** *(2026-09-28)*: the height vocabulary sweep is a criterion of the store
    slice; *Unit of work — proposed* goes to the pacer; the load-bearing threshold and the wrapped

23. **Every ticket answers what it is for** *(the user, 2026-09-27: *any ticket must answer that
    question; right now they look like ideas without an anchor*)*. Today nothing requires it — the

## Acceptance criteria — provisional, firmed at the align

- [ ] The `questions` mechanism is declared to the shape and `mechanisms.py --check` is clean
  with it.
- [ ] Every open question of the tree is in the store, with its relations and state, and the
  store's maintainer reports a malformed entry and leaves a conforming store byte-identical.
- [ ] A turn's current question is read from the store, and a shift from it — up, down, or to
  nothing — is judged and recorded, with the question left above written on a descent.

  reported suspect until cleared.
- [ ] `/align`, `/recall` and `/conclude` work from the store; `concerns.md`, *Open issues* and
  the straw dogs are entries or views of it, with nothing lost, proved by reconciliation.
- [ ] A question that hides children is branched before it is answered.
