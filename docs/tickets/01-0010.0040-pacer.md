# Pacer

- **Status:** Planned
- **Type:** HITL
- **Depends on:** [Install /ticket](./01-0010.0030-install-ticket.md) for minting its own slices; its core rules can be aligned before that.
- **Outcome:** The agent knows, on every turn, what it does after `/recall` and what the turn's reply is scoped to, from Tier 1 rules in the entry file; and after a session break the next session resumes the delivery ring at the step it stopped, stopping only for a HITL escalation.

## Parent

[Shared harness across projects](./01-0010-dev-harness-shared-and-local.md). Minted 2026-09-06 to bind the `<temporary until="/pacer is installed">` block in `docs/process.md` to real work. The idea and the rules resolved so far live in [pacer.md](../pacer.md) until this ticket carries them.

## What to build

- The pacer's core rules, placed in `AGENTS.md`'s core part as Tier 1, with the entry contract bumped. Resolved so far, 2026-09-06: a turn holds at most one execution step; a step is the execution of a single skill; a session that starts with "what's next" starts with `/recall`, which finds the next probable move, and `/align` on what's next when nothing is active.
- The resume state a session writes at `/conclude` and reads at `/recall`, so the ring resumes where it stopped. Where it lives and what it holds are this ticket's decisions; the session record is not it.
- Ownership of `docs/process.md`: the sequence and scoping of work, with each step's rules already in the step's skill by then.
- Whether `/pacer` is a skill at all, or rules alone. The `<temporary>` condition assumes a skill; the decision may change the condition.

## Decisions this ticket's align owns

- The remaining core rules: how a step is bounded and its boundary reported; the escalation threshold at which a turn stops for a human, against the dynamic-autonomy decision on the parent.
- Resume state: owner, shape, and what `/conclude` and `/recall` do with it.
- When chaining steps inside one turn becomes allowed, and how that allowance is stated.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] The core rules are in the entry file, announced by a bumped version line, and a fresh session's first turn after `/recall` is scoped as they say.
- [ ] A session that ends mid-ring is resumed by the next session at the right step without the user restating where things stood, observed at least once.
- [ ] `docs/process.md` carries no step rules and its `<temporary>` block is gone.
