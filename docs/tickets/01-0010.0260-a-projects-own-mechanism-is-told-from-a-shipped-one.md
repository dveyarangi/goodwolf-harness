## What to build

Decision 1 of [q-dsxyi5](../questions/q-dsxyi5-where-does-a-recipient-declare-its-own-mechanisms.md#decisions):
a mark in a mechanism's declaration says the mechanism is the project's own, and its parts are
found by the claim each already carries on its first line. The shape check stops holding such a
mechanism's files to the painted doors and reports what they name under `docs/` as the project's,
because a mechanism that travels nowhere reserves nothing in another tree; an unmarked file is
held as before. A declared part is held to its claim wherever it stands, the first amendment of
[the register's entry](../rule-failures.md) on the recipient's unnamed mechanism. An origin's
marked mechanism leaves the manifest with its parts and the blocks it installed, as the origin's
local blocks do, and a recipient's maintain clock reads its marked mechanisms, since they are the
ones it makes. The mark's wording is the plan's, shown to the person before it is written: the
declaration's format is a rule.

## Acceptance criteria

- [x] 2026-11-16 — In a tree made by an install, a mechanism of its own marked in its declaration, whose doc,
      skill and claimed script name a record of its own under `docs/`, passes the shape check, and
      the check's report lists those citations as local.
- [x] 2026-11-16 — The same path named by an unmarked file under the core directory fails the check as a leak.
- [x] 2026-11-16 — A declared part standing outside the core directory that does not open with its mechanism's
      claim fails the check.
- [x] 2026-11-16 — A tree installed from an origin that holds a marked mechanism receives none of its files and
      no block it installed.
- [x] 2026-11-16 — An update of a recipient that holds a marked mechanism leaves its files and blocks, reports
      them as the recipient's own, and its gate passes.
- [x] 2026-11-16 — A recipient's maintain clock reads its marked mechanisms, and none that came with core.
- [x] 2026-11-16 — A declaration that names no owner fails the check.
- [x] 2026-11-16 — In a recipient an unbound `not yet` passes only as the kind alone: a reason after it, a
      wrapper that binds nothing and a longer word fail the check.
- [x] 2026-11-16 — `/verify` passes against this ticket and the governing docs: judged twice by a
      judge that did not build it, its findings mended or held as limits in the plan; the eight
      checks and the suite, 1141 tests, pass.
