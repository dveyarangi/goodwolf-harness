## Why this exists, and what its align decided

[TICKET-FORMAT](../../.agents/skills/ticket/TICKET-FORMAT.md) requires `Outcome` be *copied verbatim
from the ticket header* into the queue table. A copy maintained by hand is not an index; it is a
second home, and it drifts. On 2026-09-06 two rows carried paraphrased outcomes and were repaired by
hand — and the check that found them exists only because the data is duplicated.

- **Decided: the table is not committed** *(the user, 2026-10-10)*. It is an index, rendered on
  request and never committed beside its records, as the mechanism shape's R4 says. The exception
  argued here rested on *this harness has no wake hook*, untrue since the hooks reached every host;
  and nobody reads the committed table as such — not the user, and no script parses it.
- **Decided: a reader without the tool sees the file list** *(the user, 2026-10-10)*: the ticket
  files under `docs/tickets/`, or the agent telling them what stands. A recipient already holding a
  table keeps looking at its own; the queue is the project's from its first line.
- **Decided: the list** *(the user, 2026-10-10)*. `tickets.py --list` prints the live tickets in id
  order, a row each: a link to the ticket whose text is its id and slug; the status word, its
  qualifier left in the ticket; the type; the ticket's title, in a column named `Title` so it is not
  taken for the header's `Outcome`; and a link to the
  question it answers whose text is the question's id and slug. Its readers are whoever orders the
  queue and whoever is about to mint, looking for the ticket a task belongs to — the second
  [q-0024.0011](../questions/q-0024.0011-how-does-a-new-task-find-the-live-ticket-it-belongs-to-before-another-is-minted.md)'s.
- **Decided: `Last updated` goes** *(the user, 2026-10-10)*, from the queue and its arrival state,
  and the format's field row with it: when the hand-written part last moved is the log's, and a
  list rendered on request is as of its asking. Landed the same day by a separate edit.
- **Decided: the list's readers are told** *(the user, 2026-10-10)*: a rule in the ticket
  mechanism's rules file, installed in `/recall` and `/ticket` — *read the live tickets with
  `tickets.py --list` before ordering the queue or minting a ticket*. How the queue is ordered stays
  q-0027's, and how a task finds its ticket q-0024.0011's; the rule gives them the occasion only.

## What to build

- `tickets.py --list`, as decided above: the live tickets only, never `done/`; a ticket whose
  header it cannot read is reported, not left out.
- The queue's table goes. The queue keeps what is written by hand: the order, the candidate, what
  follows what.
- `TICKET-FORMAT` loses what nothing copies any more, in every place it says it — the shelf's
  stated ownership of the queue table, the placement note, the queue-row sentence under *One
  basename per work item*, the *update the queue* convention, and the queue section with its table
  template, field table and the `Outcome` verbatim rule — the straw dog bound to q-0020 around it
  resolved.
- `/ticket` stops amending a table, `/maintain` stops updating a queue row, and **P8 retires**: the
  rule deleting a closed ticket's row exists only because a person keeps the rows. Retract it from
  `ticket.rules.md` rather than leaving it installed.
- The new rule above lands in `ticket.rules.md` and is installed in `/recall` and `/ticket`.
- The arrival state, `QUEUE-ARRIVAL.md`, loses its empty table.
- The ticket mechanism's doc names the list as an output with its two readers, and its *rendering
  the queue* moment carries its instruction instead of `not yet`.
- `docs/process.md`'s pointer at the removed section is repaired.
