## What to build

- Every record an agent edits carries its kind visibly on itself — the ticket, the RFC, the
  question entry, the queue's hand-written part — each format saying where the mark sits, the
  check holding it equal to the format's declaration *(the user, 2026-10-08: the kind in the
  record is what keeps the editing agent from adding rubbish at once)*. Documents without a
  format — architecture, glossary, spec, research, session records — carry it on themselves;
  whether they are this slice's or a slice split from it is the align's.
- The ticket format admits the first kind alone: the header, `What to build`, the criteria, the
  parent and scope sections; the decisions section and the narrative go. A decision lands from
  the question's body into its durable home before the ticket closes; the story goes to evidence
  or a session record; the ticket links. Narrowed is not thinned: the ticket still holds all that
  is actual for the work, the existing state where it bears included, and fills the picture by
  references rather than by restating *(the user, 2026-10-08)*.
- The whiteboard is the body of the question the task stands on: the body's format gains a
  decisions section the mover carries, and a `close` refuses while a decision in the body cites
  no durable home.
- `/align`'s three rules that target the ticket — land and rewrite, open on what the ticket is,
  sweep — are retargeted to the question's body; the ticket is minted from it once the form is
  seen.
- The queue holds the order, the candidate and what follows what, hand-written; it holds no
  table, the live tickets listed from their headers by `tickets.py --list` on request, never
  committed *(the user, 2026-10-10, at 01-0011.0040's align)*; the completed-step narrative is removed whole, landed 2026-10-10 by a separate edit *(the user)*; the
  ordering rule stays a straw dog until a mechanism owns it.
- Core owns the ticket's contract — what a unit of agreed work must state, and that done is
  every criterion holding with verification among them — and the file is its default form; a
  project's board is admitted where it reads as the contract. The contract and the seam only;
  no adapter.
- Records written before this slice are brought to their kind at their next touch, by
  `/maintain`; the queue first.
