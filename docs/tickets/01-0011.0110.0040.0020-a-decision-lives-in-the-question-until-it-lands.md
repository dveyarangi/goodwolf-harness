## What to build

- An align's decision lands as it is made: final in its durable home, or as a straw dog bound to
  the nearest open ancestor whose answer can change its form or home, which that ancestor's close
  brings due; the question that decided closes against where it landed *(the user, 2026-10-11,
  [q-0024.0009.0003.0002.0001](../questions/q-0024.0009.0003.0002.0001-when-does-a-closing-question-land-its-decision-itself-and-when-does-it-hand-it-to-its-parent.md))*.
- A decision with nowhere to land yet is a line in the `## Decisions` list of the body of the
  lowest question holding everyone who needs it, and takes `→ <link>` when it lands. `close`
  refuses while a line of the closing question's list has no `→`, naming it: land it, or move it
  to the nearest open ancestor still holding everyone who needs it; a root closes only when all
  have landed. An answer citing a question's list is not landed for `due`.
- The ticket format admits the header, `What to build`, the criteria, `Parent`, `Out of scope`
  and `Parent scope addressed`; the decisions section, the narrative and the incepted stage go.
  Narrowed is not thinned: the ticket still holds all that is actual for the work, the existing
  state where it bears included, and fills the picture by references rather than by restating
  *(the user, 2026-10-08)*. A ticket carries `Minted`; one minted before the format moved is held
  to the required sections and the `/verify` box alone.
- `/align`'s landing rule is the questions mechanism's, Q6; the ticket keeps P7 as one clause,
  rewrite the ticket in place; P10 opens an align on its work and P11 sweeps the question's body.
  The ticket is minted from the body once the form is seen.
- The ticket format states the contract — what a unit of agreed work must say, and that done is
  every criterion holding with verification among them — with the file as its default form and a
  project's board admitted where it reads as the contract. The contract and the seam only; no
  adapter. The seam's wording is the user's to review.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A `close` on a question whose `## Decisions` list holds a line with no `→` is refused
      naming it; one whose lines all carry `→`, or that has none, closes — both tested.
- [ ] A straw dog bound to a question whose answer cites a question's list is not due; one
      whose answer cites a durable home is — both tested.
- [ ] A live ticket minted after the format moved holds no section the format no longer admits,
      one minted before is not reported for it, and one without `Minted` is reported — tested.
- [ ] `/align`'s rules land a decision as it is made and name the question's body, not the
      ticket, as where what cannot land yet is recorded and swept.
- [ ] The ticket's contract is stated in one place core owns, and names what a substitute board
      must read as; the user has reviewed its wording.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against the spec and the governing docs.
