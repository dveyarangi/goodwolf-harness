## What to build

- The question entry's body gains a decisions section the mover carries; `close` refuses while a
  decision there cites no durable home, naming the decision.
- The ticket format admits the header, `What to build`, the criteria, `Parent`, `Out of scope`
  and `Parent scope addressed`; the decisions section and the narrative go. Narrowed is not
  thinned: the ticket still holds all that is actual for the work, the existing state where it
  bears included, and fills the picture by references rather than by restating *(the user,
  2026-10-08)*. A live ticket minted before the format moves is not reported for it.
- `/align`'s three rules that target the ticket — land and rewrite, open on what the ticket is,
  sweep — name the question's body; the ticket is minted from it once the form is seen.
- The ticket format states the contract — what a unit of agreed work must say, and that done is
  every criterion holding with verification among them — with the file as its default form and a
  project's board admitted where it reads as the contract. The contract and the seam only; no
  adapter. The seam's wording is the user's to review.
