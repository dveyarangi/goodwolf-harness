## What to build

What the question's [Where it stands](../questions/q-7ebz2f-how-do-sessions-on-separate-branches-share-one-question-store-and-one-registry-of-sessions.md#where-it-stands) says, on one machine (decision 16), its
decisions landing through the children, in this order:

- [0100](./01-0010.0250.0100-a-questions-id-never-changes.md) — A question's id never changes.
- [0110](./01-0010.0250.0110-the-questions-live-on-a-branch-of-their-own.md) — The questions live on a branch of their own, seen at docs/q.
- [0030](./01-0010.0250.0030-a-session-in-a-worktree-opens-a-question-through-the-coordinator.md) — Every write of a question goes through a server raised on demand.
- [0070](./01-0010.0250.0070-a-body-is-edited-on-its-fingerprint.md) — A body is edited through a call on its fingerprint.
- [0090](./01-0010.0250.0090-the-rows-live-in-a-database.md) — The sessions' rows live in the server's database.
- [0040](./01-0010.0250.0040-a-question-closes-against-work-not-yet-on-main.md) — An answer link into a branch not yet merged names the branch.
- [0020](./01-0010.0250.0020-the-harness-runs-in-a-worktree.md) — The harness runs in a worktree of its own; independent.
- Withdrawn: [0010](./done/01-0010.0250.0010-the-mode-is-a-setting-the-person-sets.md), one mode
  and nothing to set; [0050](./done/01-0010.0250.0050-sessions-in-worktrees-see-each-other.md), into

  [0080](./done/01-0010.0250.0080-a-branchs-links-follow-a-move.md), moot under decisions 2 and 6.

Each of the person's trees is migrated once, the harness living in no one else's yet.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Two sessions, one in the main checkout and one in a worktree of its own, see each other's
      position and one tree of questions, and two questions opened at once get two ids —
      observed by someone who did not build it.
- [ ] Each of the person's trees, migrated once, works as before from its main checkout, its
      store converted and on its branch, the server raised by its first call; the store's and the
      session's tests green.
- [ ] Every child ticket is Done.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket and the governing docs.
