## What to build

Decision 8: the body is taken from the coordinator into a local file with the fingerprint it
began from, edited by ordinary means, and given back on that fingerprint; on a mismatch it is
refused with the three versions. The skills that edit bodies — align, questions, plan,
ticket, discover, maintain's repairs — take that path for every session; their text is the
self-improvement ladder's *a rule*, at this tree's dial.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A body edited from a branch lands in the store's ref, `refs/gw/questions`, through the coordinator.
- [ ] Two sessions editing one body at once: the second is refused with the three versions, and
      its merged body then lands.
- [ ] `/verify` passes against this ticket and the governing docs.
