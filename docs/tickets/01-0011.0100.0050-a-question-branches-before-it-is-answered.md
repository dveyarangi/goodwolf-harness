## What to build

- The branch judge on the draft: does the answer contain a shape whose expansion would change it,
  and which; a generic hidden-complexity prompt, nothing height-specific; `jev` or `agent`.
- The draft pipeline: the draft held where the chat cannot see it — a subagent's report or a
  scratch file — judged, and expanded before the final reply is emitted; the choice between the
  two, and the `fork` subagent's availability, decided at this slice's plan.
- Branching recorded in the store: the children opened under the question, with the relation.
- The branch instruments, injected: the ticketing method's slicing for goals, and the routing
  answer of `.0080` — whether the children are a spec, tickets, an RFC or a re-slice.

## Acceptance criteria — provisional, firmed at the align

- [ ] A draft that hides a shape with children is flagged with the shape named, and one that
  does not is not; proved on the recorded specimens — rule failure 11, the day's strategy question
  answered at action.
- [ ] A flagged draft is expanded and re-drafted before anything reaches the chat, and the
  children are entries of the store under their parent.
- [ ] `/impact` returns the routing answer `.0080` owes, and `/ticket` and `/plan` receive it.
- [ ] `/verify` has been run on this ticket against its ticket, RFC, and governing docs.
