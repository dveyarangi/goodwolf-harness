## What to build

- The mark is `- **record of** <kind>`, the kind one of the method glossary's four — *what it
  intends to become*, *what must always hold*, *what exists*, *what happened* *(the user,
  2026-10-08: "kind" is too generic; `record of` reads each value as what the record is a record
  of)*. It replaces `.0030`'s `- **kind**` everywhere it stands: the six mechanism docs, the
  mechanism format, and the check in `mechanisms.py`.
- Each format says where its records carry the mark, and the check holds it equal to the
  format's declaration: the ticket's in `tickets.py`, the question entry's in `questions.py`. The
  ticket format's retired `Kind` field stays refused — the check tells the two apart.
- The queue's hand-written part carries the mark of the ticket mechanism's queue.
- An RFC carries the mark by hand: its format belongs to no declared mechanism, so no check
  holds it until `/plan` is one.
- A record a script alone writes carries none.
- Every live record is marked in this slice, and the checks hold it at once — a check cannot
  tell an old unmarked record from a new one; question entries re-rendered by `questions.py`'s
  own writer *(the user, 2026-10-10, at the plan)*.
- A ticket and its RFC closing into `done/` are marked `what happened` by the mover, as a
  question entry is by its close *(the user, 2026-10-10, at the plan)*.
- Edge records and records without a format are not this slice's:
  [q-0024.0009.0003.0001.0001](../questions/q-0024.0009.0003.0001.0001-how-do-the-records-outside-the-ticket-the-queue-the-question-entry-the-rfc-and-the-mechanism-doc-show-what-they-are-a-record-of.md).
