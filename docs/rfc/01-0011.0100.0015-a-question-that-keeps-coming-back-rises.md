# A question that keeps coming back rises — implementation plan

**Authored:** 2026-10-02

Implements [01-0011.0100.0015 a-question-that-keeps-coming-back-rises](../tickets/01-0011.0100.0015-a-question-that-keeps-coming-back-rises.md).

## The decisions it follows

[Decisions 53 and 54](../tickets/01-0011.0100-the-open-questions-are-kept-by-a-mechanism.md#decisions-landed-at-this-tickets-align)
*(the user, 2026-10-02)*:

- A held, open question is struck when a declared `at` lands on it at least twelve hours after it
  was opened or last struck.
- The strike is recorded as `struck <n>, last <time>`, written only at opening and at a strike.
- Each window line carries the count when it is above zero.
- The wake's read lists the most-struck open questions first.

No architecture changes. These are the mechanism's own record format and script behaviour, and
they fail the load-bearing counter-test.

## Settled at `/align`, 2026-10-02, while planning

1. **Any declared `at` counts** *(the user; decision 53 as amended)*. Staying on a question
   counts too. Q1 declares an `at` every turn, so a session working on one question for two days
   strikes it four times. The count ranks time spent on a question as well as its returns.
   Refused: striking only an `at` that arrives on the question.
2. **X is a constant, twelve** *(the user; decision 53 as amended)*. It is `STRIKE_AFTER_HOURS` in
   `questions.py`, beside `STALE_AFTER_DAYS`. No script reads a project's value today, and the
   first one to do so is new shape. Refused for now: reading `strike-after=` from the local block.
3. **The removal clause is `undepends q-A on q-B`** *(the user)*. It mirrors `depends q-A on q-B`.

**Settled and reported** (decision 51, not load-bearing): the stamp's time is UTC to the minute,
`2026-10-02T19:40Z`. The store is shared through Git, possibly between machines in different
zones, and a zone-less local time would compare wrongly across them.

## What the code does today

- `PARTS` has six parts. `_entry_text` writes them in that order.
- `declare` receives `today: date`, and the sessions line keeps a date.
- `_Draft.place` checks that the `at` names an open question and writes nothing.
- A closed entry that depends on an open one is a check diagnostic (`_unmarked_suspects`). That is
  why q-0008, which depends on q-0015, cannot close until the dependency goes.
- The window's lines come from `_line`, except the root's open questions, which have their own
  format in `_root_lines`.

## Stages

All stages are test-first under `/tdd`, with time injected. Each stage keeps the suite green.

**1. The part, and its check.**

- `PARTS` gains `struck`, written last.
- The check accepts an entry without it. When present, it must read
  `<n>, last <YYYY-MM-DDTHH:MMZ>` with `n` a whole number; any other form is a diagnostic naming
  the expected form.
- Tests:
  - a stamped entry checks clean;
  - an unstamped one checks clean;
  - each malformed form — a missing `last`, a zone-less time, a negative count — is reported for
    that reason.

**2. The writer stamps and strikes.**

- `declare` receives the current time, injectable. `main` passes the UTC clock. The sessions line
  keeps its date, derived from that time. How the parameter is spelled is the implementer's
  choice.
- `opens` stamps the new entry `struck 0, last <now>`.
- After all clauses apply, the `at`'s question is read:
  - unstamped: stamp it `0` and do not strike it;
  - last stamp twelve or more hours before now: `n + 1` and `last <now>`;
  - otherwise: nothing.
  
  The strike joins the draft like any other change, so the existing interference recheck covers
  it. When two sessions race, the second is refused, re-reads the fresh stamp, and does not strike.
- Tests:
  - opening stamps zero, and an `opens` with an `at` on the new question does not strike it;
  - a reach at exactly twelve hours strikes once, and at eleven hours fifty-nine writes nothing;
  - an unstamped entry is stamped at its first reach and not struck;
  - a stamp in the future, from a clock ahead, does not strike;
  - **AC2:** five ordinary reaches inside twelve hours leave every entry byte-identical, and the
    window answers *unchanged*;
  - after a strike, a second session's `at` within the hour does not strike again.

**3. `undepends`.**

- The grammar gains `undepends q-A on q-B`.
- It refuses when q-B is not among q-A's dependencies, and says so.
- The `depends on` part is removed when its list empties.
- `FORMS` gains the clause.
- Tests:
  - one of two dependencies removed;
  - the last one removed, along with the part;
  - an absent one refused, with the store unchanged.

**4. The window and the wake.**

- A window line carries `struck <n>` when n is above zero, both from `_line` and from the root's
  open-question lines. The exact placement on the line is the implementer's choice.
- The wake's read opens with a section of the open, live questions struck at least once, highest
  count first, ties by id. It is capped at a few lines (five, unless implementing shows otherwise),
  so the read stays bounded at the store sizes the window test measured.
- Tests:
  - a struck line shows its count, and an unstruck one shows none;
  - the wake lists struck open questions in rank order and leaves out closed and archived ones.

**5. The words, and q-0008.**

- [The questions skill](../../.agents/skills/questions/SKILL.md):
  - the clause table gains `undepends`;
  - *Records* declares the `struck` part: written by the script at opening and at a strike, never
    by hand;
  - the example entry shows a stamp.
- [The mechanism doc](../../.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.md), *How it works*, gains one
  sentence: a question reached twelve hours or more after its last stamp is struck, and the count
  ranks it where a session starts and on its window line.
- One declared line (the `at` may be wherever the session stands):

  `at q-0001; undepends q-0008 on q-0015; closes q-0008: decided, [the questions mechanism's *How it works*](../../.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.md#how-it-works) — the user, 2026-10-02`

- Check: `questions.py --check` and `mechanisms.py --check` are clean.

## Follow-ups and limits

- **The count mixes time spent and returns** (settled item 1). If the wake's ranking turns out to
  surface long-worked roots rather than recurring questions, that is the evidence to revisit the
  amendment.
- **Entries never reached stay unstamped.** They hold no count until their first reach.
