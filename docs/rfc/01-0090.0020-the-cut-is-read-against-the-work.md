## The shape

The slice is text: one skill and one section of the session mechanism's doc. It adds no script,
no record and no check; the cut's record is written by the next slice,
[01-0090.0010](../tickets/01-0090.0010-each-role-of-this-tree-stands-in-a-mechanism-of-its-own.md),

**The part.** `session.md` gains:

- a section, *The cut*, holding the declaration below;
- rows in *Moments*, each instructed by the skill: sessions step on each other; a zone outgrows
  its session; the cut is read against the work; a tree with no cut. *A session takes a role*
  keeps its row, instructed by `/recall`;

- a bullet in *What it produces*: **the proposal** — *what exists* — read by the person, who
  decides;

Kept apart so that it can leave as a mechanism at the cost of a move: the section, the skill's
folder, its bullet and its rows go together. *Keeps:* *The skill* — "kept apart so that it can
leave as a mechanism".

## Stages

Every instruction's text is drafted through `/skill-up` and shown to the person before it lands.
Each stage ends with the eight checks green; the slice changes no file that is not markdown. At
each stage's end the keeper is sent what it did and what a fresh tree would need to do the same,
for the journal the ticket asks.

0. **The cheapest example** — run; under *The cheapest example*.
1. **The section and the skill**, after the person's answers to *To decide*; S11 as *To decide*
   3 answers. Shown, then landed; L13's sentence sent to the keeper. Proof: `mechanisms.py --check` — criterion 4; the texts
   under their headers before the commit — criterion 5.
2. **The judged runs.** Each run is judged by a second subagent that did not build it.
   - *A collision* — criterion 1. A fixture of two sessions' rows and commits: both edit the same
     two mechanism docs; both also write one ticket, as holders of its unit by turn; one edits a
     skill no mechanism claims. Given `/pack`, a fresh subagent names the docs' zones as the
     overlap and a way to separate them, and leaves the ticket and the unclaimed skill out of it.
     A control subagent without `/pack` is given the same fixture; the judge says what the skill
     added.
   - *This tree's cut* — criterion 2. A subagent of this session is handed the entry file, whose
     local rule lists the roles, so it is no blind run. The run is a session the person starts in
     a clone prepared for it: the local rule's list removed and re-installed; the records that
     hold the accepted cut deleted; the history squashed to one commit, since commit subjects
     name the zones. It runs in two halves: the zones from the code and the domain alone, each
     citing its rule; then, given the rows, the sessions mapped onto them. The judge reads the cut
     against the one the person accepted —
     [q-mz6gd4](../questions/q-mz6gd4-where-does-a-session-of-this-tree-read-each-roles-zone-and-brief-and-which-session-holds-it.md#decisions)
     decision 1 as decisions 4 and 6 amend it, seven roles on six sessions, Путь a zone the local rules describe alone — and checks that a new role
     is proposed only on a repeated collision or an outgrown zone. The audits' role came from the
     person's word, and the whole has audits run as subagents; the rules are not expected to
     derive it, and the difference goes to the person with the others.
   - *The declaration* — criterion 3. Given the section alone, a fresh reader says where a role's
     zone and who holds which are written, who reads each and at which moment, and answers one
     case the table does not list: a running session, after the cut changed.
3. **`/verify`**, then the keeper told that the declaration stands.

## Impact

**Impact.** One new skill folder and one section of `session.md`, with its bullet and moment
rows; nothing else. Every session of every tree that holds core is handed one more skill
description, the price of the person's call that `/pack` derives the cut.

**Hidden edges.**
- The declaration names readers the next slice builds, the shape check and the installer;
  their cells are straw dogs and fall due when it lands.
- S11 has a session write "the role's name" on its row; decision 5 has the row bear the
  session's name. *To decide*, 3.
- L13 has this tree's store session hand a repeated collision to the keeper as a rule's
  failure; stage 1 sends the keeper the sentence that has it run `/pack`.
- A recipient tree gets `/pack` at its next update and holds no cut; the skill's moment for a
  tree with no cut is what it meets.
- `/pack` reads git history and the sessions' words, which this tree's commit subjects carry;
  a tree whose subjects name no zone gives it less, and the outside read carries more.

**Leave alone.** The rows, the window and the holds; the shape's format and check; the
installer; the local rules beyond L13's one sentence; the roles' mechanisms.

**Validation, 2026-11-21.** A reviewer that did not write the plan found three blocking problems:
- the blind run in a clone was not blind, since a subagent is handed its parent's entry file;
- the rule placing an installed block in a zone gave a zone a part of a file;
- a tree with no table was said to hold no cut, against the default the whole gives.

They also found the three added rules and the declaration's homes above the dial, cells resting
on open questions unwrapped, the moment a running session learns of a change missing, the
collision run too easy to prove the skill, the journal step and four parts of `session.md`
forgotten, and L13 left disagreeing with `/pack`. This amendment answers all of them; the rules
and the homes are put to the person.

**Recommendation.** Proceed: the narrowest slice that lets the next one write to a declared
form, with the three rules the example showed missing put to the person.
