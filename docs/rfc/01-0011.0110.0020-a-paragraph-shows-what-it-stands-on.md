## What governs it

- The spec's *Names and marks*: the three marks, the box, what carries none, `debug=on`.
- The owning question's body, where the rule's text was agreed with the user on 2026-10-07 in
  three corrections — the box instead of an empty anchor, the footing apart from the principle,
  the lamp for principles and the anchor for footing — and the puzzle piece for a classification.
- R5: a mechanism's rule lands in the entry file only as an installed block; while the mechanism
  is undeclared, the rule is written by hand and wrapped as a straw dog bound to the question of
  what the mechanism owns. No mechanism owns principles, so the rule is a straw dog bound to the
  owning question.
- The glossary's *Kind of statement*, landed by `.0010`: the names the marks cite.
- *Tier is paid by every session*: the rule's occasion is every claiming paragraph of every
  reply under `debug=on`, so tier 1 is its tier and no cheaper home exists.

## Decisions this plan makes

1. **One paragraph, by hand, in the entry file's general section**, after the bullets and before
   the installed blocks, wrapped `<straw-dog question="q-0030">`. It holds both sentences the spec
   gives this slice: that a principle or an agentic classification carries a name at its home and
   is cited by it, and the marks themselves.
2. **Core states the glyphs as the emoji**: `💡`, `🧩`, `⚓`, `⬜`. A local override of a
   hand-written rule is impossible — an override names a rules-file id, and this rule has none
   until a mechanism owns it — so the spec's *a project overrides them as L6 does* is a seam
   left open, and the user's own glyphs are what ships. Noted in the straw dog's body by what it
   says, not by naming the seam.
3. **The box is a blockquote**: `> ⬜ no principle fits — <what it rests on>`, in the lamp's place.
4. **The rule text**, fixed here because the user grades against it:

   > **A paragraph shows what it stands on.** Every principle, and every classification an
   > agent applies by judgement, carries a name at its authored home and is cited by that name.
   > Under `debug=on`, a paragraph that claims or recommends opens with the marks it earns, in
   > this order: `💡 *<name>*` the one principle it is governed by; `🧩 *<name>*` the
   > classification it sorts by; `⚓ *<footing>*` what it rests on — a decision, evidence or the
   > user's word, by its record or by who and when. Where no principle fits, a blockquote
   > `> ⬜ no principle fits — <what it rests on>` stands in the lamp's place, and the box is the
   > signal: a principle is missing from the list, or the reasoning has none. A paragraph that
   > links, narrates or restates carries no mark.

5. **The contract moves to v34**, and the findings record says what changed.
6. **Grading is the ticket's second and third box and is not this slice's to check**: the
   ticket stays open after `/verify` until the user has read a week of replies, as `.0040` did.

## Impact — 2026-10-08

**Impact.** The entry file (one paragraph, the version line) and the findings record; every
reply under `debug=on` from the next session on; every recipient at its next update, where the
rule shows nothing unless its local block sets `debug=on`. The rules-level fingerprint of all
five mechanisms moves again — the clock, marked at the next pass. **Hidden edges.** The straw
dog's wrapper is stripped on install, so the paragraph ships as a plain rule naming no
question — written to stand alone. The marks and Q1's rows are two instruments at tier 1 on the
same reply; the rows name the turn's question, the marks a paragraph's footing, and neither
restates the other. A principle named in a mark that has no bold lead anywhere is itself a
finding for the list — the box's purpose. **Leave alone.** Q1 and L6; the glossary; the
skills. **Recommendation:** proceed.

## Cheapest refutation

That the marks can be honest without ritual: the replies of 2026-10-07 and 2026-10-08 carried
them by hand before the rule existed; the user's grade over a week is the test, and a box that
appears on a claim the agent could not found is the positive result.
