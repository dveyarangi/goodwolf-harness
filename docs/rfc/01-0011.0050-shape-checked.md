## Shapes

### The `not yet` row — `MECHANISM-FORMAT.md` § Moments and § Parsing

The *kind, and why* cell of a `not yet` row is a straw dog:

```md
| ordering the queue | — | <straw-dog until="the pacer owns it" ticket="docs/tickets/01-0020-pacer.md">not yet</straw-dog> |
```

- **Body**: the kind, `not yet`, and nothing else. Words after it are a diagnostic — *the reason
  belongs in `until`*.
- **`until`**: the condition that ends the gap, in the row's own words — what a person would
  read as why the moment is uninstructed. Required, as on every straw dog.
- **`ticket`**: the binding, a repository-relative path to a live ticket. Required. It is the
  row's referent.

An unwrapped `not yet` is **unbound** and a diagnostic. A binding that does not resolve, or
resolves under `done/`, is a diagnostic as today — *closed work fills no gap*. The other three
kinds keep their form: kind, ` — `, the clause, the referent as a backticked core path; a
wrapper around one of them is an ordinary straw dog on the cell and is read as such, kind from
the body. The parsing rule's one exception — *except a `not yet` referent, which is a markdown
link* — goes; every referent is now a code span or a binding, and the cell carries no link.

**What ships.** The shear strips the wrapper; a recipient's row reads `not yet`. That row is
upstream's gap, and the recipient's check must not fail on it. Which tree is the origin is a
fact the installer records ([`.0130`](../tickets/01-0010.0130-harness-installs-into-another-tree.md));
until then only the origin runs the check, and the diagnostic's code carries a `TODO` naming
that ticket, per the entry file's straw-dog form for code. **For `.0130` to inherit:** a wrapper
in a table cell is an *inline* tag — opened and closed on one line, the cell's text between —
and the install spec describes the shear over block wrappers only. The lister already walks
inline tags; the shear must strip them the same way, leaving the row a row.

**Two readers, one binding.** `straw_dogs.py` lists the row like any straw dog — condition,
owner, unknown-owner diagnostic — and rules nothing about `done/`, because whether a condition
holds is `/maintain`'s judgement. `mechanisms.py` rules on the gap: an archived binding fails.
Both are right and neither replaces the other; the format shelf says so in one sentence.

### The allowlist — a record of mechanism-shape, declared in `MECHANISM-FORMAT.md`

One file, `.agents/mechanisms/allowlist.md`, at the root of the register beside the mechanism
directories — beside it, not part of it: the register is still the directories, the index is
still rendered and never written, and the shelf's *no file holds the register* stays true. The
allowlist is a record of one mechanism that happens to live at the register's root, because what
it lists is what the register does not hold. Declared in the shelf with the three fields every
record carries:

- **A record** is the file; an **entry** is one row of its one table, one core artifact no
  declared mechanism claims. **Tier 2**: read by `mechanisms.py --check` on every `/verify`, by
  the installer for the manifest, and by whoever adds a skill, through R6.
- **What removes an entry**: a `not yet` row is a straw dog and retires when its binding's ticket
  lands, through `/maintain`; an `unowned by design` row is kept by design.

Grammar, after a title line and a preamble that is not read:

```md
| what | kind, and why |
|---|---|
| `.agents/skills/align/` | <straw-dog until="01-0017 declares the align mechanism" ticket="docs/tickets/01-0017-io-graph-coherent.md">not yet</straw-dog> |
| `docs/glossary.md` | <straw-dog until="…" ticket="…">not yet</straw-dog> |
```

- **what** — a backticked repository-relative path: a skill directory under `.agents/skills/`
  with its trailing `/`, or a `docs/` place — a directory with its trailing `/`, or a file. A
  path under `.agents/` must exist; a `docs/` place is not checked for existence, because a
  place is what a recipient creates and `.0140`'s check is what consumes the row.
- **kind, and why** — the moments table's absence grammar, restricted to two kinds: `not yet`,
  wrapped, bound to the ticket that will declare the artifact's mechanism; `unowned by design`
  with its clause. `elsewhere` or `embedded` here is a diagnostic — an allowlist row is a claim
  that nothing owns the artifact, not a pointer to what does.
- A `what` that a declaration already names — a skill whose `SKILL.md` is some mechanism's
  instruction — is a diagnostic: *named by `<slug>` and allowlisted*. One home.

**What this slice writes into it:** the nineteen skill rows, each `not yet` bound to the ticket
that will declare its mechanism — [01-0017](../tickets/01-0017-io-graph-coherent.md) for most,
and where the queue already routes a declaration elsewhere, that ticket: `/recall` to
[01-0020](../tickets/01-0020-pacer.md), `/edge` to
[01-0010.0100](../tickets/01-0010.0100-remaining-named-corpus.md). The binding is read off the
queue and the tickets at `/implement`, not decided here. **What it does not write:** the `docs/` place
rows. Nothing reads a place row until `.0140`'s check exists, and a row nothing reads is the
thing the register's directory rule reports; `.0140` writes them when it consumes them. This
slice ships the grammar that admits them.

**What the installer reads off it** *(resolved at `/plan`, for `.0130` to inherit)*: rows under
`.agents/` name what ships; rows under `docs/` name what the recipient has by construction and
are never copied. The `what` column's first path segment is the discriminator.

### The reverse pass — `mechanisms.py --check`

After the declarations are read, one more question of the same register: **every directory
under `.agents/skills/` is named by a declaration or held by the allowlist.** Named means some
declaration's `instruction` bullet is `<directory>/SKILL.md` — the shape's *one instruction file*
is the whole of what "a mechanism names a skill" means; a skill's appendix files ride with it.
A skill that is neither is a diagnostic: *no mechanism names `.agents/skills/x/` and the allowlist
does not hold it*, and the run fails. A skill two declarations name is a diagnostic too —
*named by `<slug>` and `<slug>`* — since naming a mechanism and naming its skill are one act and
a skill is one mechanism's instruction file; nothing checks this today, and the reverse pass is
the first place it can be seen.

The report gains two keys beside `declarations` and `diagnostics`:

- `skills` — one entry per installed skill: its directory, `named by` (a slug or null), and
  `allowlisted` (the row's kind, why and referent, or null).
- `allowlist` — the rows as read, kind, why and referent each, for the person reading a failure
  and for the installer.

An absent allowlist is an empty one: no diagnostic of its own, because every unclaimed skill
already is one.

### R6 — `mechanism-shape.rules.md`, into `/skill-up` and `/mechanism`

```md
## R6 — a skill is claimed or listed

- **target** `.agents/skills/skill-up/SKILL.md`
- **target** `.agents/skills/mechanism/SKILL.md`
- **authority** the user, 2026-09-08

<rule>
A skill is a mechanism's one instruction file, or sits in `.agents/mechanisms/allowlist.md`
with its reason: `not yet`, bound to the ticket that declares its mechanism, or `unowned by
design`. Write the row as you add the skill.
</rule>
```

`/skill-up` gains the anchor heading `## Installed from other mechanisms` at its end — the
heading `/maintain` carries for the same purpose — and one row in the anchor table names it.
`/mechanism`'s target is already in the table; R6 joins its block under `## Records`. The
sentence *A skill belonging to no mechanism goes in the allowlist, with its reason* leaves
`/mechanism`'s body: the rule is read at two occasions, so it is installed at both, the
owner's own skill included, and no body restates one.

### The guesser — `straw_dogs.py`

`_KIND_ROW` and the `KINDS` import go. A wrapped row is blanked as every wrapped span is; an
*unwrapped* `not yet` row is now what it always was, an unwrapped straw dog, and its tells are
candidates for a person to judge. The guesser never fails the run, so this is a finding, not a
failure. `elsewhere` and `embedded` rows carry no tells today; if one does, it is a real
candidate.

### The eleven rows

Each `not yet — <clause>, [t](../../../docs/tickets/T.md)` becomes
`<straw-dog until="<condition>" ticket="docs/tickets/T.md">not yet</straw-dog>`. The clause
becomes the condition, rephrased where a clause states a fact rather than what ends it —
*nothing lists them* → *something lists them, .0030* is the shape. Eleven rewrites, judgement
per row, at `/implement`.

## Flows

**Reading a row.** Cell → wrapper present? → attributes read through the corpus reader's
binding grammar → body → kind. `not yet`: referent is the binding; body beyond the kind is a
diagnostic; no wrapper is *unbound*. Other kinds: as today, from the body.

**Closing a ticket a row names.** Paired close moves the ticket; the mover retargets the row's
binding into `done/`; the next `mechanisms.py --check` reports the row as naming an archived
ticket; `/maintain` rewrites the row to the instruction that now exists — the straw dog retired
by its own machinery.

**Adding a skill.** `/skill-up` reads R6 and writes the allowlist row, bound to the ticket that
will declare the mechanism; `mechanisms.py --check` reads it and says nothing. Adding one without
the row: the next `/verify` fails on the reverse pass.

**Declaring a mechanism.** The declaration's `instruction` names the skill; the allowlist row
now contradicts it — *named and allowlisted* — and is removed in the same slice, its straw dog
retired.

## Stages

Each stage leaves the verification set green; the order is what makes that true.

1. **The shelf.** `MECHANISM-FORMAT.md` states the row form, the parsing rule without its
   exception, the allowlist record, the two-readers sentence. Nothing runs against the shelf, so
   the set stays green; but the shelf and the check disagree until stage 2 lands, so **stages 1
   and 2 are one commit**.
2. **The binding form, with the rows.** The wrapper's attribute grammar moves from
   `straw_dogs.py` into `docs_corpus.py` beside the binding it already owns — `straw_dogs.py`
   imports from `mechanisms.py` until stage 3, so `mechanisms.py` cannot import from it. Red
   first: a wrapped `not yet` is read with its binding as referent and its condition as why; an
   unwrapped one is unbound; a body beyond the kind is a diagnostic; a binding into `done/` is
   archived. The existing fixtures that write the link form move to the binding form. Then
   `_absence` reads the wrapper; the eleven rows are rewritten in the same stage, so
   `mechanisms.py --check` stays green across the commit. The `TODO` for `.0130` goes beside the
   unbound diagnostic.
3. **The lister.** `_KIND_ROW` and the import go; the guesser test flips to *an unwrapped
   `not yet` row is a candidate; a wrapped one is not*. One end-to-end case: a mechanism doc
   whose row binds a ticket, the ticket archived through the mover, the binding followed it, the
   check reports *archived*. The straw-dog count over the declared scope rises from 23 to 34
   plus the one `TODO`; the queue header's number moves.
4. **The allowlist and the reverse pass.** Red first: an installed skill named by no declaration
   and absent from the allowlist fails; one allowlisted as `not yet` with a binding passes; one
   both named and allowlisted fails; an `elsewhere` row in the allowlist fails; a `.agents/`
   `what` that does not exist fails; a `docs/` `what` that does not exist does not; two
   declarations naming one skill fail; an allowlist row's wrapper is listed by
   `straw_dogs.py`'s survey with its condition and owner. Then the parser and the pass; the
   file with its nineteen rows; the report's two keys. **R6 lands
   here, not earlier** — the ticket's rule is that an instruction that cannot be followed is not
   installed, and until this stage there is no allowlist to write a row into: R6 written into
   the rules file, `/skill-up` given its anchor, the sentence taken out of `/mechanism`'s body,
   `inject_rules.py mechanism-shape --install --overwrite`, and `inject_rules.py --check` green.
5. **The doc.** Mechanism-shape's moments, parts, produces and evidence as above; the `not yet`
   row about the allowlist retired. `mechanisms.py --check` green over the doc that describes it.

## Tests

Behavioral, through the harness every case in `.agents/scripts/test/` uses: a temporary
repository, files written, the script run, JSON and exit code asserted. Each case proves the
reason, not the path:

- *Wrapped row* — the referent is the binding's path and the why is the condition; the report
  carries both.
- *Unbound* — the diagnostic names the occasion and says *unbound*, not *does not resolve*.
- *Body beyond the kind* — the diagnostic says the reason belongs in `until`.
- *Archived binding* — after the mover, the diagnostic says *archived*; before it, none.
- *Reverse pass* — the failing case names the skill directory; the passing case's `skills`
  entry shows the allowlist row it matched.
- *Named and allowlisted* — the diagnostic names both the slug and the row.
- *Named twice* — the diagnostic names both slugs and the skill.
- *Allowlist row listed* — the survey's statement carries the row's `until` and `ticket`, and
  its line is the row's.
- *Guesser* — the flipped case asserts the unwrapped row's tell and line, and the wrapped
  row's absence from the candidates.

## Limitations and follow-ups

- **The recipient's check** fails on a sheared `not yet` until `.0130` records which tree is the
  origin. The `TODO` names it; nothing here can lift it.
- **The place rows** are `.0140`'s to write; the grammar admits them from this slice.
- **The map of maintenance owed** — `not yet` and `embedded` rows across the corpus, and the
  nineteen allowlist rows — is [01-0017](../tickets/01-0017-io-graph-coherent.md)'s to work
  through; this slice makes it readable, not shorter.
- **Whether `unowned by design` ever holds for a skill** is unknown; no row uses it here. The
  grammar admits it so the allowlist is not by construction a list of debts.
- **A recipient's own skills.** The reverse pass reads every directory under `.agents/skills/`,
  and a recipient that adds a skill of its own has one no shipped mechanism names. The
  allowlist is core, and an installed block is a paragraph after an anchor — not a table row —
  so how a recipient's local file contributes allowlist rows is
  [`.0110`](../tickets/01-0010.0110-project-facets-injected.md)'s to shape, not this slice's.
  Stated so it is inherited, not discovered.
- **A `TODO`'s ticket path is not retargeted on close** — the mover rewrites `.md` only, by
  design. When `.0130` closes, the `TODO` beside the unbound diagnostic reports as *unknown
  owner* and `/maintain` retires it with the code it marks. Existing behaviour, not this slice's.
- **A pipe inside `until`** must be written `\|`, as the shelf already requires of any cell.

## Resolved at `/plan`

Seven choices the align did not make, each local and reversible, stated so `/implement` does not
choose again:

1. **A wrapped `not yet`'s body is the kind alone**; a clause after it is a diagnostic. The
   user's words: body is `not yet`, the reason belongs to the tag.
2. **The wrapper's attributes are read through `docs_corpus`**, which already owns the binding
   grammar the mover rewrites, so nothing is checked that would not also be repaired;
   `straw_dogs.py`'s attribute regex moves there and both scripts read one grammar. The lister
   keeps its own walker; it needs spans and nesting, the check does not.
7. **R6 is installed in the stage that creates the allowlist**, by the ticket's own rule.
3. **Named means the instruction bullet.** A skill is a mechanism's one instruction file; an
   appendix under the same directory rides with it and needs no row.
4. **A `docs/` place is not checked for existence** in the allowlist; a `.agents/` path is.
5. **The installer ships `.agents/` rows and never copies `docs/` rows** — stated here for
   `.0130`, which reads the allowlist for its manifest.
6. **R6 targets `/mechanism` as well as `/skill-up`**, by the self-install policy of 2026-09-20,
   and the body sentence leaves.
