## Governing boundaries

- **A question asking the person is an open question of the store**, its id kept for good, its
  decision landing in its body at the close, written by the store's calls only
  ([the questions mechanism](../../.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.md)).
- **It stays in the wake until it closes** — no week's horizon, as the path's *touched lately* has
  (q-0037.0010.0009.0009.0001, decision 2).
- **Scripts enumerate and decide nothing**
  ([Responsibilities](../architecture.md#responsibilities)).
- **A decision on another running session's question is that session's to put to the person** (S9,
  installed in the entry file).
- **Who leaves such a question is not this slice's.** The mark is the store's, open to any
  caller; the rule by which a pass with no person leaves one is 01-0011.0110.0100's, with
  `/maintain`'s section for such a pass.

## Impact

**Must change.**

- `.agents/scripts/gw/questions.py`: a part `asks`, kept by every write of the entry; a call

  to the target; the wake's section.
- `.agents/scripts/gw/test/test_questions.py`: the cases below.
- `.agents/skills/questions/QUESTION-FORMAT.md` and the calls table in
  `.agents/skills/questions/SKILL.md`: the part and its call.
- `.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.rules.md`: Q2, reinstalled into `/recall`.
- `.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.md`: the wake's read gains the section; the moment of
  marking a question as asking the person.

**Must verify.** A wake with nothing asking shows no such section; the store's check green on this
tree; the verification set green.

**Hidden edges.**

- **The part must stand in the store's list of parts**, or the next write of the entry — `at`
  with its strike, `lean`, `rung`, `suspect`, `move`, `reword` — drops it unread; tested for each.
- **A merge or a supersession closes the question its target carries on**: the target takes the
  mark, as it takes the decisions; any other close ends it, *deferred* included — a deferral is
  the person's decision to wait.
- **`ask` on a question that already asks reports it and writes nothing**; on a closed question it
  refuses. A closed entry carrying the part by hand is a diagnostic, failing the check.
- **The wake's section helper writes `(none)` under an empty heading**; this section is added only
  when a question asks.
- **No bound, and first.** Every asking question is listed, one line each: a wait hidden behind a
  count is a wait that sinks. The section opens the wake's read, before what is most struck, so a
  host that cuts a long context cuts the window last read, not the decisions waiting.
- **A question in another running session's subtree** is listed with that session's tag and is
  not put by this one: it is the other's to put.
- **The store's other *waits*** — a question waiting on another, a decision waiting for its home —
  keep the word; the mark is called *asks* and its call `ask`.

**Leave alone.** The marks and dueness; the path and its horizon; *suspect*; `/maintain`.

## Stages

Each stage ends green; the tests run, a script changing.

1. **The part, red first** in `test_questions.py`:
   - `ask` on an open question writes `asks the person, <today>`, and the check passes;
   - `ask` on a closed one refuses and writes nothing; `ask` on one already asking writes nothing
     and says so;
   - after `at`, `lean`, `rung`, `suspect`, `move` and `reword` on it, the part stands;
   - `close … decided` ends it; `close … merged` and `close … superseded` carry it to the target;
   - a closed entry carrying the part by hand fails the check;
   - the wake lists every asking question, more than any bound, opening its read; a question under
     another running session's position carries its tag; a store with none shows no heading.

   Then `questions.py`.
2. **The instructions**: the format and calls table, the doc's wake and moment, Q2 in the rules
   file and installed; `inject_rules.py --check` and `mechanisms.py --check` green.
3. **Read live**: in a scratch copy of the tree, so no running session's wake shows a test
   question, one question is marked with `ask`; a subagent with none of this session, started
   there as a session, writes its first report under `/recall`; a judge who did not build it reads
   whether the question is put to the person as a decision. The question is then closed and the
   wake read again for no section.
