## Governing boundaries

Each choice below stands on a line of
[q-0037.0010.0009.0009](../questions/q-0037.0010.0009.0009-how-does-maintain-run-on-its-own-as-a-scheduled-task-what-scope-it-takes-where-it.md)'s
`Where it stands` — *it leaves each decision as an open question of the store marked as waiting on
the person; every wake shows every waiting question until it closes, and `/recall` puts each to
the person as a decision; its decision lands in its body when it closes* — or on a decision of
[q-0037.0010.0009.0009.0001](../questions/q-0037.0010.0009.0009.0001-how-does-what-a-pass-leaves-for-the-person-wait-in-the-wake-until-it-is-settled.md).

- **The mark is a part of an open question, written by a call of the store**, as every part is
  ([the questions mechanism](../../.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.md)); an id moves when
  its question is re-parented, and the store rewrites what names it.
- **Every wake shows every asking question until it closes** — no horizon, no bound (decision 2).
- **The wake's order stands**: what is most struck opens its read (ticket 01-0011.0100, its
  decision 54); the asking questions follow it.
- **A decision on another running session's question or unit is that session's to put** (S9): a
  question a running session holds, or stands on, or lies under the question or unit it stands on
  or holds, carries that session's tag in the section, and S9 does the rest.
- **A question still branching is put through its children** (the store's branching rule).
- **Scripts enumerate and decide nothing** ([Responsibilities](../architecture.md#responsibilities)).
- **Who leaves such a question is the next slice's**: the night pass, 01-0011.0110.0100. Until it
  lands, the part is written only by an `ask` call made in a session.

**Hidden edges.**

- **A part outside the store's list is dropped by the next write of its entry**, unread and
  unwritten; the part stands in the list, and a test runs every event of the store over an asking
  entry.
- **A merge or a supersession** carries the mark to its target when the target is open, keeping the
  earlier of the two dates where both ask; into a closed target it refuses while the closing
  question asks. Any other close ends the mark with the question.
- **A gate cannot tell the person's word.** The store cannot know who spoke; the rule is said where
  closing is taught, and the close's pointer names who decided, as every close's does.
- **`ask` on a question already asking** writes nothing and reports it, exit 0; on a closed one it
  refuses, exit 1.
- **The wake's section helper writes `(none)` under an empty heading**; this section is added only
  when a question asks.
- **After a compaction the hook hands over the window alone**, without the wake's sections; the
  asking questions come back at the next session's wake. Named, not changed here.
- **The section's lines** are ordered by date, then id.
- **The heading names the person**, not *you*: the wake is the agent's to read, and a question
  addressed to it would be settled by it.

**Leave alone.** The marks and dueness; the path and its horizon; *suspect*; the wake's order;

## Stages

Each stage ends green; the tests run, a script changing.

1. **The part, red first** in `test_questions.py`:
   - `ask` on an open question writes `asks the person, <today>`, and the check passes; on a
     closed one it refuses, exit 1, writing nothing; on one already asking it writes nothing,
     exit 0, and says so;
   - every event of the store run over an asking entry leaves the part standing, the moves and
     renames of its ancestors included;
   - `close … decided`, `pruned`, `moot` and `deferred` end it; `merged` and `superseded` carry it
     to an open target, the earlier date kept, and refuse into a closed one;
   - a closed entry carrying the part, and a value not of the form `the person, YYYY-MM-DD`, each
     fail the check;
   - the wake lists every asking question, more than any bound, after what is most struck, by
     date then id; one a running session holds, one it stands on, and one under the question it
     stands on or holds each carry its tag, and one under none of them carries none; with none
     asking, no heading.

   Then `questions.py`.
2. **The instructions**: the format, the calls table and *Closing*, Q2 and its authority, recall's
   line, the doc's wake and moment with the decisions landed; Q2 installed;
   `inject_rules.py --check` and `mechanisms.py --check` green.
3. **Read live**: a git clone, in the scratchpad, of the commit that carries stages 1 and 2, so no
   running session's wake shows a test question; in it, one open question on no copied session's
   position marked with `ask` by a session registered there for the purpose and standing elsewhere.
   A subagent with none of this session, told it is the session starting in the clone, takes its
   wake by the bare `--wake` its host would leave it to and reads the clone's own `/recall`, and
   writes its first report under it. A judge who
   did not build it reads whether the question is put to the person as a decision — a row of the
   reply's ⚖️ table. The question is then closed and the wake read again for no section.

Reversible, and the plan's, not the person's: a supersession carries the part as a merge does; a
merge or supersession into a closed target refuses while the closing question asks; where both
ask, the earlier date stands; the part's value carries its date.
