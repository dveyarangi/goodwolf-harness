## Governing boundaries

- **A wait is an open question**, its id kept for good, its decision landing in its body at the
  close, written by the store's calls only (`questions.py`; the store's rules in
  [the questions mechanism](../../.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.md)).
- **A question waiting on the person stays in the wake until it is closed** — no week's horizon,
  as the path's *touched lately* has (q-0037.0010.0009.0009.0001, decision 2).
- **The store already keeps a flag the wake shows until cleared**: *suspect*, set and cleared by
  its own calls, its section in every wake. The wait takes that form.
- **Scripts enumerate and decide nothing**
  ([Responsibilities](../architecture.md#responsibilities)): the store lists what waits, the agent
  puts it to the person.
- **`/maintain` already sends a finding it may not settle to an open question of the store**
  (its B2); a pass leaves its wait there, not in a second home.

## Impact

**Must change.**

  `.agents/skills/questions/SKILL.md`: the mark and its call.
- `.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.rules.md`: Q2, which tells `/recall` what to take from
  the wake, gains the waiting questions; reinstalled.
- `.agents/mechanisms/questions/questions.md`: the wake's read and the mark.
- `.agents/skills/maintain/SKILL.md`, B2: a finding at or above the dial that the pass may not put
  to a person present is opened and marked waiting.

**Must verify.** A wake with nothing waiting shows no waiting section; the store's check green;
the verification set green.

**Hidden edges.**

- **The wake's section helper writes `(none)` under an empty heading**; the waiting section is added
  only when a question waits, as the person asked of it.
- **No bound.** The section shows every waiting question, one line each: a wait hidden behind *and
  N more* is a wait that sinks. Its length is the person's backlog of decisions, which is the news.
- **A pass with a person present** puts its decision to them in the reply; it marks a question
  waiting only when the reply ends with the decision untaken, so the next wake does not ask again
  what was answered.
- **Deduplication** is the store's existing finding of two questions that may be one; a pass reads
  the waiting list before opening another.
- **The mark is the store's, so the rule into `/recall` is the questions mechanism's**, not the
  maintenance mechanism's.

**Leave alone.** The marks and dueness; the path and its horizon; *suspect* and its meaning.

## Shapes

An entry that waits:

```

```

The call, and its end:

```
questions.py wait q-N --session <tag>     # marks it; a closed question refuses
questions.py close q-N …                  # the question leaves the wake's section with its close
```

```
waiting on you, each to put as a decision:
  q-0037.0019.0004.0001 What is the written queue's role, now that the path is not read from it? (2026-10-26)
```

## Stages

Each stage ends green; the tests run, a script changing.

1. **The mark, red first** in `test_questions.py`: `wait` on an open question writes the mark and
   the check passes; on a closed one it refuses and writes nothing; a waiting question closed
   leaves the wake's section; the wake shows every waiting question, none hidden, after *suspect*;
   a store with none shows no heading; a hand-written mark on a closed entry is a finding of the
   check. Then `questions.py`.
2. **The instructions**: the format and calls table, the doc, Q2 in the rules file and installed,
   B2's sentence; `inject_rules.py --check` and `mechanisms.py --check` green.
3. **Read live**: in a scratch copy of the tree, so no running session's wake shows a test wait, a
   question is marked waiting; a subagent with none of this session, started there as a session,
   writes its first report under `/recall`; a judge who did not build it reads whether the wait is
   put to the person as a decision. The question is then closed and the wake read again for no
   section.
