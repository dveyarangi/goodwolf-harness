## Resolved at `/plan`

- **Relations are ids; the owner and the answer are links.** An id is placement-only and never a
  path (decision 24), so *part of*, *depends on*, *merged into* and *superseded by* are written
  as ids and resolved by the script over the live directory and `done/`. The owner and the answer
  are relative markdown links, and those are what the mover repairs when a subtree moves to
  `done/` (decision 34's *links repaired by the mover*).
- **A parent's answer changed** means the parent is `closed:superseded`. Decision 24 makes a
  reopening a new closure of kind *superseded* rather than a history on the entry, so it is the
  one way an answer changes that a script can see. The maintainer reports a closed child that is
  not suspect under such a parent.
- **A closed entry with an open dependency** is reported only if it is not suspect. Decision 33
  records the dependency when an answer is decided under an open question, and the entry is born
  suspect; reporting those as well would repeat what the entry already says.
- **The position is on an open question**, and `at` names it on every line but `nothing`. The
  close, branch and prune rules' *move the position* becomes the agent's `at`. The script refuses
  `at` on a closed entry instead of guessing a parent.
- **State `installed`**, as for the ticket mechanism, which also names nothing that retires it:
  install adds the parts and uninstall could remove them. Decision 41 is about retirement, and
  `always on` would mean nothing could ever install or remove it.
- **The window is the measured rendering**, the one in
  [the window test's generator](../research/window-test/gen.py) under `render_window`, with each
  line's state, kind, flags and lean added (decision 26), closed entries shown only on or under
  the path, and a last line naming the next free id. A change to the format reruns the test
  (evidence).
- **`/align`'s record-resolutions sentence becomes ticket's P11.** Decision 15 keeps recording
  resolutions ticket's and installed. The hand sentence under that heading, *sweep the ticket
  for internal consistency at the end*, becomes a rule in `ticket.rules.md` targeting `/align`, and
  the heading stays as the anchor R5, P7 and P10 already install under.
- **`debug=on` shows the working** *(the user, 2026-09-29; decision 25 amended)*. One sentence
  in the entry file, after the switch table: `debug` is `off` unless the project sets it; `on`
  shows each mechanism's working in the reply. Not a row of the table — it decides nothing about
  proceeding — and not per mechanism. This project sets it in `local.rules.md` as L4. Q1 shows
  the declared line only under `debug=on`; the `--declare` call is made either way.
- **A mid-turn declaration shows where it happened** (under `debug=on`). The user's amendment
  to decision 39 lets the agent call `--declare` again during the turn. So that such a write can
  be corrected in the same turn, as decision 25 wants for the first line, the reply quotes each
  mid-turn line at the point in the reply where the event is reported.
- **Ids are `q-` and at least four digits**, growing past four digits rather than overflowing
  (decision 34 projects five to six figures). The slug is taken from the question's words.

## The store

`docs/questions/` is a painted door: `docs/questions/`, `docs/questions/done/` and
`docs/questions/position` join `PAINTED_DOORS` in `mechanisms.py`, under a `TODO` bound to
01-0017.0010, the same as the ticket records. `docs/concerns.md` leaves the list when the fold
lands.

**An entry**, `docs/questions/q-0041-which-package-manager.md`:

```md
# q-0041 Which package manager do we use?

- **part of** q-0012
- **depends on** q-0007, q-0009
- **state** open, suspect
- **owner** [01-0011.0100](../tickets/01-0011.0100-the-open-questions-are-kept-by-a-mechanism.md)
- **answer** [decision 24](../tickets/01-0011.0100-the-open-questions-are-kept-by-a-mechanism.md#decisions-landed-at-this-tickets-align) — the user, 2026-09-28
- **lean** the flat directory, for insertion cost
```

The title line carries the id and the question. Every bullet is optional except **state**. The
state is `open` or `closed:<kind>`, where the kind is one of `decided`, `pruned`, `merged`,
`deferred`, `moot` or `superseded`, and it may carry `, suspect` after it. What the answer
carries depends on the kind: a link with provenance for *decided*; `until <condition>, meanwhile
<default>` for *deferred*; an id for *merged* and *superseded*; a reason for *pruned* and *moot*.
The format is declared as a record in the skill with its three fields (MECHANISM-FORMAT §
Records). What an entry is: one file. Its tier: read by the script, and by a person through
`/questions`. What removes one: it moves to `done/` with its wholly closed subtree, and is never
deleted.

**The position**, `docs/questions/position`: one line of ids, the current question first and
then up to four recent positions. Only the script writes it. With no file, the window says so,
and the wake falls back to the delivery status's current step (decision 39).

## The declared line

One line with clauses separated by `; `. Free text in a clause (a question, a reason, a
condition) may not contain `;`, and the parser refuses a line where it does.

| clause | writes |
|---|---|
| `at q-N` | the position; required on every line except `nothing`, and q-N must be open |
| `opens q-N: <question>` · `opens q-N under q-M: <question>` | a new open entry, a root or under q-M; q-N must be the next free id |
| `opens q-N between q-M and q-K: <question>` | the new entry under q-M, with q-K re-parented under it; q-K must be part of q-M |
| `moves q-K under q-M` · `moves q-K to root` | re-parents q-K; refused if it would make a cycle |
| `depends q-A on q-B` | adds q-B to q-A's *depends on* |
| `closes q-N: <kind>[, <pointer>]` | the state and the answer, in the form above for each kind |
| `suspects q-N` · `clears q-N` | sets or removes the suspect flag |
| `leans q-N: <line>` | the lean |
| `assigns q-N to <path>` | the owner |
| `nothing` | a turn that places nothing; the position is left as it is |

A prune that would leave an open child under the pruned question is refused. The children that
stand on their own are moved out first with `moves` in the same line, and the rest are closed
(decision 37). The whole line is validated against the store before anything is written, the
entries are written first and the position last, and a refusal writes nothing.

## The script — `questions.py`

`.agents/scripts/gw/questions.py`, standard library only, reusing `docs_corpus.py` for citations.
Modes, each named, and a bare call refuses:

- `--window` prints the window from the position; with no store or no position it prints the
  fallback notice and exits 0.
- `--tree [q-N]` prints the live tree, whole or under q-N: the index of decision 34, rendered and
  never committed.
- `--declare "<line>"` parses the line and writes it. It is the one writer, for the agent now and
  for the judge's typed value later.
- `--check` is the maintainer. It reports **diagnostics**, which fail the run: a malformed entry;
  an orphan, meaning a relation that names a missing id; a cycle in *part of*; a closed child that
  is not suspect under a superseded parent; a closed entry that is not suspect with an open
  dependency; a position that is malformed or names a missing or closed entry; a filename whose id
  or slug disagrees with its title. It reports **findings**, which do not fail the run: likely
  twins, meaning title pairs that share enough content words; and wholly closed subtrees that
  nothing open depends on and that are ready for `done/`. It writes nothing, and a conforming
  store stays byte-identical.

## Stages

Each stage keeps the suite green; the suite runs at every stage's end.

1. **Reader and maintainer.** `questions.py --check` over a store read from disk. Tests cover each
   diagnostic in both polarities; each finding, and that findings leave the exit code at 0; a
   conforming store left byte-identical; a store with no directory. `PAINTED_DOORS` gains the
   store. `repository.py`'s guard is extended from the live marks to `docs/questions/`, so a
   suite run that changes the live store fails the case that did it.
2. **The window and the tree.** Tests over a fixed store: path, frontier, the current root's open
   titles, one line per other root, recents, closed entries only on or under the path, a suspect
   under the path, the next free id; and the fallback without a position.
3. **The writer.** `--declare`. One test per clause writes and reads back. Refusals each name
   their reason and leave the store byte-identical: a malformed line, a `;` in free text, an id
   that is not the next one, `at` on a closed entry, a between whose lower is not part of the
   upper, a cycle, a prune that leaves an open child, a kind without its pointer. A round trip
   opens, attaches, closes and prunes, then reads back through `--check` clean.
4. **The declaration.** The doc, the skill, the rules file, the evidence and the architecture
   surface. The recall and conclude anchors are added, and the four blocks and
   P11 installed. The entry contract goes to v18, with the `debug` sentence. `mechanisms.py
   --check` and `inject_rules.py --check` are clean. The questions check joins the local
   verification set (`local.rules.md` L3), and `debug=on` is set as L4; both reinstalled.
5. **The fold**, in the order under *The fold of `concerns.md`*: the four entries through
   `--declare` and `questions.py --check` clean over them; the repointed lines; last,
   `concerns.md` deleted and its door row removed, with `mechanisms.py --check` showing no leak.
6. **The strip.** `/align` per the table above. `straw_dogs.py` lists every new wrapper as bound.
7. **`/verify`** against the ticket, this RFC and the governing docs.

Q1 goes live in stage 4 but reaches a session only at its next start. The implementing session
works under the old contract, and the first session after it is the first live use.
