## Settled while planning

- **A missing parent is opened and its children moved under it, in the turn** *(the user,
  2026-10-05; ticket decision 8)*. Opening it is Q1's second kind, a new question under the
  lowest one that contains the message, so Q1 needs no sentence of its own for it. Whether the
  grouping is right and worth the renames is the agent's judgement; the renames are the script's
  work. A grouping the agent cannot settle is uncharted. The test for a missing parent, the
  moves and what the reply says of them are the skill's.
- **The wake places, and shows its process** *(the user, 2026-10-05)*. Q2 is unchanged: a session needs a
  position for its window to be drawn around, and the wake is where one is taken from the person
  or the queue. The wake's reply shows `▶ /recall`, not that question, since no message landed on
  it. Decision 5's *stored position left as it was* is about a position already held.
- **The hook's line fills in no id.** Naming the stored id is what offered the stale position.
- **Uncharted is a glossary term; process is not.** *Process* is used in its plain sense and the
  rule says what it means where it is read.

  > - **On a question**: call `at` on the lowest question that contains the message. One that
  >   only resembles it is not its home; from a question that does not contain it, go up.

  >   and open nothing. Not found, `open` it under the lowest question that contains it, and
  >   call `at` on it.
