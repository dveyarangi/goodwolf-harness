# Every record shows what it is a record of — implementation plan

- **record of** what it intends to become

**Authored:** 2026-10-10
**Last amended:** 2026-10-10

Implements [01-0011.0110.0040.0010 every-record-shows-what-it-is-a-record-of](../tickets/01-0011.0110.0040.0010-every-record-shows-what-it-is-a-record-of.md).

## What it changes

The [spec](../spec/01-0011.0110-every-written-thing-has-a-source-of-truth.md) has every record an
agent edits carry its kind on itself, so the editing agent meets the kind in context; the method
glossary's *Kind of record* holds the rule. `.0030` gave the six mechanism docs the mark as
`- **kind** what must always hold`. This slice renames the mark to `- **record of** <kind>`
*(the user, 2026-10-08)*, gives it to the ticket, the queue, the question entry and the RFC, and
has each check hold the mark equal to its format's kind — reporting one that is missing, of
another kind, or the old `- **kind**`.

`/impact`: the parent's split impact, 2026-10-08; nothing here exceeds it but the decisions below.

## Decisions taken at this plan

*(the user, 2026-10-10)*

1. **Every live record is marked in this slice, and the checks are strict at once.** The
   ticket's *next touch by `/maintain`* is replaced: a check cannot tell an old unmarked record
   from a new one, so the *none is reported* criterion would go unproven. Tickets, the queue, RFCs
   and mechanism docs are marked with the edit tools; question entries are re-rendered by
   `questions.py`'s own writer in a one-off call, uncommitted — the script is the only writer of
   an entry's parts.
2. **A record that closes into `done/` is marked `what happened`** — the glossary's *Archived
   record*. A question entry's mark already turns so at close; the ticket mechanism's output
   becomes *what it intends to become, what happened once closed*.
3. **The mover writes that mark.** P3 gives the header to the maintainer because it holds
   judgement — the status, the date, the boxes. The mark holds none: it follows from the shelf the
   record lands on, and the mover is what lands it there, as `questions.py close` does for an
   entry. Written by the mover it cannot be forgotten; nothing checks an archived record. The
   mover's docstring names the exception; P3 stays as it was, the mark being none of the
   checkbox, status, date or prose it keeps the mover from *(the user, 2026-10-10, at the
   implementation)*.
4. **Edge records and records without a format are not this slice's** — `docs/edge/` and the
   architecture, glossary, spec, research and session records. They wait on
   [q-0024.0009.0003.0001.0001](../questions/q-0024.0009.0003.0001.0001-how-do-the-records-outside-the-ticket-the-queue-the-question-entry-the-rfc-and-the-mechanism-doc-show-what-they-are-a-record-of.md).

## Where the mark sits, and what holds it

| Record | Where | Kind | Held by | Said by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| mechanism doc | the header bullet after the title | what must always hold | `mechanisms.py --check` | `MECHANISM-FORMAT.md` |
| ticket | the header's first line, before `Status` | what it intends to become | `tickets.py --check` | `TICKET-FORMAT.md`, *The record* and *The header* |
| the queue | the line after its title | what it intends to become | `tickets.py --check` | `TICKET-FORMAT.md`, *The queue*; `QUEUE-ARRIVAL.md` |
| question entry | the first part | open: what it intends to become; closed: what happened | `questions.py --check` | the questions skill, *Records* |
| RFC | the line after its title | what it intends to become | nothing, until `/plan` is a mechanism | `/plan`'s RFC-header rule |

- The mark has no colon; a ticket's fields carry one inside the bold. That is what tells the mark
  from the retired `- **Kind:**`, which stays refused as it is.
- An old `- **kind**` bullet is reported as itself in each check, not passed over and not taken
  for the end of a header.
- Each script holds its kinds as constants, as `.0030`'s `DOC_KIND` does; no script reads
  another mechanism's doc.
- Archived records stay exempt from every check.

## Stages

Red first, each stage green at its end. Before each, `git status`: other sessions work in this
tree, and only this slice's paths are staged.

**Stage 1 — the mechanism doc.** Tests: the renamed mark is clean; a missing one, another kind,
and the old `- **kind**` are reported, each. `mechanisms.py` reads `record of`, reports the old
bullet as itself, and names the new mark in its messages. `MECHANISM-FORMAT.md`'s template and
header-bullet definition; the six docs.

**Stage 2 — the ticket and the queue.** Tests, each polarity: a marked ticket is clean; none,
another kind, the old `- **kind**` are reported; `- **Kind:**` is still refused and
`- **record of**` is not; the queue marked is clean, unmarked or of another kind reported.
`tickets.py` reads the header's first line as the mark when it has the mark's form, the fields
after it, and holds the queue's mark when the queue exists — a missing queue stays unreported.
`TICKET-FORMAT.md` says the kind and where the mark sits, for the ticket and the queue; the
ticket mechanism's doc lists *The queue* again as an output, and the tickets as *what it intends
to become, what happened once closed*; `QUEUE-ARRIVAL.md`'s fresh queue carries the mark. The live
tickets and the queue are marked.

**Stage 3 — the question entry.** Tests: `open` writes the open kind; `close` turns it to
`what happened`; every rewrite writes the kind the state calls for; the check reports none, the
wrong kind for the state, and the old `kind`. `questions.py` puts `record of` first among the
parts and writes it from the state on every write; the questions skill's *Records* says so. Every
live entry is re-rendered through the writer.

**Stage 4 — the close.** Tests: a pair moved into `docs/tickets/done/` and `docs/rfc/done/`
leaves marked `what happened`; an unmarked record moves unchanged; nothing else in a header
changes. `move_doc.py` rewrites the mark of a record landing on a `done/` shelf, and its
postcondition holds it; its docstring names the exception.

**Stage 5 — the RFC.** `/plan`'s RFC-header rule places the mark after the title — mechanism
work, its text shown first. The live RFCs carry the mark.

**Stage 6 — the edge.** The rename and the new marks fail a recipient's `shape` and `tickets`
gates at its next update. The recipient-facing line in
[q-0018.0020.0002.0001](../questions/q-0018.0020.0002.0001-how-does-an-edge-change-get-its-line-in-the-edge-record-when-it-is-made.md)'s
body is rewritten: every mechanism doc, live ticket, the queue and every live question entry
carries `- **record of** <kind>`, the entries re-rendered by `questions.py`.
