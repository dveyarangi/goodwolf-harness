## Governing boundaries

- **A rule lives in its rules file and reaches a file as an installed block**, a group's members
  included ([Installed blocks](../architecture.md#installed-blocks)).
- **Settings are closed**: a setting's values are enumerated by the mechanism that declares it, and
  a rule is installed or not by `when` — the rules' text is never filled in from a value
  (`inject_rules.py`, the declarations table; [Installed blocks](../architecture.md#installed-blocks)).
- **A project's own answers live in its local file** (R7); a project fact never stands in core
  (L10, the leak check).
- **A mechanism has an instruction file and declares each output's kind and reader**
  ([MECHANISM-FORMAT.md](../../.agents/skills/mechanism/MECHANISM-FORMAT.md)); a skill opens with
  its mechanism (R6).
- **What is said at a moment is the speaking skill's**; how it is said is the voice's; records are
  not the voice's (q-0037.0009.0021.0002, decisions 3 and 5).

## Raised for `/align`

1. **The form of the voice's value.** Decision 4 says the value is set in the local file *as `debug`
   is*. A setting's values are closed and a value fills no rule's text, so a setting cannot carry an
   open name like *Lem* without growing the grammar an open value and the installer a templating of
   rule bodies — new architecture for one value. **Recommended:** the name is the project's local
   rule, L9 shrunk to it, installed into the entry file as now; the voice's own rule points at it —
   *the voice the project names in its local block*. No grammar, no templating; a recipient names
   its voice the way it writes any local answer.
2. **Which skills speak.** Decision 7 pairs a mechanism's output the person hears with its skill's
   section. The format's output kinds are the four kinds of record, and *the person reads* today
   covers JSON of a failed check and the product document alike — no reader line tells speech from a
   record. **Recommended:** a fifth kind, *said*, for an output heard by the person in a session,
   its bullet naming the skill it is said from; the check holds each *said* output's skill to its
   section and each section to a *said* output. Declared *said*: `/recall` and `/conclude`
   (session), `/maintain`, `/harness`, `/ticket` (the breakdown put to the person) and `/questions`
   (a decision settled and reported, an uncharted question asked). `/ticket` and `/questions`
   then take a section; `/tdd`, a skill of no mechanism yet, is out of the check's reach.
3. **L9's other clauses.** It also says speech invents nothing, what it tells being what happened,
   and that tables and texts for a record keep their forms' exactness. **Recommended:** both stay in
   L9 beside the name; its description of the voice and its *never agent-speak* go.
4. **A tree that names no voice.** **Recommended:** nothing is asked at install — one more question
   to a person meeting the harness. The voice's rule reads *the voice the project names in its local
   block, where it names one*; with none, the model speaks as it does.

*A reply speaks the method*, the general what of a reply, stays where it is, bound to
q-0037.0010.0012: decision 3 left its home open, and nothing here needs it moved.

**Hidden edges.**

- **`/harness`'s section is long and speaks for itself** — the three troubles and the ladder, the
  speech bench's near word-for-word replies. The voice's block lands after it; whether the section's
  text keeps its own wording is the skill's, not this ticket's: what is said at a moment stays the

  names its skill, so the pair is per skill, not per mechanism.
- **The leak check.** No text of core may name Lem; the rewrite of `MEETING.md` and the voice's

**Leave alone.** Debugging and its blocks; each skill's own wording of what it says; records and

## Stages

1. **The kind *said* and the pair check, red first** — in `test_mechanisms.py`: a *said* output whose
   skill holds no section is a diagnostic; a section with no *said* output naming its skill is one;
   a matched pair passes; a *said* bullet naming no skill is one. Then the format's paragraph and the
   check.
2. **The declarations** — the *said* outputs in five docs, the two new sections; the check green.
3. **The voice mechanism** — doc, rules file, skill; installed; the entry file and every section
   carry its block.
4. **The rewrite** — L9; the fourteen straw dogs, their wrappers removed; `ticket` and `local`
   reinstalled; the contract bumped with its findings line.
5. **Read live** — the speech bench's five moments rerun, two runs each, read by a judge who did not
   build it against the question *does this read in the named voice*; an install into an empty
   folder read for criterion 4.
