## Governing boundaries

- **The path is a reading, never a record.** It is computed from what the records already hold
  and written nowhere; two sessions compute one path (q-0037.0019, `Where it stands`). The
  architecture's rule on scripts holds it: they enumerate and transform, and decide nothing
  ([Responsibilities](../architecture.md#responsibilities)).
- **The order is the person's; the path re-ranks nothing** (q-0037.0019 decision 1; `/recall`'s
  rule, *read the next item from the order the queue states*). The path shows candidates and
  their reasons; it does not say which is first.
- **The cap's number is the local file's** (L12); no script reads it. The path counts; the agent,
  who reads the entry file, compares.
- **Sessions' positions are not work** until the ring mark exists (q-0037.0019.0001, deferred); the
  count reads tickets In progress and says so. A ticket In progress counts only where no ticket
  under it is In progress: a parent is in progress because a child is, and counted once, through the
  child.
- **The pathfinder is undeclared.** Its instruction into `/recall` and its moment in the store's
  doc are written by hand as straw dogs bound to q-0037.0019, as R5 has it for an undeclared
  mechanism; when it is declared, they become its installed blocks.
- **Every record stays as it is.** No new part of a question, no new field of a ticket, no new
  file: the path reads `owner`, `realizes`, `struck`, `depends on`, the tickets' `Status` and
  `Answers`, the product document's answers, and the straw dogs' bindings.

**Must verify.**

**Hidden edges.**

- **Circular import.** `tickets.py` and `product.py` import `questions`; the path imports them
  inside its function. A module of its own would be cleaner and is a new part of a mechanism to
  declare; not for this slice.
- **What the store can say of a new idea is when it was last touched.** It records a question's
  last strike, stamped at its opening or when it is reached again, and no boundary. The preview
  showed what that reads: an old question struck yesterday stands beside one opened yesterday. So
  the line says what it reads — *touched lately, no ticket* — the open questions with no owner on
  them or above them, stamped within the sessions' own horizon of seven days, the constant the
  session mechanism already keeps; the reading of *since the last boundary* is slice 3's and
  q-0037.0019.0001's, marked `TODO q-0037.0019.0001` at the line.
- **The roots are no candidates.** The preview put two roots among the touched and the most
  struck: a root is struck whenever its tree is. Roots are counted under *roots* and nowhere else.
- **The queue's order is not machine-readable** — see *Raised for `/align`*.
- **A recipient's path** reads the same records; one without a product document has every reason
  for the answer *unread*, which is the truth of that tree.

**Leave alone.** The queue's prose; `product.py --queue`, which renders coverage per item for the
person ordering and stays; the window and the rest of the wake; the dials and L12's number; the
store's grammar.

## Raised for `/align`

The ticket format says a position *changes when priority changes*; the queue's order is kept in
the README's prose and has moved without renumbering — `.0175` pulled forward on 2026-10-14,
01-0060.0010 put ahead of it on 2026-10-23, both by prose. So the positions do not encode the
order, and a path reading positions as the order would show `.0175` first. This slice does not
read an order at all: candidates are shown as a set with their reasons, in id order as every
listing is, and the order stays the queue's prose, read by `/recall` as today. Whether the
positions should follow the order again, or the format's sentence should go, is the pathfinder's
later slices' — the picture on a time axis — and is put to the person there, not here.

## Taken at `/plan`

1. **The path lives in the store's script, as a reading beside the wake.** Not a module of its own:
   that is a new part to declare, and the pathfinder has no mechanism yet; not in `product.py`:
   the path reads four mechanisms' records and the store is where the wake already is.
2. **Candidates are the live tickets whose Status is Ready, a handful at most**, in id order, plus
   the most struck open questions with no owner, a handful at most. Not: every live ticket — the
   path is read at every start; not: an order — the person's.
3. **A reason the records do not hold is written `unread`**, never inferred. The reasons: the
   answer served and whether it is present, from the trace link and the product document; the
   strikes; the straw dogs bound to the question or under it; what depends on it.

A question is *with a ticket* when it or a question above it is owned by a live ticket — part of
that ticket's work. A root is *thin* when it has open questions and none with a ticket. *in progress* / *present* /
*absent* is the product script's coverage word for the answer; *unread* where the question has no
trace link or the tree no product document. The counts are integers the agent compares with L12.

## Stages

Each stage ends green on the verification set; the tests run, a script changing.

1. **The reading, red first** in `test_questions.py`, a case class with a store, two tickets and a
   product document in the fixture:
   - a ticket In progress is counted and named with its question; a Ready one is not; a parent In
     progress whose child is In progress is not counted, the child is;
   - an open question with no owner on it or above it, stamped within seven days, is *touched
     lately*; an owned, an old or a root one is not;
   - a Ready ticket is a candidate with its four reasons read; a reason the records lack reads
     `unread`;
   - the most struck open question with no ticket is a candidate too; a root never is;
   - each root's open and ticketed counts, and *thin* where ticketed is zero and open is not;
   - a tree with no tickets folder and no product document prints the roots and `unread`, and
     exits 0.

   Then `--path` and the section in the wake, bounded by two constants beside
   `WAKE_STRUCK_LINES`.
2. **The wake carries it**: a test that the wake's text holds the `path:` section after
   *suspect*; the hook unchanged, since it prints the wake.
3. **The telling and the moment**: the sentence in `/recall` and the row in the store's doc, each a
   straw dog bound to q-0037.0019; `mechanisms.py --check` and `inject_rules.py --check` green.
4. **Read live** (criteria 4 and 5): a `/recall` in this tree and a freeform *where should we go*,
   read by a judge who did not build it — an agent with none of this session, given the reply
   and asked what is next and why; an install into an empty folder read for criterion 6.
