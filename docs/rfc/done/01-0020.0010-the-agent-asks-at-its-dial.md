# The agent asks at its dial — implementation plan

- **record of** what happened

**Authored:** 2026-10-15

Implements [01-0020.0010](../../tickets/done/01-0020.0010-the-agent-asks-at-its-dial.md). The five switches give
way to a dial on a ladder. The entry file states the dial's meaning, and the two known ladders stand
beside it as straw dogs of their mechanisms. Every switch reader reads the dial. A question carries
its highest rung. The install shows the ladders as lists of acts.

**Planning state:** authored 2026-10-15, after the align that settled the question's open children
and the person's acceptance of its `Where it stands`. It was planned against
[q-0037.0013.0005](../../questions/q-0037.0013.0005-which-of-the-agents-acts-need-the-persons-yes-and-which-does-it-do-on-its-own.md)
decisions 1–13, every file that names a switch or the repair policy, and `questions.py`'s parts. It
has been validated once. Five choices were taken here and are listed under *Taken at `/plan`*.

## Governing boundaries

- **The dial and its meaning are core.** Decisions 1, 10 and 11 hold the meaning. A ladder's rungs
  belong to its mechanism (decision 9): development's to the practice mechanism
  ([q-0037.0010.0002.0005](../../questions/q-0037.0010.0002.0005-what-does-the-practice-mechanism-own.md)),
  self-improvement's to self-amendment ([q-0037.0012.0001](../../questions/q-0037.0012.0001-is-harness-self-amendment-its-own-mechanism.md)).
  Both mechanisms are undeclared, so each ladder is written as a straw dog bound to its question.
- **Commit and push are no rungs.** Decision 7: work closes with its commit; a push follows the
  project's way of working with git. That way is the environment's to propose
  ([q-0037.0006.0022.0003](../../questions/q-0037.0006.0022.0003-how-does-the-environments-skill-fit-a-projects-way-of-working-to-its-requirements-set.md),
  open).
- **Repair is no rung.** Decision 6: mending is the work of the rung the mended thing is on.
  *Repair it under the repair policy*, in five rules files, goes with the switch.
- **The dial is out of the agent's reach.** Decision 13: the agent never writes its dial, and the
  host hook is built by [01-0010.0125](../../tickets/01-0010.0125-the-host-blocks-what-a-rule-forbids.md).
  So this slice's own rewrite of this tree's dial line is the person's to approve word for word.
- **The install shows acts, not rung names** (decision 12). It reads each rung's line and example
  where the ladder declares them.
- **The question store's parts are written by the script** ([QUESTION-FORMAT.md](../../../.agents/skills/questions/QUESTION-FORMAT.md#an-entry)).
  The rung part therefore needs a store call.
- **Rules reach a skill as installed blocks** (R7, R5). A rules file's text changes at its source
  and is reinstalled.
- **Out of scope by the ticket:** the reply's report by rung (01-0020.0020), recipients' switches
  (01-0010.0210), self-improvement following its dial (01-0019.0010), and the host's check
  (01-0010.0125).

- **Writing this tree's dial is writing the dial.** Under decision 13, `/implement` cannot rewrite

## Recommendation

**Proceed.** Most of the change is a substitution across eighteen files. Its parts are already
decided: no switch survives, and the one new part, `rung`, is decision 11's. The work is wide but
not deep. The entry file holds all of the meaning, so every reader points to one place.

## Taken at `/plan`

1. **The ladders stand in the entry file, one column each.** A rung's description *is* the line
   the person is shown, with its example. Every decision the agent takes is graded on these rungs,
   so their occasion is every session, and they belong at tier 1. The install reads the same text.
   Not: separate lines for the agent and for the person. The person's lines grade decisions just as
   well and are plainer.
2. **Repair keeps no conditions of its own.** The four conditions of 2026-09-05 decided when the
   agent may mend alone, and the rung now decides that (decision 6). *Repair it under the repair
   policy* becomes *repair it at the rung of what it mends*.
3. **Commit and push.** The entry file says work closes with its commit once `/verify` passes on
   it, and a push follows the project's way of working with git, as its local file states.
   - This tree's way is one local rule, *push each commit as it lands*, which is what `push=auto`
     did.
   - The install asks for the person's way with git in their words and writes it. That clause is a
     straw dog bound to q-0037.0006.0022.0003.
4. **Taking the next ticket follows decision 5.** It stands on the rung of what the ticket
   decides: its question's highest rung. A ticket at or above the dial is shown before it is
   started. `/recall` and `/maintain`'s handover read it so, and no `next-cycle` remains.
5. **One store call writes the rung.** `questions.py rung q-N '<ladder>: <rung>[, …]'` writes the
   part whole, as `realize` writes the trace link. It is called right after `open`, and again
   whenever a decision raises the question. Not: an `--rung` flag on `open` as well. That would be
   two ways to write one part.

## Shapes

> 🧩 **Proposed rule — AGENTS.md, *Autonomy*** (replacing the table, its preamble and the four conditions)
>
> Autonomy is a dial on a ladder. A method whose decisions differ in how far their consequences
> reach has a ladder, its rungs ordered by that reach and declared by the method's mechanism; a
> method without one states which of its decisions are the agent's and which the person's. A
> decision's rung is read from what it decides, never from the record that holds it; a case between
> two rungs takes the higher.
>
> - 🤖 Below the dial the agent settles and acts on its own.
> - ✋ At the dial it settles, and shows the person before acting.
> - 💬 Above it, it settles nothing: it brings the options and a recommendation, and the person
>   decides.
>
> A question carries its highest rung on each ladder it touches and stops for the person while
> that rung is at or above the ladder's dial. Mending a violation is the work of the rung the
> mended thing is on. Work closes with its commit once `/verify` passes on it; a push follows the
> project's way of working with git, as its local file states.
>
> The project sets each dial in its local block; an unset dial is at the lowest rung. The agent
> never writes its dial⟦, and the host refuses an edit that changes it⟧₁.
>
> ⟦Development's ladder, lowest first:
>
> | rung | a decision on it — as the person is shown it |
> |---|---|
> | code | writing the code inside agreed work — renaming a function, fixing a bug |
> | a feature | what one piece of work should deliver — a CSV export: which columns, what of empty cells |
> | load-bearing | a load-bearing architectural decision — a data format, a shared API |
> | a product slice | what gets built next |
> | the whole product | what the project is for |⟧₂
>
> ⟦Self-improvement's ladder, lowest first:
>
> | rung | a decision on it — as the person is shown it |
> |---|---|
> | a wording | rewording an instruction, its meaning unchanged |
> | a script | fixing a script that carries an instruction out |
> | a rule | changing an instruction or a skill |
> | the method | changing the way of working |⟧₃

₁ is a straw dog bound to [q-0037.0006.0005](../../questions/q-0037.0006.0005-which-forbidden-actions-does-the-host-refuse-instead-of-trusting-the-agent.md),
₂ one bound to q-0037.0010.0002.0005, and ₃ one bound to q-0037.0012.0001.

> 🧩 **Proposed rule — AGENTS.md, *The loop***
>
> Human checkpoints: every decision at /align; a question's `Where it stands` accepted; and
> whatever stands at or above its ladder's dial.

> 🧩 **Proposed rule — local.rules.md, L2** (yours to approve word for word: decision 13)
>
> **L2** development=a feature · self-improvement=a rule. Push each commit as it lands.

**The rung part.** It is added to `PARTS` after `realizes`. Its value is one or more
`<ladder>: <rung>` items, comma-separated, each ladder named once. The form check reports anything
else as a diagnostic. A `TODO q-0037.0010.0002.0005` marks that names and order are not checked
until a ladder's mechanism declares them.

**The skills,** each reading the entry file's *Autonomy* and naming no switch:

- `/questions`, *Who settles what is not found*: a decision below its ladder's dial is settled in
  the turn and reported; at the dial, settled and shown before it lands; above, brought to the
  person or opened. A question opened is given its rung by the store's call, and the rung is raised
  when a higher decision turns up. The agent never lowers it.
- `/ticket`: a split stands on the rung of what its tickets decide. Shown at or above the dial,
  minted below it.
- `/commit`: commit when the work closes; push as the local file states the project's way with git.
- `/conclude`: the session's unpushed commits follow that way. The push switch and its *no other
  reply* rule go.
- `/implement` and `/verify`: commit as `/commit` says, and mend at the rung of what is mended.
- `/harness`: the install writes the dials and the way with git. Its introduction shows each ladder
  as the entry file's lines, numbered and marked 🤖 ✋ 💬 at the proposed dial, and the person names
  the line for ✋.

## Stages

Each stage ends green on the verification set.

1. **The rung part.** Red first in `test_questions.py`:
   - the call writes the part;
   - a second call replaces it;
   - a value out of form is refused and writes nothing;
   - a hand-written part out of form is a diagnostic;
   - a well-formed part on an archived entry passes.

   Then the part, its form check, and the `rung` call with its usage line.
2. **The entry file and the glossary.** *Autonomy*, *The loop*'s checkpoints line and the reply
   rule's `🛠️` are rewritten as above. *Autonomy switch* gives way to **Dial**, **Ladder** and
   **Rung**. *Authority* is reworded. The contract goes to v92, with its findings line.
3. **The rules files.** *Under the repair policy* becomes *at the rung of what it mends* in the
   four rules files, each reinstalled with `--overwrite`. `/maintain` line 62 is changed by hand.
   `inject_rules.py --check` stays green.
4. **The skills, under `/skill-up`:** `/questions` (and `QUESTION-FORMAT.md`'s `rung`), `/ticket`,
   `/commit`, `/conclude`, `/implement`, `/verify` and `/harness`. Then the ticket and maintain
   mechanism docs and `move_doc.py`'s docstring. A search for `next-cycle`, `breakdown=`,
   `commit=`, `push=`, `repair=`, `repair policy` and `switch` over `.agents/` and `AGENTS.md`
   finds nothing but git's own `switch`.
5. **This tree.** L2 lands on your yes. Then `docs/process.md`, and A13 and A14 with their lines
   through `/product`. The product check stays green.
6. **The judgement cases** (criterion 2). Ten decisions from this tree's records go to the person
   for their rungs: today's five, three from the queue's history and two closures. They are given
   to an agent with none of this session, which places them, and the two placements are compared.
   The case set and the result are kept as dated evidence in q-0037.0013.0005. A mismatch on a
   rung's line is a wording finding for that ladder's line, not a pass.
7. **The install trial** (criterion 3). An install into an empty folder, by a fresh agent. Its
   introduction is read for the two lists, their marks and the question it asks.
