## What it changes

`product.py --queue` renders, for the person ordering the work, two things beside the queue and
never in it ([q-0037.0018](../../questions/q-0037.0018-what-is-a-projects-product-document-to-the-method-and-which-of-the-methods-mechanisms-keeps-it-and-the-projects-work-in-agreement-both-ways.md),
decision 7): what each item realizes, and the answers left out, by pain and by goal. The order
stays the person's; nothing is ranked.

## Shape

**An item** is a live ticket — under `docs/tickets/`, not in `done/` — that owns an open question:
the store's `owner` part names it. What it realizes is that question's trace link as
`questions.realizes` reads it, its own or its nearest ancestor's. The ticket's own `Answers` field
says the same and is held to agree by the ticket check, so the product mechanism reads the store it
already relies on and never the ticket mechanism's reader. Items come in ticket-id order, the order
`tickets.py --list` gives, parents before children.

**Left out** is every answer whose coverage is not *present* and that no item realizes. An answer
not yet decided is among them, marked so: nothing can build it before its purpose question is
decided. Each is listed under every pain and goal its row marks with `●`, so an answer serving
three pains is seen three times; one marking none is listed under *serving no stated pain or goal*.

**Pains and goals by their names.** The answers table's header names each pain column by the pain's
number and each goal column by its `G<n>`, as the format's example does; the document's numbered
list of pains and its goals' bold leads give their words. A column the document does not name is
shown by its header alone.

**Output**, plain text in the shape `--coverage` has: one line per item —
`<ticket id and slug> — <question id>: <answers>`, the answers as `A7 <name>`, or `derived`, or
`no trace link` — then one block per pain, per goal, and for none, each answer a line under it with
its coverage. Rendered on request, never committed; `--queue` writes nothing, like `--coverage`.

**Hidden edges.**
- **An item's link may come from an ancestor.** A ticket whose question has no link of its own
  shows what its nearest linked ancestor realizes — trace propagation, as coverage reads it. The
  line shows the answer, not where it was inherited from.
- **A ticket with no open owned question** — its question closed while the ticket is still live —
  is no item and is not listed; the ticket check, not this view, says whether that is drift.
- **Undecided rows are always left out.** In this tree A15, A21, A51, A52 appear under their pains
  at every render until their purpose questions are decided — that is the information, not noise.

## Stages

Each test-first, each green.

1. **Items.** Over a fixture tree — two live tickets and one in `done/`, their questions owned, one
   linked, one inheriting from a parent, one derived — `--queue` lists the two live ones in id
   order with what each realizes, and not the done one.
2. **Left out.** Over the same tree with answers marking pains and goals: an answer realized by an
   item is absent; one realized only by a question no ticket owns is listed; a present one is not;
   an undecided one is listed and marked; an answer marking two pains appears under both; one
   marking none under *serving no stated pain or goal*. Names come from the document; a column it
   does not name shows its header.
3. **Nothing written.** The tree's bytes are the same after `--queue`.
4. **Docs.** `/product`, the product doc; this tree's `--queue` read and its first lines recorded

## Found while building, 2026-10-14

- **An item is a ticket, not each question it owns.** The first render over this tree listed a
  ticket once per owned question — 01-0030 six times — since a ticket owns its sub-questions too.
  An item is now one line per ticket: its topmost owned questions, those no question of its own
  holds, and the answers all its owned questions realize, each once. The test was changed first
  and seen to fail.
- **One rule of *present*.** Coverage and the queue's left-out list read it from one function.
- **This tree, the first render**: 37 live tickets with an owned open question; left out under the
  three pains and both goals, 24 answers in all, four of them not decided (A15, A21, A51, A52); none
  serving no pain or goal. 01-0010.0175 shows A49 only, inherited from q-0037.0006, though its
  re-cut also drafts the product document — its question carries no link of its own.

## Limits

- No weight, no rank: ordering by pain waits on the pain's measure, q-0037.0018.0004.
- An item is a ticket. An open question no ticket owns builds an answer for coverage but is no item
  here, so an answer realized only by such questions is *left out* of the queue while *in progress*
  in coverage — exactly the gap the person is shown to choose about.
