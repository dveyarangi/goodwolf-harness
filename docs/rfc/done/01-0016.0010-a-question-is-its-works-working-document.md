## Governing boundaries

- **The question is the working document.** [q-0037.0009.0017](../../questions/done/q-0037.0009.0017-what-does-spec-own.md),
  decisions 1 and 2 *(the user, 2026-10-15)*. A question's body runs `Where it stands`, `Decisions`,
  then dated evidence. A question a ticket answers carries its `Where it stands`, and a check holds
  that. The format half has already landed in [QUESTION-FORMAT.md](../../../.agents/skills/questions/QUESTION-FORMAT.md#the-body);
  the check is this slice's to build.
- **A change's path.** [q-0037.0013.0005](../../questions/q-0037.0013.0005-which-of-the-agents-acts-need-the-persons-yes-and-which-does-it-do-on-its-own.md),
  decision 8: load-bearing work and anything above it is aligned on its question until its
  `Where it stands` is accepted. Decision 9 makes the path development's, so it belongs to the
  practice mechanism. While that mechanism is undeclared, its text is written as a straw dog bound
  to [q-0037.0010.0002.0005](../../questions/q-0037.0010.0002.0005-what-does-the-practice-mechanism-own.md).
- **Core and instance.** [ADR 0002](../../adr/0002-core-may-not-depend-on-the-instance-half.md) and
  the entry file's *Core and instance*. Once `/spec` is gone, nothing in core declares `docs/spec/`,
  so core names neither that folder nor any file in it. The five specs stay as this tree's own
  documents until they are folded.
- **Findings never fail.** In `questions.py`, a `Finding` is what a person should look at, and the
  store is not wrong because of it. The new report is a finding: 38 of the 40 live tickets' questions
  lack a `Where it stands` today, and the ticket's own wording lists them for their next touch.
- **Live rows only.** R1 checks only the live rows of a record-bearing mechanism. Done tickets keep
  their `Parent scope addressed` as history.
- **Updates delete retired core files.** `harness.py` update removes a core path that the new ref no
  longer ships. A recipient therefore loses `.agents/skills/spec` on its next update, and its own
  `docs/spec/` files stay where they are.

## Shapes

**The finding** (`questions.py`, under *for a person to settle*). One function joins the check's
`findings`:

```python
def _unstated(entries, index) -> list[Finding]
```

It reports, for each live open entry with an owner whose parent's owner differs, a body that has no
`## Where it stands` line, as
`"<id> is answered by <owner> and has no Where it stands"`, at line 1. The heading is matched as a
whole line, the same way the `## Decisions` list is found. Owners are compared as the parts' text,
so two links to the same file compare equal. The mover already writes them that way.

**The entry file**, rewritten as proposed text for `/implement`; ⟦…⟧ marks what is wrapped as a
straw dog bound to q-0037.0010.0002.0005:

## Stages

Each stage follows `/tdd` and ends green on the project's verification set.

1. **The finding.** Red first: `test_questions.py` covers the check against a three-entry store. It
   expects a finding on an owned open entry without the heading. It expects none on that entry's
   child with the same owner, none on a closed owned entry, and none once the heading is added. Each
   case asserts the finding's text, so the test fails for the right reason. Then `_unstated`. A real
   `--check` lists 42 findings on this tree, counted at authoring. Thirty-eight are live tickets'
   questions: every one except q-0037.0009.0017's and q-0037.0013.0005's. The other four are
   stragglers the finding is right to name: q-0037.0001.0016 and q-0037.0010.0009.0003.0001 are open
   under done owners; q-0037.0012.0004.0001 is owned beneath an unowned parent; and q-0037.0014 is
   owned by a spec.
2. **The section goes.** The `test_tickets.py` fixture loses the section. One added case gives a
   narrowed ticket carrying `Parent scope addressed` and expects the extra-section diagnostic. Then
   `NAMED_SECTIONS`, the *five* wording in `tickets.py`, `TICKET-FORMAT.md` and `ticket.md` line 46
   becomes *four*. The section is removed from the 15 live tickets, and `tickets.py --check` stays
   green.
3. **Core forgets `docs/spec/`.** The `PAINTED_DOORS` row and its TODO go. `docs/spec/` leaves
   `_WORKING_RECORDS`, and `test_straw_dogs.py`'s loop follows. The `harness.py` and `docs_corpus.py`
   comments are reworded to cite the architecture's install contract. `mechanisms.py --check` and
   `straw_dogs.py` stay green.
4. **The skills and formats.** Under `/mechanism` and `/skill-up`, because every one of these is a
   change to how work is done here:
   - `.agents/skills/spec/` is deleted.
   - `/ticket`, `/maintain` (P4's straw dog), `/implement`, `/discover` and `/align` change as
     above.
   - `TICKET-FORMAT.md`'s `Parent` and `Parent scope addressed` change.
   - The maintain mechanism doc loses lines 53–56, the P4 row and lines 161–166.
   - `ticket.md` line 54, `mechanism-shape.md` line 89, and the glossary's lines 127 and 169.
   - Then the straw-dog listing has no straw dog left bound to q-0037.0009.0017 except the one on
     `/spec` itself, which goes with the skill.
5. **The entry file.** The text above lands, the contract moves to v91, and its findings line is
   written.
6. **Instance docs.** `process.md`, `product.md` A12 and A13, `pacer.md` and the root README say
   *an align on its question* and *`Where it stands` accepted* where they said spec. The product
   check stays green.
7. **The residue.** The question from *Taken at `/plan`* item 3 is opened and its `Where it stands`
   written. Criterion 1 is confirmed by a search: `/spec`, `docs/spec` and *a spec* as a record
   name nothing under `.agents/` or in `AGENTS.md`.
