**Why these words.**

- **The roots by their words, not as "purpose root" and "structure root".** Neither term is in the
  method's glossary, and core may not lean on this project's; the seeded words are what every
  store holds and what `product.py` finds the roots by.
- **No condition.** A tree that has the mechanism has its document, and one that does not take the
  mechanism gets none of its rules
  ([q-0037.0018](../../questions/q-0037.0018-what-is-a-projects-product-document-to-the-method-and-which-of-the-methods-mechanisms-keeps-it.md),
  decision 11, the user, 2026-10-14). Until the install creates the document, an agent told to
  write an answer into a document that is not there writes it in the format; `/product` needs no
  sentence for it.
- **Numbers stay `/product`'s.** U2's last sentence defers to it rather than restating it: the
  format keeps a replaced answer's id and gives its replacement the next, and a second statement
  here would be a second home to drift.
- **U3 says nothing of propagation.** The questions skill's records section already says a
  question without a link carries its nearest ancestor's.
- **The waiting row is rewritten, not doubled.** An undecided row (this tree's A15, A21, A51, A52)
  already holds the answer's id; deciding its question empties its *waits on* cell. Removing the
  straw dog before the closure keeps the wake from reporting a due straw dog the same step
  repaired.
- **The building question carries its own link wherever it opens.** Opened below a structure
  question that realizes another answer, it would otherwise inherit that answer by trace
  propagation; `realize` replaces what it would carry. Placement follows Q1's *lowest question
  that contains it* — see the decision below.
- **The zigzag needs no rule of its own.** A question whether an answer or its causes must change
  is a question about what the project is for: Q1 already opens it under the purpose root, and its
  decision is allocated by U2's last sentence; `/product`'s first goal already keeps ids. Its
  history — the structure work it came from — is the body's, as decision 4 says.

## Limits

- The statement at opening is not enforced: an orphan is found, never refused.
- A decision with nothing to build closes where Q6 lands it; U2 does not fire.
- A decided sub-question of a purpose question a row waits on: whether it settles that row or adds
  one is the person's call at the align; U2 rewrites a row only for the question it waits on.
- A replaced or retired answer leaves its building questions linked to it; whether they move to
  the replacement is the person's call at the align.
- The format says an answer is retired in place but gives a retired row no mark, so the check
  would report a retired answer nothing builds as unrealized. U2 names retiring and adds nothing
  to it; the mark is the format's to give, outside this slice.
- A pain's measure, weighting the queue by pain, and the queue's view of what each item realizes
  are q-0037.0018.0004's and `.0050`'s.
