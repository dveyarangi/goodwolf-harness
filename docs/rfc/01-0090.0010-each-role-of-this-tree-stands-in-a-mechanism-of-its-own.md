## Where the plan reads an accepted line one way

Seven readings the person sees before the plan is accepted. Each is the narrowest the format and
the records allow.

1. **"Its parts and what it produces are the role's zone."** A role's mechanism lists what the
   role holds under *What it produces, and who reads it*, in the bullet form that section already
   has. An output whose whole name is one code span is a mechanism of the tree, by its slug, or a
   path or path pattern in it. No field and no table is added to the format: the whole's own
   words name the section.
2. **"The name stands for the paths that mechanism declares."** A mechanism's name stands for its
   directory, the folder of each skill it names and its parts — never for its records: questions
   are no part of a zone, and neither are the tickets or the session records.
3. **"Every role has a mechanism of its own."** Five local mechanisms, `always on`, each a doc and
   one skill of the same name: `role-keeper`, `role-rules`, `role-store`, `role-product`,
   `role-practice`. The prefix is needed: `product` and `practice` are already the names of
   mechanisms those roles hold. The names are the plan's and the person's to change.
4. **"A table in its local rules file, installed into the entry file."** The table,
   `| role | session |`, stands in the body of local rule L13: a role by its mechanism's slug, a
   session by its name. The installer already carries a rule's body into the entry file, and a
   script finds the table by its header row, in the local file alone. The alternative — a table
   in the file's preamble, which the installer would have to learn to render — adds a part for
   the same text.
5. **"A session is named by its name."** The name is the one in the session's title, as today. A
   session that holds several roles has one name and a row for each role. What a session's row
   carries is the store's session's to say, and this plan asks nothing new of the row.
6. **"What the table does not name is held by the session that holds the harness."** In this tree
   that is the session the table gives `role-keeper`, whose zone names the `harness` mechanism.
   No setting says it a second time.
7. **The first cut**, drawn from the list the local rules hold today and from
   [q-dyc63i](../questions/q-dyc63i-which-standing-roles-does-this-tree-have-where-every-session-develops-the-harness-itself.md);
   each session confirms its own line, and the cut is the person's to judge:

   | role | session | holds |
   |---|---|---|
   | `role-keeper` | Хранитель | the mechanisms `harness`, `edge`, `debugging`; the entry file and its loader files, the local rules file, the method's glossary, the journal of the way of working and the record of what each version of the entry contract changed; and whatever no role names |
   | `role-rules` | Хранитель, until the person opens a session for it | the mechanism `mechanism-shape`, the rule-failure register, the folder of `/skill-up` |
   | `role-store` | Хранилище | the mechanisms `questions`, `session` |
   | `role-product` | Продукт | the mechanism `product` |
   | `role-practice` | Практика | the mechanisms `ticket`, `maintain`, `delivery`, `practice`; the folders of `/align`, `/impact`, `/commit` |

   What the keeper keeps once the rules leave it was to be put to the person (q-dyc63i,
   decision 2): this row is that proposal. The landing on main has no mechanism and no row while
   it is not opened.

## The shape

**The format, first.** *Keeps:* *What carries a role*. The format shelf gains, under the doc's
*What it produces*: an output whose whole name is one code span names a mechanism of the tree or
a path in it, and a name that resolves to neither is a diagnostic; a mechanism or a file that two
mechanisms name is a diagnostic, unless each of the two bullets is wrapped as a straw dog — the
mark of a file not yet moved apart. An output named in words, or by a code span with words
beside it, is read as today. The shape's doc gains the invariant in one sentence — what one
mechanism names as its output no other names, or both say so — with its pointer to the shelf;
the reason stays with the whole. The method's glossary gains *Zone* and *Role* in the whole's
words, *Role* wrapped as a straw dog bound to
[q-p4sdnv](../questions/q-p4sdnv-what-is-an-agent-role-and-which-mechanism-keeps-roles.md),
where the word has a second account.

**One reader.** The shape's script answers, for a mechanism, which files its named outputs stand
for; the check uses it now, and the telling at an edit
([01-0090.0030](../tickets/01-0090.0030-a-session-crossing-into-anothers-zone-is-told-whose-it-is.md))
and the guard
([01-0010.0125.0010](../tickets/01-0010.0125.0010-an-edit-waits-for-the-skill-that-keeps-its-file.md))
read through it later. A call that answers whose a path is belongs to the slice that tells.

**The roles' mechanisms.** *Keeps:* *What carries a role*; *Two trees*. Each is
`.agents/mechanisms/role-<name>/role-<name>.md` and `.agents/skills/role-<name>/SKILL.md`,
`owner local`, `always on`, with no rules file and no script. The doc: what the role answers for
in its title line; its moments — taking the role, a change asked by another role's session, what
the nightly pass leaves in its zone; its one part, the skill; what it holds, as named outputs,
and who reads each — the seams with the other zones; the grading. The skill is the brief: what
the role answers for, what a successor reads first, what stands open in the zone and where, whom
it asks. It names the mechanisms it holds by their slugs and no skill of theirs (R8). Its
description names its use cases alone: taking the role, and asking what the role answers for.

**The table and the local rule.** *Keeps:* *Who holds which*. L13's body becomes: the table; the
default — what the table does not name; where a session reads its name; that a mechanism's
rules, skills and scripts are written and put to the person by the session whose zone holds it,
and a change in another's is asked of that session. The list of roles leaves it. Each rule of the
entry file it overrides is named there, as R7 asks, and the rule stays wrapped as a straw dog.
The checks gain: a role the table names that is no mechanism of the tree is a diagnostic, and so
is a row with an empty cell. A tree with no such table has nothing to check and passes.

**The entry contract** takes a new version with L13's block, and its findings an entry.

## Docs, first

The format shelf and the shape's doc are the architecture here: stage 1 writes their sentences
before the check that holds them, and no role's mechanism is written before the form it is
written to.

## Stages

Each stage ends with the checks green, and with the tests green where it changed a file that is
not markdown. Every instruction's text — each skill's, the local rule's, the format's — is
drafted through `/skill-up` and shown to the person before it lands, by the session that wrote
it. Each stage writes its step into [the journal](../research/standing-sessions-journal.md).

0. **The cheapest example first.** A local mechanism with one skill, written in a clone of this
   tree and run through the shape's check, the installer's check and the ship: the first of its
   kind here. What it showed is under *The cheapest example*, below.
1. **The format and its check.** The shelf's sentences, the shape's invariant and its *Moments*
   and *What it produces* rows, the glossary's two entries. Then tests first, each refusal
   watched to fail in both polarities: an output's name that resolves to nothing; a mechanism
   named by two; a file named by two; the same two marked, passing; a doc whose outputs are
   named in words, unchanged. Then the reader and the three diagnostics. Proof: the tests; the
   check over this tree, whose docs name no output by a whole code span today.
2. **The keeper's two roles**, as the form's first use: `role-keeper` and `role-rules`, each
   text shown. Proof: the checks; a fresh subagent given one skill and nothing else says what the
   role answers for, what its zone holds and whom it asks; a second judges the answer against
   the doc.
3. **The other three**, each by its session, asked with stage 2's two as the example; each shows
   its own text to the person and lands it in its own commit. Proof: as stage 2, per role.
4. **The table.** L13 rewritten, shown, installed with overwrite; the table's two diagnostics,
   tests first; the contract's version and its findings entry. Each standing session is told the
   ref, and its row is read for the name the table gives it.
5. **The judged tests and the ship.** The criteria's two fresh readers; an install from this ref
   into an empty tree, read for the roles' files; `/verify`.

## Impact

**Impact.** Ten new files, all local; the format shelf, the shape's doc and its script with its
tests; the method's glossary; the local rules file and its block in the entry file; the entry
contract's version and its findings. Every session of this tree is handed five more skill
descriptions.

**Hidden edges.**

- These are the first mechanisms declared local here: the check's and the ship's rules for one
  have run on tests alone until now.
- A brief wants to name the skills its role holds, and R8 forbids one skill naming another
  installed mechanism's; a brief names mechanisms by slug.
- A recipient whose own mechanism names an output by a whole code span that resolves to nothing
  is refused at its next update. Core's docs hold none; the recipients' keepers are told the ref.
- game-delta and fimonitor keep their roles as a list in their local rules. Nothing here touches
  that, and nothing reads it: their zones stay unknown to the check until they write them so.
- Three of the five texts are other sessions' to write, and the table waits for all five: the
  check refuses a row whose role has no mechanism.
- The row's name is the session's, and the session mechanism's rule on it says "the role's
  name"; whether that sentence changes is asked of the session that keeps the rows.
- `/maintain`'s marks know nothing of the five until its next pass.
- A skill no mechanism names sits in a role's zone by its folder; when it is given a mechanism
  ([q-gbt7cy](../questions/q-gbt7cy-does-each-skill-state-only-what-it-owns.md)) the role's bullet
  is rewritten to name that.

**Leave alone.** The rows, the holds and the window; the installer's grammar and its tables; the
guard; the recipients' local rules; a role's rights and skill set; the session mechanism's doc
and rules.

**Recommendation.** Proceed, in the five stages after the example of stage 0.
