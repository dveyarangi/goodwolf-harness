# An output declares its kind — implementation plan

**Authored:** 2026-10-08

Implements [01-0011.0110.0030 an-output-declares-its-kind](../tickets/01-0011.0110.0030-an-output-declares-its-kind.md),
third slice of [the spec](../spec/01-0011.0110-every-written-thing-has-a-source-of-truth.md):
the mechanism format carries the kind of each output, the shape check reads it, the five docs
are brought to it, `/maintain`'s one rule becomes two repairs, and `/skill-up` says what a skill
is to the mechanism shape.

## What governs it

- The spec's *The mechanism shape and maintain*, and the glossary's *Kind of record* landed by
  `.0010`: the four kinds, and that a format whose every record is of one kind declares it once.
- The mechanism format's *What it produces, and who reads it*: every artifact names its reader
  there; the section is prose and never parsed. This slice makes one thing in it parsable — the
  kind on each bulleted output — and leaves the prose alone.
- *Absence is not clearance* and the shape's incept rule: the check is written before the docs
  are brought to it, and watched to fail in both polarities.
- `/maintain`'s A1 and the maintain doc's *How it works*, which say *both ways*; the spec splits
  them by kind.

## Decisions this plan makes

1. **The form.** A bulleted output in *What it produces, and who reads it* reads
   `- **<output>** — *<kind>* — <who reads it…>`: the bold name, then the kind in italics
   between dashes, then the reader prose as today. The kind is one of the four — *what it
   intends to become*, *what must always hold*, *what exists*, *what happened* — or
   *its format's*, for an output that is a record of another mechanism and takes that format's
   kind: a maintenance pass's repaired files, its moved records. A format admitting two kinds by
   state says the first and the rule after it: *what it intends to become, what happened once
   closed* — the check reads the first.
2. **The check reads bulleted outputs only.** A top-level `- **…**` line under the heading, up
   to the next heading, must carry a kind; prose in the section is not read. A bullet with no
   kind is the diagnostic *'<output>' names no kind*; one with a phrase outside the five, *'<output>'
   names a kind the format does not admit*. A section with no bullets passes this check and is
   still held to the existing absence check. The check's reading of a section's bullets is one
   small reader beside the table readers, not a parser of the prose.
3. **The five docs' outputs, assigned.** Harness: the recipient's core *what exists* (regenerated
   from the announced ref by an update), the announce line *what exists*, the report *what
   exists*, a fresh tree's delivery status *what it intends to become*, the repository line
   *what must always hold* (authored once by the person, the only home), the pending command
   *what exists*. Maintain: the pass's report *what happened*, repaired files and moved records
   *its format's*, a retired straw dog's replacement sentence *what must always hold*, the
   listing script's guesses *what exists*, the rules file *what must always hold*, the marks
   *what exists*, the clock's report *what exists*. Mechanism shape: the declaration *what must
   always hold*, the evidence *what happened*, `--check`'s and the installer's reports *what
   exists*, `--index`'s render *what exists*, the rules file *what must always hold*, a skill's
   claim *what exists* (it states the skill's standing, and the check reads it). Questions: the
   entries *what it intends to become, what happened once closed*; the sessions file, the window,
   the wake's read, the fingerprints, the hook's answers and the check's report *what exists*; the
   rules file *what must always hold*. Ticket: the tickets *what it intends to become*, the
   queue's table *what exists*, the maintainer's report *what exists*, the mover's report *what
   happened*, the rules file *what must always hold*. Thirty-eight bullets; any the
   implementation finds unassigned here takes the kind its reader prose implies and the
   verification names it.
4. **The two repairs.** A1 reads: *docs to the meta-rules and their format; a record to the
   thing it is of, by its kind — one of what must always hold repairs the thing, one of what
   exists is regenerated from it; live records to their declared format; every fact to one
   home.* The maintain doc's *How it works* says the same in its sentence; its Moments row on a
   landed slice keeps *both ways*, since verification reads both directions by design.
5. **`/skill-up` says what a skill is to the shape**: one sentence at the head of its writing
   rules — a skill is, for the mechanism shape, a record of the kind *what must always hold*,
   and the rules below are that kind's invariants for an instruction. No other skill is told.
6. **The doc carries its own kind, visibly** *(the user, 2026-10-08: the kind must be in the
   record, or it is not in context; the point is that the editing agent adds no rubbish at
   once)*. The declaration's header gains a third bullet, `- **kind** what must always hold`,
   beside *instruction* and *state*; the header check reads it and reports a doc that names no
   kind or another than the one a doc is. The format's declaration is the authored home, the
   bullet the mark at the thing, and the check holds them equal. The same pattern reaches every
   other record an agent edits — the ticket, the RFC, the question entry, the queue — in `.0040`,
   where each record format says where its mark sits; documents without a format at `.0040`'s
   align or a slice split from it. This slice touches no record file but the five docs.

## Impact — 2026-10-08

**Impact.** `mechanisms.py` (one reader, two diagnostics), its tests, `MECHANISM-FORMAT.md`
(the bullet form and the five admitted phrases), the five mechanism docs (thirty-eight bullets
gain a kind), `/maintain`'s A1 and the maintain doc, `/skill-up` (one sentence). Every recipient
with its own declared mechanism meets the new diagnostic at its next `--check`: a recipient doc
whose outputs name no kind fails. **Hidden edges.** That recipient failure is the real cost of
the slice: xuanxue-workshop declares a mechanism of its own, and its doc will fail the check
after the update until its bullets carry kinds — the update's note must say so, and the
diagnostic's wording must tell the reader what to write. The output level of every mechanism
moves (its doc), and the rules level of all five (the format is in the shape's skill files) —
the clock, marked at the next pass. *Its format's* admits a kind the four do not name, by
design; a reader may misuse it for an output of the mechanism's own, which judgment catches,
not the check. **Leave alone.** The Moments and parts tables and their readers; the prose of
every produces section; the records themselves; the leak check. **Recommendation:** proceed,
with the recipient cost named in the slice's criteria.

## Cheapest refutation

That every output has one of the four kinds: assigning thirty-eight bullets by hand found two
that fit none — repaired files and moved records, which are other mechanisms' — and the fifth
phrase is the refutation's residue, kept narrow by its definition.

## Stages

1. **The check, red first** (`/tdd`): five tests in `test_mechanisms.py` — on the fixture's
   produces section, a bullet with no kind is a diagnostic naming the output; one with a kind
   outside the five is a diagnostic naming the output; one with each admitted kind, including
   *its format's* and the two-kind form, is none; on the header, a doc with no *kind* bullet is
   a diagnostic, and one whose kind is not *what must always hold* is a diagnostic naming what
   it said. Then the readers in `mechanisms.py` — the produces bullets beside the table readers,
   the kind bullet beside the state — green.
2. **The format.** `MECHANISM-FORMAT.md`'s header gains the *kind* bullet, its produces
   paragraph the bullet form and the five phrases, one sentence each; nothing else moves.
3. **The five docs**, the *kind* bullet in each header and each output's bullet to decision 3;
   `mechanisms.py --check` clean.
4. **Maintain and skill-up.** A1 and the maintain doc's sentence to decision 4; the sentence in
   `/skill-up`.
5. **The checks.** The suite; `mechanisms.py --check`; `inject_rules.py --check` (A1 is the
   maintain skill's own text, not an installed block — unchanged for the installer);
   `maintain.py --check` reporting the levels due, left to the next pass.

Stage 1 is red until its reader lands, then green; every later stage keeps the set green.

## Tests

- `test_mechanisms.py`: the three tests of stage 1, each asserting the diagnostic's text names
  the output, so a passing test proves the reason and not only that something was raised. Prior
  art: `test_must_account_for_what_the_mechanism_produces`.
- No test for the docs' assignments: they are judgment, verified by `/verify` against decision 3.

## Out of scope

The kind of a record declared per record (the question store's open/closed is declared by the
format's two-kind phrase, not per entry). The queue's rendering and the ticket's thinning —
`.0040`. The mark back — `.0050`. A recipient's own docs: they are told, not repaired.
