## 1. The kinds of outside reader

- **"a subagent the builder starts"**, a judge (rows 1, 4, and 7 for the practice doc's judge). It
  alone returns a verdict the builder closes on, and what is withheld is the builder's conclusion.
- **"a fresh subagent that reads only what a session would"**, also "a fresh agent, given this doc
  alone" and "a blind run" (rows 2, 7, 11, 12, 13, 32). The reader is the case an instruction is
  tried on, not its judge; a second party scores it against a key or reads its return.
- **"a separate reasoning process that has none of your context"** (rows 10, 3). It is withheld
  the repository itself and returns "material, never a verdict".
- **"a subagent briefed with the same bounds"** (rows 14, 15). It finds or repairs; it is given
  the session's bounds, with no isolation and no verdict.
- **"someone who did not build it"** as a later session (rows 5, 6). It is pre-registered and
  outside by time and task, not by a withheld context.
- **The person** (rows 3, 8, 25, 27, 30, 37). They decide, accept or grade, gated by the dial.
- **A script** (rows 18, 38, part of 19). It reads form, never content; "a clean run means nothing
  was caught".
- **"a pass"** (rows 16, 17, 19-22, 26, 36). A later reading with no rule about who produced the
  thing.
- **Another session** (rows 33, 34). Barred from a running unit; a standing session keeps outside
  reviews.
- **"a judge outside the generator"** (row 31). Not built.

## 4. The ten skills

- **discover** is one: a separate process, material only (`.agents/skills/discover/SKILL.md:83,24`).
- **impact** is neither: the working agent, inline (`.agents/skills/impact/SKILL.md:45-46`).

  align.
