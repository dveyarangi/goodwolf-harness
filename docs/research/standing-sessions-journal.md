# Standing sessions — how this tree built the way of working

A record of what happened, a step per entry, for
[q-ja5mp4](../questions/q-ja5mp4-does-the-method-have-one-session-direct-the-others-an-orchestrator-and-what-does-it-owe-the-sessions-it.md).
The way of working is built first in this tree and offered to every tree, so each step also
names what a fresh tree would need to take it by itself; that sketch is
[q-wit7by](../questions/q-wit7by-what-does-a-fresh-tree-under-the-harness-need-to-set-up-standing-sessions-by-itself.md)'s
material, never a rule until it lands in a skill or a rules file.

## 1. Evidence from a tree already working so — 2026-11-15

The person asked this session to question game-delta's orchestrator about how it works with its
sessions, and to have it survey the sessions it serves. Two reads came back: the orchestrator's
own report with all eight sessions' answers (game-delta f24efe9), and an outside read by a
subagent of this tree ([the audit](game-delta-orchestration-audit-2026-11-15.md)). They agree: nine
sessions cut by the game's domains move fast; the shared checkout is the root of most collisions;
the reply's form is the main excess; the orchestrator directs no work, and its duties that need no
judgement — ids, the nightly sweep — lapsed.

<straw-dog question="q-wit7by">
A fresh tree needs: a way to ask the sessions it already runs how the work feels to them, the
answers kept in their own words, and a read of the tree's records by someone other than the
session that keeps the way of working.
</straw-dog>

## 2. The shape chosen — 2026-11-15

Four options were put to the person; the person took the fourth: **a keeper of the harness, one
area among the others.** It directs no work. It is the one writer of the rules, the mechanisms,
the rule-failure register and the updates from core. Its arbitration is finding where areas
collide and bringing each collision to the person as a decision. Merging into main is a second
such area, the lander. What needs no judgement is mechanism: ids, lines kept apart by a worktree
per writing session, landing through the lander, a notice of changed rules, the scheduled sweep.
The keeper does not raise the coordinator; the first call that finds none does.

<straw-dog question="q-wit7by">
A fresh tree needs: the keeper's area named in its roster from the second standing session on;
every other session registering a rule failure and handing the amendment to the keeper instead of
landing it.
</straw-dog>

<straw-dog question="q-wit7by">

</straw-dog>

## 6. A recipient takes the way of working in order — 2026-11-15

<straw-dog question="q-wit7by">

## 8. The sweep's findings reach the keeper — 2026-11-15
