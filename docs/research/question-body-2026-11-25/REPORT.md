**Three forms.** *As it is*. *Apart*: the same text, the stop, `Where it stands` and the decisions in one
file, 23.7 thousand characters, the rest in a file beside it. *Rewritten*: [a head of 16.4 thousand](draft.md),
the whole restated to its latest date, the old text in a file beside it; drafted by a subagent.

**How.** An examiner, a subagent that wrote none of it, read the entry whole, wrote the questions and the
key, and graded every sheet blind to its form, the order shuffled and the citations cut. Readers were fresh
subagents (Claude Code, the host's read-only kind), given the files alone.

**Round one: questions.** Fourteen, for six kinds of reader, two readers to a form; and three of the
fourteen alone, one reader to a form.

The exam stood at its ceiling, and its questions warn that something was overtaken. The examiner had first
read the entry as failing the person who accepts it, the builder and a neighbouring area, with over twenty
places overtaken and not marked so; on the sheets it took those three verdicts back. One thing differed in
kind: that the first of the proposed tests *has run*, which the entry nowhere settles, was said by five of
the six sheets of the other forms and by none of the three rewritten ones, which call it unsaid.

**Round two: no question, no warning.** "The person has just written «привет»": what they are told, and a
note to whoever builds. Two readers to a form; twelve points, written before a sheet was opened.

No sheet would have the folder or its calls built. The differences are within chance. The error that
matters stands in four sheets of six, in both forms but *apart*: the 22 % of the tree that clearing saves
over waking uncleared chats, told to the person as what dropping the letters saves; both of *apart*'s
understate what a folder saves over clearing. One of *apart*'s readers was replaced: the first mistyped its
folder's path and the host refused it. Of the rewritten form's two, one read the head and one section of
the evidence; the other read everything, the head more than once by a mistyped path, so its 35 is partly
that.

**What is read off.** The entry is right for a reader that reads it whole, and nearly every reader of it
did. Setting the evidence apart changed neither what was read nor what was right. Rewriting the head let one
reader of two stop early in round two, saved nothing in round one, and is where holes come to be seen.
An entry of this size costs some 25 thousand tokens once, against a first request of 73 thousand.

**Limits.** One entry, two readers to a form and one drafter; agents, not the person, who is one of its
readers; readers told to be economical in round one and not in round two.
