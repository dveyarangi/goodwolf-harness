## What was asked

Fourteen open leaf questions of this tree, drawn at random, each hidden from a copy of the store
and its words handed to a reader as a new question, with the window of a session standing near it
(six) or elsewhere under the same root (eight). The reader answers the parent. *Exact* is the
question's parent in the store, which is one person's placing and not the only defensible one.

- *preview*: the first 2,000 characters of the window as drawn until then, all that Claude Code put
  in context of one past its cap.
- *whole*: that window in full, about 60,000 characters, which no host showed.
- *short, whole*: the new window, and the whole one where the short holds no place.
- *short, tree*: the new window, and the tree under a question it shows, as `--tree q-N` draws it;
  two readers, A on Sonnet as the other three are, B on the session's model.
