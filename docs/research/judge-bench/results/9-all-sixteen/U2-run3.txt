PART: The draft makes "the answer" the relation's end and fingerprints "the text of the answer or lean", but the store holds no answer text: a closed question only points to its answer (a link plus who and when) and the answer lives in a doc outside the store.
CONTRACT CHANGE: The ends of the relation, and so what a changed-answer detector can see, change. A fingerprint of the stored pointer line would stay silent when the linked doc changes and fire on a re-pointing or a reword of the line. A fingerprint of the linked doc would fire on any edit to it and would reach outside the store. A closed answer therefore has no identity of its own for `assumes` to attach to, so the relation may have to name a question plus its closing event or a doc, which removes the "ends are answers, not questions" claim or the script-finds-it claim. The "a reword fires nothing" rule from the earlier draft also has to be redone, because a lean is a free line and a fingerprint of it fires on every rewording.
REASONING: The draft commits to a shape: a new relation `assumes` between answers (or the lean of an open question), fixed by a text fingerprint so that a script, not the agent's memory, finds the changes. Its justification (the answer as the subject, the fingerprint as the recommendation) rests on there being an answer text to hash. The tree's questions script says "a closed question points to its answer and never holds it", and the decided answer is validated as a link to a doc, so the store has no answer body. This undermines both decisions the draft puts to the person: the ends (answers) and how to fix the version (a fingerprint rather than a date). Asking the person to choose between date and fingerprint leaves this premise unexamined. The date alternative does exist in the pointer (who, date), which shows the choice changes what an "answer" is. Other candidates, such as how `assumes` differs from `depends on` and who judges whether an assumption broke, were already examined in the earlier exchange, or are local detail, so they pass the counter-test.
