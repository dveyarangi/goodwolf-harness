PART: The draft fixes depends on and assumes as two separate relations told apart by a wording-dependent test, without examining that the only operational difference is blocking, that every dependency is already an assumption, and that the store's real "depends on" is a wait (cannot be asked until B is answered), which the draft's redefinition changes.
CONTRACT CHANGE: The mechanism may be one relation (assumes, anchored to B's answer) with a blocking flag, not two kinds of relation. Under the draft's test the relation's type, and so whether it blocks, flips when A is reworded, yet the store holds that a reword fires nothing. Depends on would also gain a "B's answer changed" trigger that the store does not check. The relation record format, the check, and the rename and reword behaviour all depend on which of these it is.
REASONING: The draft commits to a definition: depends on means A's question presupposes B's answer and so blocks, assumes means A's answer takes B's as a premise and does not block, and the test "can A's options be listed without B's answer" separates them. In the tree, depends on is defined as "cannot be asked until q-B is answered", and the script only reports a closed A whose dependency is still open, which is an answer decided under an open ground. It does not detect a changed B answer, so the draft's account that a changed B damages A's question and therefore blocks is new, and the blocking does not follow from it. The record the draft says it updated already says a dependency is also an assumption by construction. The draft's own example shows one pair moving from assumes to depends with the wording of A. That makes the type a property of the wording, not of the pair, and a blocking behaviour hangs on it. The draft takes this as settled and puts to the person only whether to accept it and whether to move to /align. That choice leaves the premise unexamined: whether two kinds are needed at all, or whether one relation with a blocking attribute does the job. Settling that later would rewrite the record format, the check and the reword rules, so it is not local detail.
