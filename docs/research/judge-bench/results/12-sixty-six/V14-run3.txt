PART: The trigger condition of the proposed late re-check ("while the new conversation still hasn't done anything") is not examined against the turn protocol: the new session's first turn is told to call `at` or `open` on a question, so it acquires a position (and a window history) before the re-check's second chance, and the session is then no longer "empty"; what happens when the new id already holds its own position is also unsettled.
CONTRACT CHANGE: The fix cannot be gated on "no position and no history", because the first turn itself writes a position (the very reply being answered pins q-0027). The late link needs a different gate, such as a pending-link marker on the registration, and it must define a merge or override rule when the new id already has its own position. That changes what the fix promises (resume always carries the position over, versus only when the agent happens not to act on its first message) and what depends on it (position semantics, the ended-old-row rule, and fork handling, since a fork also shares the first uuid and a late check could end a still-running parent).
