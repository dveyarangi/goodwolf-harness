PART: The draft makes the difference between depends on and assumes a property of how A is worded, but it never asks whether the two are really two relations. Both point at B's answer, both lead to the same action (A is marked suspect when B's answer changes), and the only difference left is blocking. It also never checks that the store's depends on records no answer to pin, so a changed answer of B cannot find a closed A that depends on it.

CONTRACT CHANGE: The proposed contract is two relations, depends on and assumes, told apart by a wording test (can A's options be listed without B's answer?). Once examined, it could become one answer-pointing relation with a blocking strength. Pinning and detection would then apply to depends on as well, which today only flags a closed A whose dependency is still open. Or it could stay two relations, with depends on given the pinning that assumes gets. Either way, a reword of A would no longer silently switch which relation applies, which would change what /align is asked to approve.

REASONING: The draft commits to a definition of two relations (presupposition versus premise, tested by the wording of A) and to /align on that basis. In the tree, `depend q-A --on q-B` records only a question id, and the check `_unmarked_suspects` flags a closed A whose dependency is still open as one it was "decided under". That is already premise-like behaviour, which weakens the draft's claim that the store's re-check has the shape of a presupposition. The q-0001.0023 record also admits that a dependency is an assumption by construction, so the new relation adds blocking and answer-pinning, not a different kind of dependence. Nothing in the code actually refuses to ask a blocked question, so "blocks" is advisory. The draft says a changed answer of B puts a depending A under attack, but depends on has no mechanism for finding A in that case, only for B reopening. The wording test makes the relation depend on how A is phrased, and the draft itself says a reword fires nothing, so a reword could flip depends on to assumes without anything noticing. These points decide whether the store has two relations or one with a strength, and what the record format and script must carry, so they are not local detail. The earlier exchanges examined the ends (answers rather than questions) and the pinning, but not whether the distinction survives once both relations point at answers.
