PART: The draft makes the relation's ends "answers" and fingerprints "the answer's text or lean", yet in this store a closed answer is only a pointer line to a doc, ADR or code (the skill: "the answer is never the deliberation itself"), so what counts as "that answer changed" is left unexamined.
CONTRACT CHANGE: The promise "a change in the assumed answers finds the dependent answer" narrows or moves. Either it covers only changes the store itself records (a changed pointer line, a rewritten lean, a re-close), and so misses the common case of the pointed doc being amended in place, or it must fingerprint the pointed doc. That second option crosses the core/instance boundary, since core may not depend on docs/ records. It also leaves an open question's lean as the usual assumed thing, and leans are rewritten every session by /conclude, which makes every fingerprint mismatch noisy.
REASONING: The draft commits to a shape: a relation `assumes` whose ends are answers, which lives only as long as the answer that wrote it, which differs from `depends on`, and which records a fingerprint of the assumed answer or lean so the script can detect change. I checked the questions skill and script. Entries hold `answer` as a pointer (link plus who and when) and `lean` as a free line with no date; the check at questions.py around line 661 does complain about a closed entry depending on a reopened one, as the draft says. The distinction table and the "answers, not questions" correction hold up and were what the person asked about. The fingerprint recommendation, however, rests on the assumption that the answer's text is the answer. Here the text is a pointer, and the substance lives in docs the store does not read. A fingerprint of the pointer line misses in-place doc amendments and fires on cosmetic pointer edits. A fingerprint of the doc crosses the core/docs boundary. Lean churn is a second, lesser worry. This decides what the mechanism can promise and where its boundary lies, so it is not a local detail. Other candidates (clearing edges when an answer is rewritten, call syntax, naming) are local and pass the counter-test.
