PART: The draft makes answers the relation's ends and snapshots them by fingerprint, but in this store an answer is not an addressable object: it is one line of the question's entry (a pointer such as a link to a doc or ADR plus who and when), and an open question's lean is a free-text line rewritten at will, so what the fingerprint covers and when it fires was never examined.
CONTRACT CHANGE: What "assumes" promises changes: either the link ends up on the question id with the existing event firing (close, re-close, suspect) after all, or the snapshot has to cover what the pointer points to (the decided doc) instead of the pointer line, and the lean case needs its own rule. As drafted, the fingerprint would fire on a rewritten date or wording and miss an amended ADR, and every lean edit would flag every dependent; detection also moves from event-driven (the earlier design, where each call touching B prints who rests on it) to a state comparison that a new check or window has to run. That alters the relation's end, the trigger, and what the closure and lean calls must do (clear or refresh the link).
REASONING: The draft commits to a relation whose subjects are answers, recorded with a fingerprint of the target answer or lean so that a script finds the change, with the agent still judging whether the assumption broke. I considered the choice of subject (answers versus questions), the distinction from depends on, the date-versus-fingerprint choice, and the claim that the check already treats depends on as an assumption. I verified the last in questions.py (_unmarked_suspects reports a closed entry depending on an open one). In questions.md and SKILL.md the unit is "the open question", an answer is the single part `answer` of an entry that is overwritten on re-close and normally holds a link and attribution, and the stated principle is that the same question means the same answers. So "answer" as a snapshot-able end has no identity of its own, and the draft's own example (an answer as a proposition) does not match what the store holds. The fingerprint-versus-date table puts the choice to the person but leaves this premise untouched. The premise bears on the contract (what the relation ends on, what fires it, what close and lean must do), not on a local detail, so it fails the counter-test. The distinction from depends on and the choice of the word are local and pass.
