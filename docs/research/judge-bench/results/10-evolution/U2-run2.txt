A text fingerprint makes every rewrite of B's lean or answer wording a change, mostly for open questions like the landscape, so the relation either floods with false suspects or the script stops deciding and the agent again judges what counts as a change. That moves the boundary between what the script finds and what the agent judges, and adds a per-link stored fingerprint to the entry schema (unlike the plain id lists of part of and depends on), which the check, the move/rename call and archiving to done/ all have to carry.
