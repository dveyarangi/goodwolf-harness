PART: The draft makes "an answer" the endpoint of `assumes` and pins it with a fingerprint of the answer's or lean's text, but the store never holds an answer as content: a closed question's answer part is only a pointer (a link to the deciding doc plus who and when, or a bare id for merged/superseded), an open question carries no answer part at all (the check refuses one), and a lean is free text changed by reword.
CONTRACT CHANGE: What is pinned, and so what a change means, is redefined. A fingerprint of the pointer line cannot see the deciding doc or ADR change underneath it, so it would miss the real invalidation and fire on harmless relinks or lean rewordings. The relation might instead have to be pinned to a closure event or to the cited doc, and the check's "ground went away" rules (superseded/pruned/moot) would need to say what an endpoint is for non-decided kinds. The claim that assumes "dies with the answer that made it" and that the script, not the agent, finds the change also depends on this, and the fingerprint may be redundant with the earlier plan of printing dependents at every call that touches B.
REASONING: The draft commits to a new relation `assumes` between answers, not questions, with a fingerprint chosen over a date and the agent's judgement left for whether the assumption actually broke. The move from questions to answers, and the table against depends on, were what the person asked about, so that part was examined. What the draft did not check is what an answer is in this store. The questions skill and the store's check state that a closed question points to its answer and never holds it, that an open question carries no answer, and that a decided answer is a link to a doc. A fingerprint of that text therefore does not track the content the assumption rests on, and for leans it fires on any rewording, though the store holds that the same question means the same answers and never the same wording. This crosses the store, the check and the doc records, and it is what the shape's guarantee (the script finds broken assumptions) depends on, so it is not a local detail. Reasons to pass: the choice of endpoint is settled by the person, and the fingerprint-versus-date choice is put forward as a decision. But that choice leaves its premise, that an answer has a fingerprintable content, unexamined.
