PART: The draft makes a closed question's answer point at a line in an open ancestor's `## Decisions` list and makes `due` read that pointer as "not yet arrived", but it does not say what happens to the pointer (and to the straw dogs waiting on the question) when that line later lands or is moved up again to a higher ancestor.
CONTRACT CHANGE: The moved-not-copied rule leaves the closed child's answer pointing at a list the line has left. The draft therefore needs either a rule that the answer is rewritten or follows the line, or a different test for "landed" than the cited-file test `due` uses today. That changes what a closed answer promises and when waiting straw dogs fire. It changes the "close refuses / move up / root closes only when all landed" shape from a purely local close-time check into a cross-record link-following contract.
REASONING: The draft commits to moving the guard for homeless decisions from the ticket to `close` of the question. A line without `→` blocks closing, it moves to the nearest open ancestor that holds everyone who needs it, and the root closes only when everything has landed. The ancestor choice, the root case and the ticket's first criterion are consistent with the already-accepted decision 3 and with the ticket text, so those pass. The tree shows `due` in `questions.py` judging "landed" only by whether the answer's cited files differ from the owner's file. A pointer to an open ancestor's own question file would read as due at once, so the draft's special case ("such an answer is not yet arrived") is a new `due` contract. The draft does not examine the pointer's lifecycle: the line can move up again when the ancestor closes, can follow a merge or a prune of the ancestor, and finally lands in a doc, while the child's answer stays frozen. Other mechanisms depend on this: straw dogs bound to the child, the mover, and the wake report. Their firing time and the validity of the link are not local detail. The refusal text, the root rule and the removal of "ticket does not close" are local and pass the counter-test.
