PART: The draft makes "the answer" the subject of the link and recommends fingerprinting its text, but in this store the answer is not a content-bearing, addressable thing: a closed entry's answer field is a pointer line to a decision record ("the answer is never the deliberation itself"), and a lean is a one-line revisable string; what counts as "B's answer changed" is not examined.
CONTRACT CHANGE: What the relation promises and what it watches. A fingerprint of the pointer or lean line fires on rewording of the pointer or a lean tweak (against the store's principle "the same question means the same answers, never the same wording", and against the earlier draft's "a reword fires nothing"). It stays silent when the pointed ADR or doc is edited under the same pointer, which is where the real change happens. So either the link must target the pointed record or a declared answer revision, or the fingerprint recommendation fails. Also the earlier event trigger (B closes, closes again, is marked suspect) already found changes; what the fingerprint adds over it is not shown.
REASONING: The draft commits to a relation `assumes` whose ends are answers (or leans), distinct from depends on (ordering, blocking), with a stored fingerprint so the script detects staleness. I checked the questions mechanism and skill: the unit is the open question, one entry per question, and the answer is a pointer plus attribution kept on the entry, replaced by a re-close; there is no answer record with an id or content of its own. The ends decision (answers rather than questions) and the depends-on overlap are surfaced to the user, so they pass. The date-or-fingerprint choice is also surfaced, but it is framed as a small encoding choice when it decides what "changed answer" means for pointer-style answers and for leans. That meaning crosses the store/docs boundary and reverses the reword rule the earlier draft relied on, so it can't be settled later as local detail. Clearing a stale link when an answer is rewritten is implementation detail and passes.
