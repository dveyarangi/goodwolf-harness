Here is the concept in depth, as the eighteen families give it.

**What a question is.** Its set of possible answers plus a presupposition (Belnap). The same words under a different contrast class are a different question (van Fraassen). A question *arises* only once its presupposition is settled, and the roots of the chain are not questions but presuppositions nobody asks (Collingwood).

**"Serve" is four relations, not one.** *Helps answer* (PHI's word, and the one I borrowed); *answering it partially answers the parent* (Roberts); *all of these are needed* or *one of these suffices* (KAOS's AND/OR); *cannot be asked until* (presupposition).
