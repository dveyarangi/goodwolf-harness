## Правило 1 — у принципа есть имя

> Every principle carries a name at its authored home — in the entry file's general section the bullet opens with it in bold, in a mechanism's doc and the glossary the term is the name — and is cited by that name and nothing else.

Для сегодняшних буллетов `AGENTS.md` имена вышли бы такими: **one shape is not a class**, **a document informs**, **recency for evidence**, **Occam**, **a capable reader**; из docs механизмов — **one home per fact**, **every output has a reader**; из сегодняшнего — **source of truth**, **authored once, installed at the thing**, **silence is not evidence**.

## Правило 2 — абзац несёт своё основание

> Under `debug=on`, open each paragraph that claims or recommends with what it stands on: `⚓ *<name>* — ` for a principle, by its name; `⚓ *<record>* — ` for a decision or evidence, by its record; `⚓ — ` when it stands on the agent's judgement alone. A paragraph that links, narrates or restates carries none. Tag what the paragraph stands on, never everything that bears on it.
