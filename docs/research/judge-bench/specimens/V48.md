## The missing parent, in practice

We agreed the agent opens a missing parent by itself. I had assumed that was a single call. It isn't:

- The store's "insert between" call takes one child only. Gathering four children under a new parent means one opening and several moves.
- Each move renames the moved question and everything under it: files, links, session rows, and every provisional text tied to those questions.

For the lifecycle example, that would rename around twenty questions on the strength of one message about deleted symlinks.

**How I planned it:** the agent opens the new question under the far one that contains it and stands on it, moving nothing. The reply names the children it would gather and proposes the move. The move runs when you say so.

This keeps your decision intact: the question is opened without waiting for you. Only the re-homing of existing questions waits.

- **Open and propose the move**: the new question exists immediately and nothing is renamed on a guess. Until you answer, the new parent sits beside the children it should hold.
- **Open and move at once**: the tree is right immediately. But a wrong reading rewrites ids across the store, and undoing it means renaming everything back.

It also makes the rule shorter. Opening a missing parent becomes the ordinary "open it under the lowest question that contains it", so the entry file needs no extra sentence; the detail lives in the skill.
